# Liquidity and Market Microstructure of Tokenized Carbon Assets Trading in Blockchain-Based Voluntary Carbon Markets: A Mean-Centered MMRM with HAC Corrections

## Citation

Sukmawati Sukamulya and Veronica Tri Kusuma. "Liquidity and Market Microstructure of Tokenized Carbon Assets Trading in Blockchain-Based Voluntary Carbon Markets: A Mean-Centered MMRM with HAC Corrections". Journal of Risk and Financial Management, 3 May 2026. https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm19050331.

## One-line summary

On-chain evidence from $KLIMA, BCT and MCO2 shows that liquidity in tokenised carbon markets tracks trading behaviour and token design more than underlying carbon fundamentals.

## Key claims

- The study analyses daily on-chain data for $KLIMA, BCT and MCO2 using a mean-centered MMRM with HAC corrections.
- Transaction value is positively associated with market depth.
- Fragmented trading intensity is associated with wider bid-ask spreads.
- Market expansion is linked to higher price volatility, particularly in structurally thin markets.
- A regulation index acts mainly as a homologizer moderator, with stronger effects in thinner markets such as MCO2.
- The paper describes the Toucan Protocol as issuing the Base Carbon Tonne (BCT) token on Polygon through a bridging mechanism.
- The authors report modest explanatory power, so the relationships are partial and context-specific.

- It gives empirical market-microstructure evidence on the bridged-credit tokens that Verra's 2022 crypto-instruments stance targeted.
- It supports the policy view that fragmentation and thin markets are the main liquidity limits for tokenised carbon.
