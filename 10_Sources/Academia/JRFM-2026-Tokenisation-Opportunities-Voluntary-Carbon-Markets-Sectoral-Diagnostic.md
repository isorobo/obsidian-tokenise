# Tokenisation Opportunities in Voluntary Carbon Markets: A Sectoral Diagnostic

## Citation

Author names not captured from the fetched text (single-author affiliation: ISM University of Management and Economics, Vilnius). "Tokenisation Opportunities in Voluntary Carbon Markets: A Sectoral Diagnostic". Journal of Risk and Financial Management, 1 January 2026. https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm19010028.

## One-line summary

The paper builds a sector tokenisation opportunity matrix from registry project data to show where tokenisation can best absorb frictions in voluntary carbon markets.

## Key claims

- Voluntary carbon markets are growing rapidly but stay structurally fragmented because of verification delays, lifecycle opacity, inconsistent metadata and capital mobilisation bottlenecks.
- The paper treats tokenisation as sector-aware financial infrastructure covering the full credit lifecycle, not as a digital overlay.
- It uses 1495 registry-compliant projects from the Berkeley Voluntary Offsets Database (BVOD v2025-06).
- It develops the sector tokenisation opportunity matrix (STOM) on three indicators: total credits issued, percentage retired, and average credits per project.
- The paper names Verra as one of the third-party registries that issue the underlying credits.
- The author flags a limit: registry-reported data may underrepresent informal or unlisted project flow.
