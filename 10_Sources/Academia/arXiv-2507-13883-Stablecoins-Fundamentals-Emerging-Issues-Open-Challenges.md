# Stablecoins: Fundamentals, Emerging Issues, and Open Challenges

## Citation

Ahmed Mahrous, Maurantonio Caprolu and Roberto Di Pietro. "Stablecoins: Fundamentals, Emerging Issues, and Open Challenges". arXiv (econ.GN), 18 July 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13883.

## One-line summary

A structured literature survey of stablecoin research covering stability, novel designs and regulation, with a list of open research gaps.

## Key claims

- Stablecoin capitalisation exceeded USD200 billion as of January 2025.
- Annual transaction volumes exceeded 10 trillion dollars in 2023 and nearly doubled in 2024.
- Academic research on stablecoins is described as fragmented, incomplete and sometimes contradictory.
- The survey frames research questions on stability, novel designs and implementations, and regulatory challenges.
- Identified gaps include security and privacy, underexplored stablecoins, unexamined failure cases, governance mechanisms and accounting treatment.
- The arXiv page notes acceptance in the International Review of Economics & Finance (Volume 111, 2026).
