# SoK: Stablecoins for Digital Transformation -- Design, Metrics, and Application with Real World Asset Tokenization as a Case Study

## Citation

Luyao Zhang. "SoK: Stablecoins for Digital Transformation -- Design, Metrics, and Application with Real World Asset Tokenization as a Case Study". arXiv (econ.GN), 4 August 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02403.

## One-line summary

A systematisation of knowledge that unifies fragmented stablecoin research in a taxonomy and benchmarking framework, with RWA tokenisation as the applied case.

## Key claims

- The paper addresses fragmentation in stablecoin research across disciplines.
- It offers a taxonomy of stablecoin systems by custodial structure, stabilisation mechanism and governance.
- It proposes a performance evaluation framework for different stakeholder needs.
- An open-source benchmarking pipeline supports transparency and reproducibility.
- A Real World Asset tokenisation case study shows the framework in application.
