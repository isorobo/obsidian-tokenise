# xRWA: A Cross-Chain Framework for Interoperability of Real-World Assets

## Citation

Yihao Guo, Haoming Zhu, Minghui Xu, Xiuzhen Cheng and Bin Xiao. "xRWA: A Cross-Chain Framework for Interoperability of Real-World Assets". arXiv, 16 September 2025 (v3 revised 8 December 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12957.

## One-line summary

xRWA combines decentralised identifiers, verifiable credentials and a settlement channel to cut repeated authentication and multi-step settlement for real-world assets moving across chains.

## Key claims

- RWAs such as bonds, commodities and real estate represented on blockchains face repeated authentication and slow multi-step settlement when they operate across chains.
- The framework centres on identity management, authentication and interaction.
- It uses Decentralized Identifiers and Verifiable Credentials with custom attributes.
- An authentication protocol based on Simplified Payment Verification avoids redundant checks across chains.
- A cross-chain channel settles RWAs without closing the channel.
- Simulations suggest the framework is feasible and more efficient for cross-chain RWAs.

## Significance

- Gives a technical view of cross-chain settlement and identity for tokenised assets, a layer the vault's legal and regulatory sources mostly assume.
- Complements the SoK and taxonomy papers that note hybrid designs with off-chain legal guarantees.
- Evidence is from simulation only, so treat efficiency claims as indicative.
