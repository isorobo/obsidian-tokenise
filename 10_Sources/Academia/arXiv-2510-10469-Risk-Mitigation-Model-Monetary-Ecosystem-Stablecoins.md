# A Risk Mitigation Model of Monetary Ecosystem with Stablecoins

## Citation

Hongzhe Wen and R.S.M. Lau. "A Risk Mitigation Model of Monetary Ecosystem with Stablecoins". arXiv (q-fin.RM), 12 October 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10469.

## One-line summary

A hybrid monetary design combining full reserve backing, interoperable redemption and central bank liquidity support is shown, on the 2023 USDC depeg, to damp stablecoin run dynamics.

## Key claims

- Stablecoin market capitalisation passing USD250 billion in 2025 makes their systemic risks material.
- The proposed architecture has three mechanisms: complete reserve backing, interoperable redemption and central bank liquidity support for par convertibility.
- The 2023 SVB-related USDC depeg is the test case.
- The model reduces peak peg deviations, shortens stress persistence and stabilises redemption queues.
- Removing maturity transformation and adding a liquidity backstop addresses bank-run risk before it arises.
- The framework supports integrating stablecoins into the regulated financial system while keeping monetary stability.

- It models a policy design (reserves plus central bank backstop) that bears on stablecoin reserve requirements.
- The USDC depeg calibration gives a concrete stress case.
- It extends the hybrid monetary ecosystem line of work already in the vault.
