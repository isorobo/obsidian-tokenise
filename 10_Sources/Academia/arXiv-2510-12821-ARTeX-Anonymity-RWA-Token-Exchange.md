# ARTeX: Anonymity Real-world-assets Token eXchange

## Citation

Jaeseong Lee and Junghee Lee. "ARTeX: Anonymity Real-world-assets Token eXchange". arXiv, 10 October 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12821.

## One-line summary

ARTeX proposes a trading platform for RWA tokens that protects trader anonymity while keeping safeguards against illegal activity.

## Key claims

- Blockchain transparency means the anonymity of traders cannot be guaranteed.
- Prior mixers for fungible tokens and privacy schemes for NFTs do not fit RWA tokens well.
- ARTeX is a token trading platform designed to protect trader anonymity for RWA tokens.
- The design adds safeguards against illegal activity.
- The authors concede it is difficult to propose a protocol that completely disconnects seller and buyer information.

## Excerpts

> "This paper proposes a new token trading platform, the ARTeX, designed to resolve these issues."

## Significance

- Raises the tension between on-chain privacy and compliance obligations for RWA token trading venues.
- Limited legal analysis; useful mainly as a technical reference on privacy for tokenised-asset markets.
