# SoK: Blockchain Agent-to-Agent Payments

## Citation

Yuanzhe Zhang, Yuexin Xiang, Yuchen Lei, Qin Wang, Tian Qiu, Yujing Sun, Spiridon Zarkov, Tsz Hon Yuen, Andreas Deppeler, Jiangshan Yu and Kwok-Yan Lam. "SoK: Blockchain Agent-to-Agent Payments". arXiv (q-fin.GN), 4 April 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03733.

## One-line summary

A systematisation of blockchain-based payments between autonomous AI agents, organised in a four-stage lifecycle with the main trust and security gaps identified.

## Key claims

- AI agents are expected to handle complex workflows autonomously, pointing to Agent-to-Agent (A2A) ecosystems.
- Trust and security challenges remain, particularly for financial transactions between unknown and untrusted agents.
- Blockchain offers programmable settlement, transparent accounting and open interoperability as a foundation.
- The paper systematises blockchain A2A payments (for example X402) in four stages: discovery, authorisation, execution and accounting.
- Key challenges are weak intent binding, misuse under valid authorisation, payment-service decoupling and limited accountability.
- Future directions are cross-stage consistency, behaviour-aware control and compositional payment workflows.
