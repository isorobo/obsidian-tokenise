# The Hidden Plumbing of Stablecoins: Financial and Technological Risks in the GENIUS Act Era

## Citation

Aronoff, Daniel; Calabia, F. Christopher; Brownworth, Anders; Samuel, Ashwanth; Narula, Neha. "The Hidden Plumbing of Stablecoins: Financial and Technological Risks in the GENIUS Act Era". arXiv (econ.GN), 18 April 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17167.

## One-line summary

The paper argues that par redemption of US dollar stablecoins under the GENIUS Act depends on Treasury and repo market functioning, broker-dealer capacity and blockchain rail reliability, not only on backing-asset quality.

## Key claims

- US dollar stablecoins are increasingly used as payment and settlement instruments beyond crypto markets.
- Par-value redemption depends on more than the quality of backing assets.
- Even conservatively backed stablecoins can face stress from redemption surges and market-intermediation bottlenecks.
- Treasury and repo market functioning and broker-dealer balance-sheet capacity condition the ability to meet redemptions.
- Reliability of the blockchain transaction rails is a further source of risk.
- Durable stability will likely require coordinated oversight of financial-market infrastructure, prudential regulation and software governance.
- The authors suggest the principles may carry over to other jurisdictions (67 pages, per the arXiv listing).
