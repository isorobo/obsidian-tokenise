# Tokenized but Illiquid? Evidence from Real-World Asset Markets

## Citation

Mafrur, Rischan. "Tokenized but Illiquid? Evidence from Real-World Asset Markets". arXiv:2606.01131 (cs.CE; q-fin.CP), 31 May 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01131.

## One-line summary

Using an Ethereum-based monthly panel of nine non-stablecoin RWA tokens across Treasury, gold, and private credit categories, the paper finds that on-chain representation does not produce liquid secondary markets, and that holder breadth predicts liquidity better than asset value.

## Key claims

- On-chain representation and secondary-market liquidity are distinct outcomes; tokenisation changes ownership form but does not automatically create active trading.
- Gold-backed tokens (PAXG, XAUT) exhibit broader holder bases and more persistent on-chain activity than Treasury-backed or private-credit tokens.
- Outstanding asset value does not reliably predict observed liquidity once participation breadth is controlled.
- Liquidity is measured using turnover, active addresses, and an active-month indicator; descriptive statistics and panel regressions reveal substantial heterogeneity across token categories.
- Liquid secondary markets in tokenised assets require market design, investor participation, and additional conditions beyond on-chain issuance.

## Significance

- Provides the first panel-level empirical test of whether RWA tokenisation actually produces liquidity, directly challenging vendor and industry claims about liquidity transformation.
- Distinguishes asset categories (Treasury, gold, private credit) by empirically observed secondary-market activity, giving the wiki concrete evidence to calibrate claims about tokenised instruments.
- Reveals that holder breadth -- not asset value -- drives observed liquidity, which has implications for how token distribution mechanics should be evaluated in the wiki's market-infrastructure analysis.
- Complements the risk-scoring framework in arXiv:2605.29689 by establishing the liquidity baseline that the risk scores measure.
