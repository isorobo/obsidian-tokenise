# How Agentic Is Agentic Commerce? A Population-Scale Measurement of x402 Adoption and Authenticity

## Citation

Ling, Shengchen, Yajin Zhou, Lei Wu, and Cong Wang. "How Agentic Is Agentic Commerce? A Population-Scale Measurement of x402 Adoption and Authenticity". arXiv:2607.12575 (cs.CR), 14 July 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12575.

## One-line summary

The paper measures every x402 stablecoin settlement on Base over 280 days and finds most of the headline adoption figure is manufactured, not genuine machine-to-machine commerce.

## Key claims

- x402 settles a stablecoin payment on-chain for each AI-agent purchase and is the most widely deployed protocol for autonomous agent-to-agent commerce.
- Settlement counts cannot serve as an adoption metric because a facilitator sponsors gas fees and no on-chain marker identifies who controls a payment.
- Over 280 days, Base carried 136,708,672 x402 settlements worth $44,121,383.81, with payer, recipient, and value concentration all above a Gini coefficient of 0.98.
- Of those settlements, 21.20% are fictitious and 63.78% are internal transfers within a single linked cluster.
- Genuinely independent value is bounded between $187,861.35 that demonstrably reaches a nameable service and $20,258,746.09 (45.92% of value) that is not provably manufactured.
- The manufacturable component forms a coherent, operator-driven economy that is star-shaped, machine-timed, and gas-subsidised.
- Settlement count measures manufacturability rather than genuine agentic economic adoption.

## Significance

- Supplies the first rigorous, population-scale audit of a live stablecoin-settled agentic-commerce protocol, directly testing claims made by IMF and AWS materials already in the corpus about agentic-AI payment adoption.
- Shows that headline metrics for agent-to-agent stablecoin volume can overstate genuine economic activity by an order of magnitude, a caution for any regulator or bank citing x402 settlement counts as evidence of market scale.
- Ties stablecoin rails (USDC on Base) directly to the agentic-AI trading and payments pillar of the wiki-tokenise corpus, with concrete on-chain forensic methodology.
- Identifies gas-subsidised, operator-driven settlement clusters as a structural feature of current agentic-commerce infrastructure, relevant to AML/CFT and market-integrity analysis of stablecoin rails.
