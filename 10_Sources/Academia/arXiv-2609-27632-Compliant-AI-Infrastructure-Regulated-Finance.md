# Compliant AI Infrastructure for Regulated Finance: A tiered multi-agent framework with DLT audit trails for financial operations in DACH

## Citation

Walter Kurz and Reinhard Magg. "Compliant AI Infrastructure for Regulated Finance: A tiered multi-agent framework with DLT audit trails for financial operations in DACH". arXiv, 23 September 2026 (v2 30 September 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27632.

## One-line summary

A compliance-first multi-agent architecture compiles regulatory intent into prohibitions, obligations and runtime budgets and records decisions in a permissioned DAG for replay and audit.

## Key claims

- The paper treats regulation as an orientation layer rather than a fixed rule set.
- A classification matrix feeds a policy compiler that derives prohibitions, obligations and runtime budgets.
- Evidence and decisions are recorded in a permissioned DAG with deterministic timestamps, supporting replay and auditing.
- Clause-level legal indexing and capability-based agent routing aim at portability across DACH and the wider EU.
- Committee activation stays policy-driven and proportionate, keeping supervisory oversight.
- The analysis focuses on financial institutions in the DACH region.
