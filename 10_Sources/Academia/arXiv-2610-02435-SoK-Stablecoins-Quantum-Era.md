# SoK: Stablecoins in the Quantum Era

## Citation

Chatzigiannis, Panagiotis; Alamati, Navid; Chakraborty, Suvradip; Le, Duc V. "SoK: Stablecoins in the Quantum Era". arXiv (cs.CR), 1 October 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02435.

## One-line summary

A systematisation of post-quantum risks and migration options for stablecoins, centred on an "authority-liability gap" between who can migrate a component and who bears the loss.

## Key claims

- Stablecoins support payments, trading, collateral and cross-chain settlement, so quantum threats to their cryptography matter for monetary control.
- The paper presents a stablecoin-specific threat model mapping cryptographic dependencies to monetary control points.
- Migration choices are classified by who can authorise a change, where it must happen, and whether it is hybrid, post-quantum native or an encapsulation of a classical component.
- The party able to migrate a component is often not the party bearing the loss if migration fails.
- The paper links migration to redemption and regulatory requirements.
- Open problems include threshold authorisation, operation-specific security levels, dormant and wrapped supply, private and compliance-enabled transfers, and measuring quantum-vulnerable exposure.
