# Dexy: A Simple Stablecoin Design Based on an Algorithmic Central Bank

## Citation

Chepurnoy, Alexander; Saxena, Amitabh; D'Angelo, Luca Gaetano. "Dexy: A Simple Stablecoin Design Based on an Algorithmic Central Bank". arXiv (cs.CE), 2 October 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03841.

## One-line summary

The paper proposes Dexy, an algorithmic stablecoin kept near an oracle price by a central-bank component intervening in a liquidity pool.

## Key claims

- Dexy has three components: a central bank, a secondary market in the form of a customised constant-product AMM liquidity pool, and a trusted oracle.
- The pool price is stabilised relative to the oracle price through central-bank interventions.
- Users mint Dexy from the bank by paying with basecoins, the base cryptocurrency asset.
- The design is an algorithmic stablecoin, not fiat-reserve backed.
- The arXiv page notes publication in the Journal of Financial Technology, special issue on Stability.
