# Decentralised Multi-Manager Fund Framework

## Citation

Abgaryan, Arman, Utkarsh Sharma, and Joshua Tobkin. "Decentralised Multi-Manager Fund Framework". arXiv q-fin.GN, 2025-07-01. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00978.

## One-line summary

A four-layer on-chain framework enables permissionless multi-strategy capital allocation through tokenised vaults that issue V-Tokens as fractional ownership claims, accommodating both human and AI participants as capital allocators and strategy developers.

## Key claims

- The framework uses four interconnected layers: capitalisation (multi-asset deposit and tokenisation), strategy (validated marketplace), execution (on-chain deployment), and allocation (dynamic rebalancing).
- Each strategy operates as a manager within a smart contract vault issuing V-Tokens representing fractional ownership.
- The system accommodates AI participants as capital allocators and strategy developers alongside human participants.
- Permissionless access removes the need for a central manager, creating a self-regulating ecosystem.
- The framework spans traditional and digital finance domains, bridging tokenised and non-tokenised asset strategies.
- Dynamic rebalancing toward superior-performing strategies is automated through the validated allocation layer.
