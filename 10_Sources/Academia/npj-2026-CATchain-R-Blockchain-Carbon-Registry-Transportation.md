# A blockchain-based carbon registry platform for credible climate action in transportation

## Citation

Xinlai Liu, Tolkien Bagchi, Charlle Lee Sy and H. Oliver Gao. "A blockchain-based carbon registry platform for credible climate action in transportation". npj Climate Action, 13 February 2026. https://www.nature.com/articles/s44168-026-00342-w.

## One-line summary

The paper proposes CATchain-R, a blockchain carbon registry that uses smart contracts and registry-mediated auditor selection, and tests it by simulation on New York City transport data.

## Key claims

- Current carbon registries face inadequate verification, double-counting loopholes and limited transparency.
- CATchain-R records annual carbon goals, plans and actions on a blockchain registry for transportation.
- It decouples project developers from verification through registry-mediated auditor selection and automated smart contracts.
- A token-based carbon credibility index quantifies performance against stated goals.
- System Dynamics simulations on New York City data (2005 to 2050) show how credibility shapes commitment execution under different reduction scenarios.
- The Discussion lists Verra among existing registries that rely on third-party audits.

## Significance

- It shows an academic design for a ledger-based registry, the architecture that Verra's Meta Registry plans point towards.
- It frames registry design as an integrity tool, which links to Verra's immobilisation questions.
- It is a design proposal tested by simulation, not a deployed registry, so treat its claims as exploratory.
