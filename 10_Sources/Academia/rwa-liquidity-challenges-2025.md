# Tokenize Everything, But Can You Sell It? RWA Liquidity Challenges and the Road Ahead

## Citation

Mafrur, Rischan. "Tokenize Everything, But Can You Sell It? RWA Liquidity Challenges and the Road Ahead". arXiv q-fin.GN, 2025-08-03. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11651.

## One-line summary

Despite $25 billion in on-chain tokenised RWAs by 2025, most tokens trade at negligible volumes, and structural barriers including regulatory gating, custodial concentration, and valuation opacity prevent genuine secondary market liquidity.

## Key claims

- Over $25 billion in tokenised real-world assets existed on-chain by 2025, yet most show low trading volumes, long holding periods, and minimal investor participation.
- Regulatory whitelisting requirements restrict the eligible buyer base and suppress secondary market depth.
- Custodial concentration in a small number of qualified custodians creates single-point-of-failure risk and entry barriers.
- Valuation opacity in private credit and real estate tokens prevents price discovery and deters institutional secondary trading.
- Hybrid market structures combining on-chain settlement with off-chain order matching offer a near-term liquidity pathway.
- Collateral-based liquidity mechanisms and transparency enhancements are identified as complementary solutions.
