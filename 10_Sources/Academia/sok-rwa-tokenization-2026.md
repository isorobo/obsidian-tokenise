# SoK of RWA Tokenization: A Systematization of Concepts, Architectures, and Legal Interoperability

## Citation

Luo, Junliang, Xihan Xiong, Zonglun Li, Hong Kang, Xue Liu, William J Knottenbelt, and Katrin Tinn. "SoK of RWA Tokenization: A Systematization of Concepts, Architectures, and Legal Interoperability". arXiv q-fin.GN, 2026-04-08. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06608.

## One-line summary

A comprehensive systematisation of real-world asset tokenisation that maps the full lifecycle from legal custody to on-chain settlement and identifies the oracle problem and regulatory fragmentation as the field's core unresolved challenges.

## Key claims

- Real-world asset tokenisation converts passive ledger entries into programmable economic agents capable of autonomous settlement and algorithmic collateralisation.
- The architecture spans four interdependent layers: legal custody, technical standards, cryptoeconomic valuation, and regulatory compliance.
- The oracle problem -- bridging deterministic blockchain code and probabilistic real-world conditions -- remains unsolved and creates systemic vulnerability.
- Sovereign debt, private credit, and real estate protocols each exhibit distinct on-chain structures, confirmed through empirical analysis of U.S. Treasury instruments.
- Asset tokenisation may serve as a transitional mechanism rather than a permanent long-term shift, compared to fully unified programmable ledgers.
- Regulatory fragmentation across jurisdictions is identified as a first-order constraint on cross-border interoperability.
