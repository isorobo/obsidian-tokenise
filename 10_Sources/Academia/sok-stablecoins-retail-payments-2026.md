# SoK: Stablecoins in Retail Payments

## Citation

Li, Yuquan, Yuexin Xiang, Qin Wang, Tsz Hon Yuen, Andreas Deppeler, and Jiangshan Yu. "SoK: Stablecoins in Retail Payments". arXiv q-fin.GN, 2026-01-01. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00196.

## One-line summary

A systematic comparison of stablecoin payment systems against card networks using the CLEAR framework finds stablecoins hold a conditional advantage in closed-loop and cross-border contexts but remain structurally weaker as open-loop retail instruments due to externalised consumer protection.

## Key claims

- The CLEAR framework evaluates payment systems across five dimensions: cost, legality, experience, architecture, and reach.
- Stablecoins deliver lower merchant fees and efficient settlement but shift consumer protection responsibility to users and intermediaries.
- Stablecoins hold a conditional comparative advantage in closed-loop environments, cross-border corridors, and high-friction payment contexts.
- As open-loop retail instruments, stablecoins remain structurally disadvantaged against card networks that internalise consumer protections through liability rules.
- Card networks subsidise consumer protections through interchange fees; stablecoins externalise dispute resolution, producing weaker safeguards and higher cognitive demand.
- The findings imply stablecoin regulation must address the consumer-protection gap before open-loop retail adoption can scale.
