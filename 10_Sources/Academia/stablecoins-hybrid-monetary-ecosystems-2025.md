# Stablecoins and the Emerging Hybrid Monetary Ecosystems

## Citation

Wen, Hongzhe, Songbai Li, R. S. M. Lau, and Jamie Zhang. "Stablecoins and the Emerging Hybrid Monetary Ecosystems". arXiv q-fin.GN, 2025-05-16. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10997.

## One-line summary

Econometric modelling of the $250 billion stablecoin market demonstrates strong peg stability and supports a two-layer hybrid monetary architecture where private stablecoin issuers hold central bank reserves, with the 2023 SVB-USDC de-peg as a cautionary case study.

## Key claims

- Stablecoin market capitalisation exceeded $250 billion by mid-2025, establishing stablecoins as a material component of global monetary systems.
- Econometric modelling confirms strong peg stability across fiat-backed, crypto-collateralised, and algorithmic stablecoin types.
- A two-layer hybrid architecture -- private stablecoin issuers backed by central bank reserves -- is proposed to ensure uniformity, security, and programmability.
- The 2023 SVB-USDC de-peg event demonstrates the systemic risk that arises when private stablecoins depend on uninsured commercial bank deposits rather than central bank reserves.
- Hybrid monetary systems enhance financial inclusivity, scalability, and dollar utility while strengthening systemic resilience under the Dybvig model.
- Public and private monies (fiat, CBDC, USDC, USDT, DAI) coexist in the proposed framework with defined roles and reserve requirements.
