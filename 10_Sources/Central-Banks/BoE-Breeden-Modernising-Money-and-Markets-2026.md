# Modernising money and markets

## Citation

Breeden, Sarah. "Modernising money and markets". City Week 2026, London, 19 May 2026. https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/speech/2026/may/sarah-breeden-speech-at-city-week-the-future-of-money-in-a-digital-world.

## One-line summary

The Bank of England's Deputy Governor for Financial Stability sets out a multi-money retail vision and a wholesale tokenisation plan anchored in central bank money.

## Key claims

- Tokenisation can strengthen financial stability and support growth in retail payments and wholesale markets.
- The UK aims for a multi-money retail system where tokenised deposits, regulated stablecoins and possibly a retail CBDC coexist.
- The Bank would publish draft systemic stablecoin rules the following month and finalise them by year-end, weighing issuance guardrails instead of holding limits.
- The Digital Securities Sandbox, run with the FCA until January 2029, allows live trading and settlement of tokenised securities, with 16 firms preparing to launch.
- The Bank has a low risk appetite for a significant shift away from settlement in central bank money, and targets a synchronisation service on RT2 by 2028.
- The PRA clarified that bank exposures to tokenised assets receive the same prudential treatment as non-tokenised equivalents where legal rights and risks are comparable.
