# A model of monetary singleness

## Citation

Hemingway, Benjamin. "A model of monetary singleness". Bank of England Staff Working Papers, No. 1,174, 27 February 2026. https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/working-paper/2026/a-model-of-monetary-singleness.

## One-line summary

A three-period banking model shows when privately issued digital money can keep par value, and why central bank reserves and cash are the stabilisers of singleness.

## Key claims

- The paper builds a three-period banking model in which banks choose the unit of account of their debt and whether it can serve as a medium of exchange.
- Small deviations from singleness can be consistent with the efficient allocation, as small deviations from par already occur today (for example ATM withdrawal fees).
- Inefficient equilibria become more likely when new digital money is issued by private entities with business models distinct from incumbent financial institutions.
- Central bank reserves stabilise singleness because they ensure issuers share a consistent asset base.
- Cash is a backstop that enables interoperability through central bank money.
