# A digital euro for the digital age

## Citation

Piero Cipollone and Valdis Dombrovskis. "A digital euro for the digital age". ECB Blog, 9 December 2025. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2025/html/ecb.blog20251209~9ba130ff20.en.html.

## One-line summary

An ECB Executive Board member and the European Commission Executive Vice-President jointly urge EU legislators to adopt the digital euro regulation in 2026 so the ECB can be ready by 2029.

## Key claims

- The ECB aims to be ready for a possible digital euro by 2029, if EU co-legislators adopt the regulation in 2026.
- Preparatory work, including pilot exercises and initial transactions, could start as early as mid-2027.
- Euro cash currently exists only in physical form, as banknotes and coins.
- The Commission presented the Single Currency Package in 2023, with two proposals now before EU legislators.
- The proposed framework would be free, simple and inclusive, accepted across the euro area, and usable online and offline.
- Bulgaria will join the euro area in 2026 as its 21st member.
