# From hype to hazard: what stablecoins mean for Europe

## Citation

Jürgen Schaaf. "From hype to hazard: what stablecoins mean for Europe". ECB Blog, 28 July 2025. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2025/html/ecb.blog20250728~e6cb3cf8b5.en.html.

## One-line summary

An ECB adviser argues that US dollar stablecoins dominate the market and that Europe needs a common regulatory approach to avoid instability, regulatory arbitrage and dollar dominance.

## Key claims

- US dollar-based stablecoins account for roughly 99% of total stablecoin market value.
- Euro-denominated stablecoins were valued at less than EUR 350 million.
- Tether (USDT) and Circle (USDC) are by far the largest issuers.
- The US GENIUS Act was signed into law on 18 July 2025, establishing a federal stablecoin framework.
- Market analysts project supply could rise from USD 230 billion in 2025 to USD 2 trillion by the end of 2028.
- The BIS Annual Economic Report 2025 warned that many stablecoins have deviated substantially from their peg.

- Gives an ECB staff view of the stablecoin market, framed around euro-area exposure to dollar tokens.
- Dates the euro-stablecoin gap at mid-2025, a useful baseline for later ECB statements.
- Complements BIS work on stablecoin rails and the vault's coverage of the US GENIUS Act.
