# Digital euro: an opportunity for banks

## Citation

Piero Cipollone and Frank Elderson. "Digital euro: an opportunity for banks". ECB Blog, 27 March 2026. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2026/html/ecb.blog20260327~51b0640c39.en.html.

## One-line summary

Two ECB Executive Board members argue that the digital euro is manageable for bank liquidity and costs, and strategically valuable for payments autonomy.

## Key claims

- The Eurosystem will not charge scheme and processing fees for the digital euro, which lowers costs for banks.
- Total bank investment costs could be EUR 4 billion to EUR 5.8 billion, or EUR 1 billion to EUR 1.44 billion a year over four years.
- Like cash, the digital euro will not be remunerated, and a reverse waterfall feature reduces the need for high holdings.
- Individuals will face holding limits, and corporates will not be allowed to hold digital euro.
- In 13 of 21 euro area countries, in-store payments depend entirely on international card schemes or mobile solutions.
- The Eurosystem expects to announce European digital euro standards by summer 2026, with a pilot exercise planned for 2027.
