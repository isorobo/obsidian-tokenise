# A highway for the future of Europe's digital finance

## Citation

Piero Cipollone. "A highway for the future of Europe's digital finance". ECB Blog, 12 March 2026. https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/blog/date/2026/html/ecb.blog20260312~5dfa697fdd.en.html.

## One-line summary

An ECB Executive Board member sets out why Europe needs its own settlement infrastructure for tokenised assets, anchored in central bank money through Pontes and the Appia roadmap.

## Key claims

- The Eurosystem expects to offer settlement of DLT-based wholesale transactions in central bank money in the third quarter of 2026, initially by linking TARGET Services to DLT platforms.
- The ECB published the roadmap for its Appia initiative in the same week as the article.
- Wholesale central bank money consists of the deposits commercial banks hold at their central bank.
- The digital euro is being developed as a digital counterpart to banknotes and coins for everyday payments.
- Most day-to-day money is created by private institutions, and people accept it because it converts one-to-one into central bank money.
- Tokenised assets and DLTs could bring faster settlement, lower processing costs and risks, and round-the-clock availability.
