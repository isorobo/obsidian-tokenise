# ESMA calls for changes to make MiCA clearer, safer and ready for emerging services

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "ESMA calls for changes to make MiCA clearer, safer and ready for emerging services". ESMA News, 30 September 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-calls-changes-make-mica-clearer-safer-and-ready-emerging-services.

## One-line summary

ESMA's response to the European Commission's MiCA review consultation asks for tighter marketing rules, stronger supervisory powers, a DeFi and hybrid-token classification framework and a framework for tokenised securities with on-chain settlement.

## Key claims

- ESMA proposes stricter marketing rules for crypto-assets, especially offerings promoted by influencers.
- ESMA asks for enhanced supervisory powers to block fraudulent websites and freeze suspicious assets.
- ESMA wants a new classification framework for decentralised finance activities and hybrid tokens.
- ESMA recommends simpler white-paper procedures and fewer duplicative authorisation requirements.
- ESMA calls for a framework for tokenised securities with on-chain settlement capabilities.
- ESMA recommends proportionate disclosure obligations for staking, lending and borrowing services.
- ESMA asks that it receive binding token classification opinions to achieve EU-wide consistency.

## Significance

- This is ESMA's primary-source input to the Commission's MiCA 2.0 review, the lead ESMA item flagged as pending in earlier runs.
- The stablecoin proposal targets regulated firms offering services linked to non-compliant stablecoins, extending ESMA's earlier non-compliant ART and EMT stance.
- The call for a tokenised securities framework with on-chain settlement bears directly on the DLT Pilot Regime review.
- The full response is ESMA75-113276571-1721 (PDF on the ESMA site); this note rests on the press release text only.
