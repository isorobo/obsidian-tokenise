# ESMA sets new supervisory priority on digital innovation from 2027

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "ESMA sets new supervisory priority on digital innovation from 2027". ESMA News, 23 September 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-new-supervisory-priority-digital-innovation-2027.

## One-line summary

ESMA launches a Union Strategic Supervisory Priority on digital innovation from 2027, with an initial supervisory focus on how firms use artificial intelligence and tokenisation.

## Key claims

- The new digital innovation USSP takes effect from 2027 and covers supervised entities' use of emerging technologies while protecting investors.
- The initial focus, with national competent authorities, is on how supervised entities use artificial intelligence and tokenisation.
- It runs alongside the cyber and operational resilience USSP set in 2025.
- ESMA is concluding the ESG disclosures USSP that began in 2023.
- ESMA keeps flexibility to address future technology developments, including frontier AI models.

## Excerpts

> "The digital innovation USSP aims to ensure supervisors have the expertise and capacity to oversee the use of new technologies."
> ~ article body

> "In collaboration with National Competent Authorities, our initial focus will be on how supervised entities use artificial intelligence and tokenisation."
> ~ article body

> "USSPs are ESMA's convergence tools that address high-risk areas of strategic importance across the EU."
> ~ article body

## Significance

- This makes tokenisation an explicit EU supervisory convergence priority, not only a policy topic.
- It pairs tokenisation with AI supervision, relevant to the vault's agentic AI and AI governance coverage.
- It follows the 2026 work programme and the 2027-2029 programming document already indexed.
