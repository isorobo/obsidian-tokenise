# ESMA sets out supervisory expectations on services related to unauthorised stablecoins

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "ESMA sets out supervisory expectations on services related to unauthorised stablecoins". ESMA News, 8 October 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-sets-out-supervisory-expectations-services-related-unauthorised.

## One-line summary

ESMA's Opinion ESMA75-113276571-1742 expects MiCA-authorised CASPs to stop serving clients in connection with non-MiCA-compliant stablecoins, with remediation within three months.

## Key claims

- ESMA published an opinion on how it expects crypto-asset services involving non-compliant asset-referenced tokens (ARTs) and e-money tokens (EMTs) to be supervised under MiCA.
- MiCA-authorised CASPs should stop offering services tied to non-compliant stablecoins to EU clients.
- The expectation covers all MiCA crypto-asset services, alone or combined, including trading platforms, exchange, order execution, transfers, custody and portfolio management.
- National competent authorities should ensure CASPs have technical, contractual and organisational controls to block clients from acquiring or increasing exposure.
- Where authorities find pre-existing exposures, they should require remediation within three months of publication.
- Any continued service must be limited to liquidation, conversion, withdrawal, transfer or safekeeping, and be time-limited, risk-based and closely supervised.

## Significance

- Turns the end of the MiCA transitional periods into a concrete supervisory expectation for non-compliant stablecoins.
- Extends the earlier January 2025 ESMA and Commission position on non-compliant ARTs and EMTs to the whole range of CASP services.
- The three-month remediation window gives a date to track for NCA enforcement.
