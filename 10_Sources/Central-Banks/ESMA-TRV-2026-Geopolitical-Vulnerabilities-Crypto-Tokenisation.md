# Ongoing geopolitical and economic vulnerabilities masked by strong investor optimism

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "Ongoing geopolitical and economic vulnerabilities masked by strong investor optimism". ESMA News, 10 September 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/ongoing-geopolitical-and-economic-vulnerabilities-masked-strong-investor.

## One-line summary

ESMA's risk monitoring release flags growing links between vulnerable crypto-asset markets and the wider financial system, renewed DeFi spillover concerns and early but accelerating tokenisation of equities.

## Key claims

- Markets remain resilient despite stretched technology valuations and heightened geopolitical tensions.
- Strong technology and AI sector performance sustains investor optimism while macro-financial conditions deteriorate.
- The gap between valuations and the weaker economic outlook raises the risk of a sudden correction.
- ESMA warns of a growing linkage between increasingly vulnerable crypto-asset markets and the broader financial system.
- Recent DeFi exploits have renewed concerns about interconnectedness and spillovers.
- Frontier AI threats to market infrastructures warrant close monitoring.

## Significance

- This is the second 2026 ESMA risk monitoring output, long flagged as overdue in earlier runs.
- It gives an EU supervisor's financial-stability framing of crypto-asset and DeFi risk.
- It records ESMA's view that tokenised equities are early stage but gaining momentum.
- Crypto and tokenisation are one part of a broader market risk report, so treat it as context, not core policy.
