# HKMA announces "ART" Framework to promote responsible A.I. adoption

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA announces "ART" Framework to promote responsible A.I. adoption". HKMA Press Releases, 15 October 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/10/20261015-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA set out an "ART" (Accountability, Resilience, Trust) framework for responsible AI in banking and signalled a forthcoming discussion paper on supervisory governance of agentic AI.

## Key claims

- The HKMA introduced the "ART" framework, expanded in the release body as Accountability, Resilience and Trust, to support responsible AI use in banking.
- It issued updated supervisory guidance on responsible AI adoption, drawing on the second cohort of its Generative A.I. Sandbox.
- The second sandbox cohort completed its technical trials, and over 60% of its use cases explored agentic AI.
- The HKMA is testing agentic AI applications through the GenA.I. Sandbox++.
- A discussion paper on a supervisory framework for agentic AI governance is planned, covering intent-anchored accountability, a registry of material AI agents and intervention mechanisms.
- The HKMA is setting up an AI training data sharing platform with Cyberport and the University of Hong Kong, with data quality assurance as a core design principle.
- The release was timed with the two-day "Finance + A.I. Forum" co-hosted with Cyberport on 15 and 16 October 2026.

## Excerpts

> "an upcoming discussion paper on a proposed supervisory framework for agentic A.I. governance."
> ~ press release body

> "The progression from generative to agentic A.I. represents an important new stage of innovation for the banking sector."
> ~ remarks of Arthur Yuen, Deputy Chief Executive, HKMA

> "which stands for Accountability, Resiliency and Trust."
> ~ remarks of Arthur Yuen (the release body spells the R as "Resilience")

## Significance

- It is a primary supervisory statement on agentic AI governance, which sits in the vault's agentic AI and AI-in-finance scope.
- The planned registry of material AI agents and intervention mechanisms previews how a major supervisor may treat autonomous agents in finance.
- The release does not mention tokenisation, DLT or stablecoins; its relevance is the AI governance angle only.
- It extends the HKMA Fintech 2030 agenda covered in earlier HKMA notes.
