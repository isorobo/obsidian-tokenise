# Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Hong Kong Monetary Authority and Bank Negara Malaysia Strengthen Bilateral Cooperation". HKMA Press Releases, 6 October 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/10/20261006-4/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA and Bank Negara Malaysia agreed on 2 October 2026 to open a joint policy workstream on cross-border stablecoin use cases and to collaborate on tokenisation and digital money, alongside RTGS and securities depository links.

## Key claims

- The HKMA (Chief Executive Eddie Yue) and Bank Negara Malaysia (Governor Datuk Abdul Rasheed Ghaffour) chaired a bilateral meeting in Hong Kong on 2 October 2026; the HKMA issued the release on 6 October 2026.
- Fintech and digital assets cooperation includes a joint policy workstream on compliant cross-border stablecoin use cases between Hong Kong and Malaysia.
- The two authorities agreed to enhance collaboration on tokenisation and digital money.
- They will share knowledge on data-driven trade finance for SMEs.
- Infrastructure cooperation covers connecting the two jurisdictions' RTGS systems and exploring a securities depository link between Hong Kong's CMU and Malaysia's RENTAS.
- Other agreed areas are cyber resilience, anti-scam and anti-fraud mechanisms, local currency use in bilateral trade and investment, and Islamic finance.
- The release does not describe a memorandum of understanding; it records areas of strengthened collaboration.

## Significance

- It is the first indexed HKMA item that pairs a bilateral central bank workstream with cross-border stablecoin use cases, extending the domestic licensing regime into international coordination.
- Tokenisation and digital money collaboration with a Southeast Asian central bank signals possible interoperability work beyond Project Ensemble.
- The RTGS connection and CMU-RENTAS depository link are conventional infrastructure steps that may later carry tokenised settlement; the release gives no timeline.
- The release is a primary HKMA statement of intent, not a binding instrument.
