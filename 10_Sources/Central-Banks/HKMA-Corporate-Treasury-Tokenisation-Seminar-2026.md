# HKMA demonstrates potential of tokenisation to corporate treasury community

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA demonstrates potential of tokenisation to corporate treasury community". HKMA Press Releases, 10 June 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260610-5/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA held its first seminar for corporate treasurers on tokenisation, attracting over 150 participants from 50 corporates and 30 financial institutions, and committed to ongoing engagement with interested corporate treasury centres.

## Key claims

- The seminar "Corporate Treasury x Tokenisation: The Hong Kong Advantage in Action" drew over 150 participants from approximately 50 corporates and 30 financial institutions.
- Panel discussions covered Project Ensemble applications for real-time treasury management and tokenisation in bond issuance and trade finance.
- The HKMA committed to follow-up engagement with corporate treasury centres expressing interest in tokenisation collaboration.
- The initiative aligns with the Government's Action Plan for Corporate Treasury Centers announced on 9 June 2026.
- Panellists cited substantial potential of tokenisation and expressed support for continued market adoption efforts.

## Significance

- Marks the HKMA's first direct outreach to corporate treasury functions, extending the tokenisation agenda beyond banks and financial institutions to real-economy actors.
- Signals that Project Ensemble's second phase targets corporate treasury as a primary demand driver for tokenised deposits and real-time settlement.
- Connects tokenisation policy to the Government's Corporate Treasury Centre Action Plan, broadening the policy base beyond pure financial regulation.
- Provides evidence of growing buy-side demand for tokenised bond and deposit instruments from non-bank corporates.
