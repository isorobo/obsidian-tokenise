# FSTB and HKMA to further unlock potential of distributed ledger technology in Hong Kong's fixed income market

## Citation

Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "FSTB and HKMA to further unlock potential of distributed ledger technology in Hong Kong's fixed income market". HKMA Press Releases, 29 June 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260629-4/.

## One-line summary

The FSTB and HKMA concluded the first phase of a DLT review, found Hong Kong's law already flexible enough for tokenised bonds, and opened a second phase on legislative reform.

## Key claims

- The first-phase review finds Hong Kong's existing legal and regulatory environment sufficiently flexible for tokenised bond issuances.
- The finding rests on the Government's three landmark tokenised bond issuances and a growing pool of corporate tokenised bond issuances.
- The Companies Registry issued FAQs confirming that a debenture holder register maintained using DLT satisfies the record-keeping requirements of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).
- The second phase will run a legislative review to broaden DLT use in the fixed income market and wider digital asset space.
- The second phase will examine electronic execution of tokenised bond issuance documents, electronic signatures for trust creation, and legal concepts of possession and transfer for tokenised instruments.
- The review builds directly on the Tokenised Bond Expert Group the HKMA convened in June 2026.

> ~ Eddie Yue, HKMA Chief Executive

## Significance

- Marks Hong Kong's shift from ad hoc digital bond pilots to a systematic legislative programme for tokenised fixed income instruments.
- The Companies Registry FAQ is a first direct regulatory confirmation that DLT-maintained registers satisfy Companies Ordinance requirements, a live question in most jurisdictions attempting tokenisation.
- Signals that the next legal frontier is possession and transfer doctrine for tokenised instruments, directly engaging property-category and transfer questions central to the wiki's doctrinal corpus.
- Converts the Tokenised Bond Expert Group's private-sector input into a concrete two-phase government work programme, closing the loop opened by that group's formation.
