# HKMA unveils Fintech Promotion Blueprint: From adoption to advancement

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA unveils Fintech Promotion Blueprint: From adoption to advancement". HKMA Press Releases, 3 February 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/02/20260203-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA released the Fintech Promotion Blueprint, a multi-pillar plan under the Fintech 2030 strategy targeting DLT and AI applications, quantum preparedness, and fintech cybersecurity standards for Hong Kong's banking sector.

## Key claims

- The Blueprint operates under the "Fintech 2030" strategic framework and emphasises sophisticated applications of AI and distributed ledger technologies.
- Four flagship initiatives cover quantum preparedness, risk data strategy for DLT applications, a fintech cybersecurity baseline, and competency development for human-machine interaction.
- The New Risk Data Strategy enhances bank data governance to support advanced analytics and DLT applications.
- The Fintech Cybersecurity Baseline establishes industry-led security standards for AI and DLT solution providers, streamlining due diligence.
- The Blueprint is supported by high-performance computing infrastructure and Data Excellence and Cyber Resilience as foundational pillars.

## Excerpts

> "The future of finance will be defined by those who can harness sophisticated technology...to reimagine what is possible."
> ~ Arthur Yuen, Deputy Chief Executive, HKMA

> "marks a significant milestone under our 'Fintech 2030' strategy"
> ~ Eddie Yue, Chief Executive, HKMA

## Significance

- Establishes DLT as a named technology pillar within the HKMA's multi-year strategic framework, giving tokenisation a formal policy foundation beyond individual pilots.
- The cybersecurity baseline for DLT providers addresses a gap in the supervisory perimeter that has limited institutional adoption of tokenisation infrastructure.
- The quantum preparedness initiative acknowledges that cryptographic security of distributed ledgers is a systemic risk requiring proactive planning.
- Positions the Blueprint as the strategic umbrella connecting Project Ensemble, EnsembleTX, and the Tokenised Bond Expert Group under a single Fintech 2030 narrative.
