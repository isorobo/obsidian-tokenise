# HKSAR Government's Fourth Digital Green Bonds Offering

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKSAR Government's Fourth Digital Green Bonds Offering". HKMA Press Releases, 29 September 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/09/20260929-6/.

## One-line summary

The HKSAR Government priced about HK$20 billion of digital green bonds in four currencies, a record for a digital bond offering, and for the first time settled the HKD tranche's primary issuance with tokenised deposits through EnsembleTX.

## Key claims

- The Government priced around HK$20 billion of digital green bonds on 28 September 2026 across four currencies, under its Government Sustainable Bond Programme.
- Tranches: HKD 5.5 billion 2-year at 3.80%, RMB 7.5 billion 5-year at 1.65%, USD 200 million 3-year at 5.023%, and EUR 450 million 4-year at 3.734%.
- The issuance is the largest digital bond offering in the world, with subscription ratios of 1.3 to 11.3 times across the tranches.
- Tokenised deposits, supported by EnsembleTX, were built into primary issuance settlement for the HKD tranche, described as the first digital bond to integrate tokenised deposits in HKD.
- The offering adopts the ICMA Bond Data Taxonomy version 2.0 to improve data consistency and end-to-end automation across the bond lifecycle.
- Secondary coverage (Ledger Insights) reports three parallel settlement options (traditional rails, tokenised central bank money and tokenised deposits) and issuance on the HSBC Orion platform connected to the CMU.
- The Financial Secretary, the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury and the HKMA Chief Executive each commented on the issuance.

## Significance

- This is the first indexed primary evidence of EnsembleTX tokenised deposits used in a live government bond settlement, moving the pilot from announcement to real-value use.
- It extends the digital green bond series already indexed for the third offering and shows scale growth with settlement-rail choice.
