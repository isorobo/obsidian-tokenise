# HKEX and HKMA Launch Pilot Project to Enable Digital Payment Solution for Derivatives After-Hours Trading

## Citation

Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited and Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKEX and HKMA Launch Pilot Project to Enable Digital Payment Solution for Derivatives After-Hours Trading". HKMA Press Releases, 18 June 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260618-8/.

## One-line summary

HKEX and the HKMA piloted wholesale e-HKD for round-the-clock advance margin payments in derivatives after-hours trading.

## Key claims

- HKEX and the HKMA launch a joint pilot exploring wholesale e-HKD for advance margin payments in the derivatives After-Hours Trading session.
- The pilot targets HKFE Clearing Corporation Limited clearing participants, who must currently submit margin deposit requests by 3pm for same-day After-Hours Trading settlement.
- Wholesale e-HKD operates on a continuous 24/7 basis, removing that fixed cut-off and enabling real-time margin payments outside normal banking hours.
- HKEX invites eligible clearing participants to join optional real-value trial transactions, subject to regulatory approval and market readiness.
- The pilot follows record derivatives volumes, with average daily contracts reaching 1.78 million in the first five months of 2026.
- Howard Lee frames the pilot as a wholesale application of central bank digital currency in a live market environment, extending the HKMA's wholesale pivot announced on completion of the e-HKD Pilot Programme.

## Excerpts

> "Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) are pleased to announce today (18 June) a joint pilot project to explore a new digital payment solution for the After-Hours Trading (AHT) session in the derivatives market."
> ~ press release body, opening paragraph

> "As Hong Kong's financial infrastructure evolves to meet the growing demands of the market, the HKMA is committed to advancing innovation that enhances efficiency and resilience."
> ~ Howard Lee, HKMA Deputy Chief Executive

> "We are delighted to collaborate with the HKMA on this latest initiative to advance market accessibility and strengthen Hong Kong's capital markets infrastructure."
> ~ Vanessa Lau, HKEX Chief Operating Officer

## Significance

- Extends wholesale e-HKD beyond the completed Pilot Programme into a live production use case for exchange-clearing margin, evidencing the HKMA's retail-to-wholesale pivot.
- Targets a concrete market-structure friction, the 3pm After-Hours Trading margin cut-off, showing tokenised central bank money solving an operational problem rather than a purely experimental one.
- Positions HKEX as the first Hong Kong exchange group to test CBDC-based margin settlement, a template other derivatives clearing houses may follow.
- Sits alongside Project Ensemble and the Tokenised Bond Expert Group as a third concurrent 2026 HKMA workstream applying tokenised money to live market infrastructure.
