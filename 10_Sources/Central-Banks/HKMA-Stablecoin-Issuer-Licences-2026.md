# Granting of stablecoin issuer licences

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Granting of stablecoin issuer licences". HKMA Press Releases, 10 April 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/04/20260410-4/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA granted Hong Kong's first stablecoin issuer licences under the Stablecoins Ordinance to Anchorpoint Financial Limited and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited.

## Key claims

- Both licences took effect on 10 April 2026 under the Stablecoins Ordinance regulatory regime.
- The two licensees are Anchorpoint Financial Limited and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited.
- Both licensees plan to complete preparatory work and launch HKD-referenced stablecoin operations within months of the licence grant.
- The HKMA maintains a public Register of Licensed Stablecoin Issuers on its website.
- Eddie Yue, Chief Executive of the HKMA, described the licence grants as "an important milestone for the development of digital assets in Hong Kong."

## Excerpts

> "The granting of stablecoin issuer licences is an important milestone for the development of digital assets in Hong Kong."
> ~ Eddie Yue, Chief Executive, HKMA

> "Citizens are advised to acquire stablecoins only through regulated channels and verify licensee information through the official register."
> ~ HKMA press release body

## Significance

- Marks the first live licensing grants under Hong Kong's Stablecoins Ordinance, translating legislation enacted in 2025 into operational regulatory supervision.
- Establishes HSBC as a licensed stablecoin issuer, the first global systemically important bank to receive such a licence in any jurisdiction.
- Anchors Hong Kong's position as a jurisdiction with a functioning stablecoin licensing regime alongside the EU's MiCA e-money token framework.
- Creates a reference point for the doctrine of licensing and reserve requirements applicable to fiat-referenced stablecoins under HK law.
