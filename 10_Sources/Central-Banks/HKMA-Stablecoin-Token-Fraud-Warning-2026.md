# Beware of tokens purported to be associated with licensed stablecoin issuers

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Beware of tokens purported to be associated with licensed stablecoin issuers". HKMA Press Releases, 28 April 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/04/20260428-5/.

## One-line summary

Within weeks of the first stablecoin licences taking effect, the HKMA warned the public about fraudulent tokens bearing the tickers "HKDAP" and "HSBC" that neither Anchorpoint nor HSBC had issued.

## Key claims

- Tokens bearing the tickers "HKDAP" and "HSBC" circulated in the market within weeks of the April 2026 licence grants but had no connection to the licensed issuers.
- Both Anchorpoint Financial Limited and The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited confirmed they had not yet released any regulated stablecoins.
- The HKMA urged the public to stay vigilant against fraudulent activities purported to be associated with licensed stablecoin issuers.
- The HKMA directed the public to verify stablecoin authenticity through official licensee announcements and to transact only through regulated channels.
- The warning demonstrates that the gap between licence grant and product launch creates market-manipulation risk.

## Excerpts

> "stay vigilant against fraudulent activities or scams that are purported to be associated with the licensees or their stablecoin issuance."
> ~ HKMA public warning

> "Neither originated from legitimate stablecoin licensees. Both licensed issuers have officially confirmed they have not released any regulated stablecoins."
> ~ press release body

## Significance

- Documents a systemic risk in the licensing-to-launch transition period: the presence of a public licence register creates a surface for impersonation fraud before licensees launch.
- Provides evidence that consumer protection doctrine must address the gap period between regulatory approval and product issuance.
- Highlights the practical function of the public Register of Licensed Stablecoin Issuers as a fraud-detection tool for retail investors.
- Informs AML/CFT policy design for stablecoin regimes: fraudulent tokens targeting licensed-issuer brands require pre-emptive disclosure and monitoring mechanisms.
