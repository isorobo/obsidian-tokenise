# HKMA establishes Tokenised Bond Expert Group

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA establishes Tokenised Bond Expert Group". HKMA Press Releases, 5 June 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260605-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA established a multi-stakeholder Tokenised Bond Expert Group to drive policy, legal, and market-practice reform to scale Hong Kong's tokenised bond market.

## Key claims

- The Expert Group first convened in May 2026 and comprises industry associations, financial institutions, legal firms, and technology providers.
- Its mandate is to explore policy measures, market practices, and innovations to broaden adoption and scalability of tokenised bonds.
- The HKMA is working with the Financial Services and Treasury Bureau to review and enhance Hong Kong's legal and regulatory framework for tokenised bonds.
- The group builds on a series of digital bond milestones: the first tokenised government green bond (2023), the first multi-currency digital bond (2024), and the largest digital bond issuance (2025).
- Regulatory improvements identified by the Expert Group will be announced separately.

## Excerpts

> "collectively explore policy measures, market practices, and innovations"
> ~ Expert Group mandate, press release body

> "exploring potential enhancements to Hong Kong's legal and regulatory regime together with the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau to facilitate the broader adoption of tokenisation technology in the fixed income market"
> ~ press release body

## Significance

- The Expert Group formalises private-public co-design of the tokenised bond legal framework, a model other jurisdictions may follow.
- Its work sits at the intersection of securities law and tokenisation doctrine, addressing questions of prospectus disclosure, transfer, and custody for digital securities.
- The group's outputs will directly shape the legal and regulatory regime applicable to security tokens in Hong Kong.
- Connects Project Ensemble's infrastructure work to the legislative layer, bridging market-infrastructure development and legal reform.
