# Treasury Markets Summit 2026

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Treasury Markets Summit 2026". HKMA Press Releases, 23 September 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/09/20260923-4/.

## One-line summary

At the Treasury Markets Summit on 23 September 2026, HKMA Chief Executive Eddie Yue set out market infrastructure initiatives to support a digital-native Hong Kong bond market, with detail on tokenisation left to his keynote speech.

## Key claims

- The HKMA and the Treasury Markets Association jointly organised the Summit, held in Hong Kong on 23 September 2026.
- Eddie Yue, HKMA Chief Executive and Honorary President of the TMA Council, gave the keynote address on bond market development.
- He outlined initiatives to encourage participation and bond issuance, deepen offshore RMB liquidity and modernise market infrastructure.
- The stated aim of the infrastructure work is to support bond market development and promote digital and cross-border fixed-income activities.
- He called on practitioners to build a "diversified, deep, dynamic and digital-native bond market".
- One panel covered the role of technology in transforming treasury market operations; over 300 participants attended.
- Secondary coverage of the keynote reports a plan to pilot tokenisation of Exchange Fund Bills; the press release itself does not state this.
