# HKMC's HK$12 billion inaugural public digital bond issuance

## Citation

Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation Limited / Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMC's HK$12 billion inaugural public digital bond issuance". HKMA Press Releases, 11 June 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/06/20260611-3/.

## One-line summary

The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation Limited priced HK$12 billion equivalent in inaugural digital bonds across three tranches, setting a record as the largest digital bond issuance globally and the longest-tenor HKD digital bond to date.

## Key claims

- The issuance comprised three tranches: HK$6 billion 2-year, HK$2.5 billion 5-year, and CNH 3 billion 3-year bonds.
- The HK$12 billion equivalent total represents the largest digital bond issuance globally as at June 2026.
- The 5-year HKD tranche set a new benchmark as the longest tenor for an HKD-denominated digital bond.
- Settlement was shortened from five business days to three business days using the Central Moneymarkets Unit's distributed ledger technology platform.
- The offering attracted over 100 investor accounts with a peak orderbook of approximately HK$24 billion equivalent.
- The HKMC is the first public sector entity in Hong Kong to issue digital bonds to the public.

## Significance

- Demonstrates that DLT-based settlement shortening (T+5 to T+3) is operationally achievable at scale in a public-sector issuance, providing evidence for further market adoption.
- The HKMC's status as a public body gives the issuance sovereign-adjacent credit, establishing a benchmark for risk pricing in tokenised bonds.
- Positions Hong Kong's Central Moneymarkets Unit DLT platform as proven market infrastructure for digital fixed-income securities.
- Supports the Tokenised Bond Expert Group's policy work with a live at-scale proof of concept.
