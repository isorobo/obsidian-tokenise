# Building the Financial System of the Future: Trusted, Connected and Resilient

## Citation

Chia Der Jiun. "Building the Financial System of the Future: Trusted, Connected and Resilient". MAS Speeches, 11 September 2026 (special address, Global FinTech Fest 2026, Mumbai). https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-the-financial-system-of-the-future---trusted-connected-and-resilient-by-md-for-gff-2026.

## One-line summary

The MAS Managing Director sets out MAS's approach to AI governance and agent accountability (including SAFR), and places tokenisation as a technology that will take a few more years to scale.

## Key claims

- The speech steers regulators toward supporting innovation while keeping it on a safe and sustainable course. I read the text through the BIS reprint (dated 21 September 2026), because the mas.gov.sg page was not fetched in full.
- MAS cites its Gen AI risk framework (2023) and AI Risk Management Handbooks (2025), with supervisory Guidelines for AI Risk Management under public consultation.
- MAS introduced SAFR (Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime) in July 2026, covering agent identity verification, action evaluation before execution and audit records.
- The speech stresses accountability, oversight and governance as AI agents take on more consequential tasks.
- Tokenisation appears briefly, alongside AI and quantum computing, as a technology that may take a few more years to scale.
- Search summaries of the speech also report Singapore-India payments connectivity through Nexus. I did not verify that point in the reprint text.

## Excerpts

> "As AI agents take on more consequential tasks and are given more autonomy, it is essential to ensure clear accountability, oversight and governance."
> ~ Speech, section on agentic AI

> "support innovation for competitiveness and growth; and also steer it on a course that is safe and sustainable."
> ~ Speech, section on AI governance

## Significance

- It is a senior-level restatement of the SAFR approach, useful as a dated policy anchor for agentic-finance supervision in Singapore.
- It signals that MAS sees tokenisation scaling more slowly than AI, which frames expectations for the Singapore FinTech Festival in November 2026.
- Only a light tokenisation link, so its value is mainly for the agentic-AI topic.
