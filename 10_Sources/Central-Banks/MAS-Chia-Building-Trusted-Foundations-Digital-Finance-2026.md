# Building Trusted Foundations for Digital Finance

## Citation

Chia Der Jiun. "Building Trusted Foundations for Digital Finance". MAS Speeches (Keynote Address at the INSEAD Digital Finance & Agentic AI Summit), 9 October 2026. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/building-trusted-foundations-for-digital-finance.

## One-line summary

The MAS Managing Director sets out Singapore's next steps for digital finance: BLOOM settlement work, tokenised MAS Bills settled in wholesale CBDC, the stablecoin framework, GL1 toolkits and SAFR v1.1 for agentic finance.

## Key claims

- Tokenisation's feasibility and value are well accepted, and Project Guardian established this; the challenge now is scaling commercial adoption.
- Tokenised transactions will only scale if settled in safe and trusted digital money, with central bank money as the anchor for settlement finality.
- MAS advances settlement using digital money, including tokenised bank deposits and regulated stablecoins, through its BLOOM platform.
- The next step is to settle tokenised MAS Bills using SGD wholesale CBDC.
- MAS has consulted on legislative amendments to implement the Single-Currency Stablecoin framework (reserve backing, capital, redemption at par, disclosure, recognition of foreign stablecoins).
- Through Global Layer One (GL1), MAS expects a network of interconnected ledgers rather than a single global ledger, and GL1 published a Market Infrastructure Toolkit and a Programmable Compliance Toolkit.
- On agentic finance, SAFR v1.1 and an open-source reference implementation (released through the Future of Finance Institute) support controls over agent identity, authority and accountability.

## Significance

- A primary MAS statement from 9 October 2026 that consolidates the Guardian, BLOOM, GL1 and SAFR workstreams, five weeks before the Singapore FinTech Festival.
- Confirms the tokenised MAS Bills settled in wholesale CBDC as the next phase after the 2025 SGD Testnet trials.
- Links the stablecoin consultation with the settlement-asset argument, useful for singleness-of-money analysis.
