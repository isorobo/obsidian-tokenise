# Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management for Financial Institutions

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore. "Guidelines on Artificial Intelligence Risk Management for Financial Institutions". MAS Guidelines, 7 October 2026. https://www.mas.gov.sg/regulation/guidelines/guidelines-on-artificial-intelligence-risk-management-for-financial-institutions.

## One-line summary

MAS finalised cross-sector supervisory expectations for AI risk management at all financial institutions, effective 7 October 2027, with separate agentic AI guidance flagged for later consultation.

## Key claims

- The guidelines apply to all financial institutions and all forms of AI technology (MAS guidelines page).
- They set out MAS supervisory expectations for managing risks arising from AI use, scaled to an institution's size and the nature of its AI use (MAS guidelines page).
- The guidelines take effect on 7 October 2027 (MAS guidelines page); Blockhead reports that some sections apply from October 2027 and others from October 2028.
- Per Blockhead's coverage, boards and senior management should oversee AI risk without a mandatory dedicated AI committee, and firms must keep AI inventories, assess materiality of each use case and apply lifecycle controls.
- Per Blockhead, institutions remain accountable for third-party AI and should consider limiting, suspending or replacing a vendor service whose risks exceed their risk appetite.
- Per Blockhead, MAS plans to consult the industry in 2027 on further agentic AI guidance.
- The full PDF could not be text-extracted in this pass, so detail beyond the MAS summary rests on secondary coverage.

## Significance

- This is the first final, binding-in-expectation MAS instrument on AI governance in financial supervision, and it frames the later agentic AI work.
- It sits beside the MAS SAFR runtime safeguards for agentic finance and the parliamentary reply on agentic AI, which cover the agent-specific layer.
- Secondary coverage (Blockhead, 8 October 2026) was used for the agentic AI timing; verify against the PDF before relying on section-level dates.
