# MAS Consults on Legislative Amendments to Implement Stablecoin Regulatory Framework

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore. "MAS Consults on Legislative Amendments to Implement Stablecoin Regulatory Framework". MAS Media Releases, 1 September 2026. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-consults-on-legislative-amendments-to-implement-stablecoin-regulatory-framework.

## One-line summary

MAS has published a consultation paper (responses due 16 October 2026) proposing Payment Services Act 2019 amendments that turn its MAS Single-Currency Stablecoin (MAS-SCS) framework into statute.

## Key claims

- MAS published a consultation paper on 1 September 2026 on proposed amendments to the Payment Services Act 2019 to implement its stablecoin framework.
- Only issuers licensed under the MAS-SCS framework may describe themselves as licensed MAS-regulated stablecoin issuers or call their coins "MAS-regulated stablecoins".
- The paper covers key requirements on value stability, capital, redemption at par and disclosure.
- MAS proposes to prohibit interest payments on MAS-regulated stablecoins, require stress testing and require recovery and wind-down plans for issuers.
- MAS proposes to recognise foreign-issued stablecoins under comparable regulatory frameworks and to permit multi-jurisdictional issuance where risks are mitigated, including Singapore and foreign issuer joint ventures.
- The release also proposes safeguarding of customer funds received before stablecoins are issued.
- Feedback closes on 16 October 2026 through a FormSG portal. Law-firm summaries (Reed Smith, Baker McKenzie) report the same date and proposal set.

## Excerpts

> "The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) today published a consultation paper on proposed legislative amendments to the Payment Services Act 2019 (PS Act) to implement MAS' regulatory framework for stablecoins in Singapore."
> ~ Media release, opening paragraph

> "The MAS Single-Currency Stablecoin (MAS-SCS) framework permits only issuers licensed under the framework to describe themselves as licensed MAS-regulated stablecoin issuers and represent their stablecoins as 'MAS-regulated stablecoins'."
> ~ Media release, body

> "MAS' proposed legislative amendments will give effect to a stablecoin framework that promotes responsible financial innovation."
> ~ Ms Ho Hern Shin, Deputy Managing Director (Financial Supervision), media release

## Significance

- This is the first primary MAS legislative step on stablecoins that the channel has seen; earlier runs had logged "no bill or consultation" through August 2026.
- It moves the framework previewed at the November 2025 Singapore FinTech Festival from policy to proposed statute, and sets a recognition route for foreign stablecoins.
- It complements the tokenised-settlement work under BLOOM and the Hong Kong stablecoin licensing sources already in the vault.
