# Stablecoins Meet the Mundell-Fleming Trilemma

## Citation

Azar, Pablo; Farboodi, Maryam; Sinha, Nish D. "Stablecoins Meet the Mundell-Fleming Trilemma". Federal Reserve Bank of New York Staff Reports, no. 1202, August 2026. https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1202.

## One-line summary

Wallet-level evidence and a small-open-economy model show that USD stablecoin adoption under crisis pressure weakens capital controls and so tightens the Mundell-Fleming trilemma.

## Key claims

- The authors build a wallet-level dataset linking geotagged Ethereum Name Service registrations to stablecoin transactions around banking restrictions, currency crises, sanctions and monetary disruptions.
- Crisis-country wallets show significant increases in USD stablecoin inflows and receipt activity during crisis weeks.
- A small-open-economy New Keynesian model has household adoption of programmable stablecoins weaken the government's enforcement technology for capital controls.
- The evidence supports the model's central assumption that flight pressure increases stablecoin adoption.
- The paper concludes that stablecoins reduce a government's ability to sustain independent monetary policy under a fixed exchange rate.
- The report page gives the date as August 2026; a Paul Hastings regulatory update dates its publication 26 August 2026, which the note uses.
