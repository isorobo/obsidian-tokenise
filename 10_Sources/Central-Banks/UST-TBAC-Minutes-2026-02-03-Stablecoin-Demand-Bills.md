# Minutes of the Meeting of the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee February 3, 2026

## Citation

U.S. Department of the Treasury. "Minutes of the Meeting of the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee February 3, 2026". Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee Minutes, 4 February 2026. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0386.

## One-line summary

The February 2026 TBAC minutes list potential stablecoin demand among the structural shifts shaping Treasury demand, alongside Federal Reserve bill purchases.

## Key claims

- A presenter named "potential demand associated with stablecoins" as one of several shifts shaping demand for Treasury securities.
- Respondents cited anticipated Federal Reserve purchases of Treasury bills as supportive of demand.
- Some dealers warned that a SOFR floating-rate note idea risked cannibalising demand for Treasury bills.
- The committee's balance sheet presentation said it would be reasonable to meet some portion of SOMA demand for bills through more bill issuance.
- The minutes mention stablecoins briefly and do not discuss tokenisation or digital assets in detail.

## Significance

- It shows stablecoin demand has moved from a stand-alone committee charge (April 2025) to a standard line item in the debt managers' demand analysis.
- It gives the official US debt-management view that complements the supervisory and rule-making sources on GENIUS Act reserves.
- The mention is brief, so treat it as a signal of mainstreaming rather than a quantitative estimate.
