# ESMA 2027-2029 Programming Document

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA). "ESMA 2027-2029 Programming Document". ESMA, 29 January 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-02/ESMA22-50751485-1625_-_2027-2029_Programming_Document.pdf.

## One-line summary

ESMA's medium-term plan commits to integrating DLT and tokenisation into the EU regulatory framework, advancing MiCA supervisory convergence, and supporting the Savings and Investments Union through 2027-2029.

## Key claims

- ESMA will develop regulatory and convergence measures to integrate DLT and tokenisation into the EU framework following the joint ESMA-EBA report under MiCA.
- The T+1 settlement transition, scheduled for October 2027, is a central infrastructure milestone in the programming period.
- ESMA will deploy the second phase of its MiCA integrated monitoring tool in 2027, embedding real-time supervisory data on crypto-asset markets.
- The European Single Access Point (ESAP) public roll-out forms part of the digital data infrastructure priorities for 2027.
- Supervisory convergence on CASP authorisation and market abuse monitoring remains a multi-year commitment through 2029.
- Simplification and burden reduction under the Savings and Investments Union (SIU) reform package will reshape Level 2 instruments across ESMA's remit.
