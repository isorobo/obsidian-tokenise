# Statement to Support the Smooth Implementation of MiCA Standards and Format Requirements

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "Statement to Support the Smooth Implementation of MiCA Standards and Format Requirements." ESMA Publications, 28 November 2025. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-11/ESMA75-1303207761-6284_Statement_to_support_the_smooth_implementation_of_MiCA_standards_and_format.pdf.

## One-line summary

ESMA sets out the mandatory data standards and machine-readable formats that crypto-asset issuers and service providers must adopt for white papers and order book records under MiCA from December 2025.

## Key claims

- MiCA white paper formatting requirements, including mandatory iXBRL format, entered application on 23 December 2025.
- ESMA published a standardised, machine-readable JSON schema for crypto-asset order book and records-keeping; NCAs expect to begin requesting data in this format within six months of the 28 November 2025 publication.
- ESMA released three specimen white paper workbooks in October 2025 covering e-money tokens, asset-referenced tokens, and other crypto-asset categories.
- The statement provides an overview of all MiCA data standards and white paper formatting requirements issued by ESMA, consolidating prior technical instruments.
- The iXBRL and JSON requirements together establish a structured data architecture for MiCA supervision, enabling automated regulatory reporting.

## Significance

- This statement marks the point at which MiCA supervision becomes data-driven: machine-readable white papers and standardised order book feeds give NCAs real-time market monitoring capability.
- The iXBRL requirement for white papers imports the EU taxonomy approach from securities regulation (ESEF) into the crypto-asset domain, creating a direct bridge between traditional and digital capital markets reporting.
- JSON order book standards allow automated market abuse detection, extending the July 2025 supervisory guidelines into a technical infrastructure layer.
- For the wiki's market-infrastructure and market-structure coverage, this document closes the gap between MiCA's legal obligations and their practical enforcement architecture.
