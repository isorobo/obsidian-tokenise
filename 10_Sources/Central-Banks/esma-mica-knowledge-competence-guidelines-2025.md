# Final Report on the Guidelines for the Criteria on the Assessment of Knowledge and Competence under MiCA

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority. "Final Report on the Guidelines for the Criteria on the Assessment of Knowledge and Competence under MiCA." ESMA Publications, 11 July 2025. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-07/ESMA35-1872330276-2380_Final_Report_on_MiCA_Guidelines_on_knowledge_and_competence.pdf.

## One-line summary

ESMA specifies the minimum knowledge and competence standards that crypto-asset service provider staff must meet before advising or providing information on crypto-assets to clients.

## Key claims

- The guidelines establish minimum knowledge standards for CASP staff who provide information or advice on crypto-assets, targeting investor protection and market confidence.
- Standards address crypto-specific risk factors including high volatility, cybersecurity risks, and the technical complexity of blockchain-based products.
- NCAs must implement the guidelines six months after their official publication in all EU languages.
- NCAs must notify ESMA of their compliance status within two months of official publication.
- The consultation process ran from February to April 2025 before the final report was issued in July 2025.
- The guidelines complement the MiCA investor protection requirements under Title V by establishing staff-level competence floors across all EU jurisdictions.

## Excerpts

> "A key objective of the guidelines is to ensure a minimum level of knowledge and competence of staff through examples, including on professional qualification and appropriate experience for the provision of information or advice."
> ~ Section 1: Objectives

> "Guidelines address crypto-market characteristics like the high volatility of crypto-assets and cyber security risks."
> ~ Section 3: Risk-Specific Criteria

## Significance

- The guidelines close a gap in MiCA implementation by setting a floor for staff competence, directly comparable to the MiFID II knowledge and competence regime for investment advisers.
- By requiring NCAs to enforce these standards across 27 member states, ESMA drives convergence in how CASPs are supervised at the individual staff level.
- For the wiki's consumer protection and licensing doctrine coverage, this document establishes the EU standard against which other jurisdictions (MAS, FCA) will be compared.
- The cybersecurity and volatility criteria establish that MiCA advice standards are crypto-specific rather than a simple port of traditional securities requirements.
