# Guidelines for the Criteria on the Assessment of Knowledge and Competence under MiCA

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA). "Guidelines for the Criteria on the Assessment of Knowledge and Competence under MiCA". ESMA, 28 January 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-01/ESMA35-24871704-2922_Guidelines_for_the_criteria_on_the_assessment_of_knowledge_and_competence_under_MiCA.pdf.

## One-line summary

ESMA publishes final guidelines specifying the knowledge and competence criteria that crypto-asset service provider staff must satisfy under MiCA, translating the July 2025 final report into binding supervisory standards.

## Key claims

- The guidelines set minimum knowledge and competence criteria for CASP staff who provide advice on, or information about, crypto-assets to clients.
- Staff must demonstrate understanding of the characteristics, risks, and regulatory status of the crypto-assets they advise on.
- National Competent Authorities must apply the guidelines by incorporating them into their supervisory practices for CASP authorisation assessments.
- The guidelines complement the July 2025 Final Report on the same topic, converting recommendations into enforceable standards.
- The document is part of ESMA's Level 3 MiCA implementation work programme alongside market abuse and reverse solicitation guidelines.

## Excerpts

> "These guidelines apply to crypto-asset service providers providing advice on crypto-assets or providing portfolio management of crypto-assets."
> ~ ESMA35-24871704-2922, January 2026

## Significance

- These guidelines are the binding output from the July 2025 final report already indexed in this vault, making them the operative legal standard for CASP staff qualifications across the EU.
- They extend the MiCA licensing regime into human capital requirements, adding a layer to the vault's analysis of how MiCA structures the CASP market.
- The knowledge requirements cover crypto-asset characteristics including tokenised instruments, connecting this source to the vault's instrument-level analysis.
