# Statement on MiCA Transitional Measures

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA). "Statement on MiCA Transitional Measures". ESMA, 4 December 2025. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-12/ESMA75-113276571-1631_Statement_on_end_of_MiCA_transitional_periods.pdf.

## One-line summary

ESMA sets out supervisory expectations for unauthorised crypto-asset service providers operating under national grandfathering arrangements ahead of the 1 July 2026 MiCA deadline.

## Key claims

- Crypto-asset service providers (CASPs) not yet authorised under MiCA must implement orderly wind-down plans for services offered in Member States where the transitional period has ended.
- Unauthorised CASPs in Member States with ongoing transitional periods must hold wind-down plans ready for immediate implementation if authorisation is not obtained before the national deadline.
- Authorised CASPs must actively manage the migration of existing clients from unauthorised providers ahead of 1 July 2026.
- ESMA co-ordinates with National Competent Authorities (NCAs) to promote consistent supervisory approaches across Member States.
- Custody and certain other MiCA services cannot be outsourced to non-EU or unauthorised third-country entities.

## Excerpts

> "ESMA sets out its expectation that CASPs not yet authorised under MiCA have implemented orderly wind-down plans for the services they provided in Member States in which the transitional period is over."
> ~ ESMA Statement ESMA75-113276571-1631, December 2025

> "Authorised CASPs [should] actively manage the migration of existing clients ahead of 1 July 2026, with authorised CASPs taking the necessary steps to onboard existing EU clients before the end of the transitional period."
> ~ ESMA Statement ESMA75-113276571-1631, December 2025

## Significance

- This statement marks the first time ESMA formally required wind-down planning from unauthorised CASPs, establishing a template for regulatory off-boarding obligations under MiCA.
- The co-ordination mandate with NCAs signals ESMA's shift to active supervisory convergence on crypto-asset authorisation, directly relevant to the vault's stablecoins and licensing doctrine coverage.
- The client migration requirement creates cross-border obligations linking this statement to ongoing analysis of MiCA's jurisdictional scope.
