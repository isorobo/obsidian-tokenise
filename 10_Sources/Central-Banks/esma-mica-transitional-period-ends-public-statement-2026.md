# Public Statement: MiCA Transitional Period Ends

## Citation

European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA). "Public Statement: MiCA Transitional Period Ends". ESMA, 23 June 2026. https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/ESMA75-113276571-1710_Public_Statement_MiCA_transitional_period_ends.pdf.

## One-line summary

Eight days before the 1 July 2026 deadline, ESMA calls on unauthorised CASPs to wind down EU services immediately and warns retail investors of risks from using unlicensed providers after that date.

## Key claims

- Unauthorised CASPs must stop onboarding new EU clients at once and restrict services to selling, transferring, or closing existing positions.
- CASPs established outside the EU cannot provide MiCA services or solicit EU clients, including in business-to-business contexts.
- Onboarding CASPs receiving client transfers must complete full customer due diligence and AML/CFT checks on migrating clients.
- ESMA and NCAs directly engage with significant unauthorised cross-border CASPs to monitor orderly wind-down with a focus on client protection, financial stability, and market integrity.
- Retail investors are warned that using unlicensed crypto-asset service providers after 1 July 2026 carries elevated risk with no regulatory protection.

## Excerpts

> "ESMA calls on unauthorised crypto-asset service providers to wind down orderly, while also safeguarding clients' interests, as the transitional period under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) ends on 1 July 2026."
> ~ ESMA75-113276571-1710, 23 June 2026

> "Immediately stop onboarding new EU clients, refrain from opening new client relationships or accounts, and cease marketing activities and solicitation."
> ~ ESMA75-113276571-1710, 23 June 2026

> "ESMA and NCAs are directly engaged with the entities concerned and will coordinate to monitor whether significant unauthorised cross-border CASPs wind down without delay, with a focus on client protection, financial stability and market integrity."
> ~ ESMA75-113276571-1710, 23 June 2026

## Significance

- This is the final ESMA public statement before the 1 July 2026 MiCA deadline, serving as the definitive contemporaneous record of ESMA's enforcement posture at the transition point.
- The retail investor warning signals ESMA's consumer protection mandate operating in parallel with licensing enforcement, relevant to the vault's coverage of market-abuse and disclosure doctrine.
- The direct NCA co-ordination on cross-border wind-down establishes precedent for how EU regulators may manage future transitions affecting tokenised-asset markets.
