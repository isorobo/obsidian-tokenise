# In the Shadow of Bank Runs: Lessons from the Silicon Valley Bank Failure and Its Impact on Stablecoins

## Citation

Du, Chuan, Ria Sonawane, and Cy Watsky. "In the Shadow of Bank Runs: Lessons from the Silicon Valley Bank Failure and Its Impact on Stablecoins". FEDS Notes, Federal Reserve Board, 17 December 2025. https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/in-the-shadow-of-bank-run-lessons-from-the-silicon-valley-bank-failure-and-its-impact-on-stablecoins-20251217.html.

## One-line summary

The March 2023 SVB collapse transmitted run dynamics to USDC and across the stablecoin market, demonstrating bidirectional contagion between traditional banking and crypto-asset ecosystems.

## Key claims

- SVB's failure in March 2023 triggered material redemption pressure on USDC because Circle held a significant share of USDC reserves at SVB, constraining access to backing assets and eroding holder confidence.
- Contagion ran bidirectionally: traditional banking stress propagated into stablecoin markets, while stablecoin liquidations created potential spillback into Treasury markets through asset sales.
- Dai's Peg Stability Modules acted as amplifiers, enabling automated arbitrage-driven conversion that dragged down stablecoins with no direct SVB exposure.
- High-quality liquid reserve composition reduced but did not eliminate run risk: the SVB episode shows that reserve quality is insufficient without assured and immediate redemption access.
- The episode provides empirical support for regulatory requirements that payment stablecoin issuers maintain segregated, immediately accessible reserve accounts.

## Excerpts

> "Stablecoins function as run-able liabilities for their issuers: holders can demand immediate redemption, creating the same structural fragility present in bank demand deposits."

## Significance

- Provides direct Federal Reserve staff evidence of stablecoin run dynamics under banking stress, relevant to the vault's stablecoins domain and reserve-requirements doctrine.
- Documents the transmission mechanism linking traditional bank failure to crypto-asset market disruption, informing cross-sector systemic risk analysis.
- Supports the vault's analysis of the GENIUS Act reserve requirements: the SVB episode underlines why immediate and segregated reserve access is the correct regulatory design.
- Establishes empirical benchmarks for evaluating whether future stablecoin regulatory frameworks adequately address contagion and peg stability.
