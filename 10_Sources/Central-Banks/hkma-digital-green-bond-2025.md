# HKSAR Government's Third Digital Green Bonds Offering

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKSAR Government's Third Digital Green Bonds Offering". HKMA Press Releases, 11 November 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/11/20251111-6/.

## One-line summary

Hong Kong issued the world's largest digital bond at approximately HK$10 billion across four currencies, integrating e-HKD and e-CNY tokenised central bank money in settlement for the first time globally.

## Key claims

- The offering totalled approximately HK$10 billion across HKD, RMB, USD, and EUR tranches, constituting the largest digital bond issuance in the world to date.
- Settlement used both tokenised central bank money and traditional settlement rails, making this the first digital bond in the world to integrate both e-CNY and e-HKD in the settlement process.
- The tokenised central bank money settlement path reduced settlement time, costs, and counterparty credit risk compared with traditional settlement.
- The bonds received Digital Token Identifiers under ISO 24165 standards and adopted the International Capital Market Association's Bond Data Taxonomy.
- The cross-border integration of e-CNY and e-HKD demonstrates interoperability between two sovereign CBDC systems in a live commercial transaction.

## Excerpts

> "The integration of tokenised central bank money in this issuance lays the foundation for future integration with other forms of digital money."
> ~ Eddie Yue, HKMA Chief Executive

> "The HKSAR Government's third issuance of digital green bond attracted overwhelming subscription."
> ~ Paul Chan, Financial Secretary

## Significance

- Establishes a live cross-border CBDC interoperability precedent between HK and mainland China, extending tokenised settlement beyond sandbox environments.
- The ISO 24165 Digital Token Identifier adoption signals the operationalisation of international standards in sovereign digital bond issuance.
- The HK$10 billion scale and four-currency structure makes this a reference transaction for tokenised-bond market-size arguments in the vault.
- Demonstrates that tokenised central bank money (wCBDC) can operate alongside traditional rails in the same issuance, informing hybrid settlement architecture design.
