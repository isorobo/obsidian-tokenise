# HKMA completes e-HKD Pilot Programme and outlines future direction of e-HKD

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA completes e-HKD Pilot Programme and outlines future direction of e-HKD". HKMA Press Releases, 28 October 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/10/20251028-4/.

## One-line summary

After two phases of piloting, the HKMA deprioritises retail e-HKD deployment and pivots to wholesale payments and tokenisation ecosystem support as the primary near-term direction.

## Key claims

- Phase 2 of the e-HKD Pilot Programme evaluated 11 industry projects across three themes: tokenised asset settlement, programmability, and offline payments.
- Users regarded e-HKD and tokenised bank deposits as interchangeable, reflecting high trust in Hong Kong's banking system rather than demand for a separate retail CBDC.
- The HKMA will focus near-term e-HKD development on wholesale payments and cross-border transactions rather than retail issuance.
- Preparatory work for potential future retail expansion is targeted for completion by mid-2026.
- A standardised token framework will be released to facilitate programmable digital money adoption across the Hong Kong ecosystem.

## Excerpts

> "The two phases of the e-HKD Pilot Programme have yielded insightful findings that shape the HKMA's understanding of the future of digital money."
> ~ Eddie Yue, HKMA Chief Executive

## Significance

- The retail-to-wholesale pivot is a significant policy signal: public perception of fungibility between e-HKD and bank deposits suggests CBDC differentiation is low in a stable-currency jurisdiction.
- The standardised token framework will define programmability standards for tokenised commercial bank deposits, informing how settlement rails are designed across Project Ensemble.
- The mid-2026 retail readiness milestone and the forthcoming token framework are trackable legislative-process markers for this vault.
- The Phase 2 Report (attached PDF) constitutes the primary quantitative evidence base for HK digital money policy through at least 2026.
