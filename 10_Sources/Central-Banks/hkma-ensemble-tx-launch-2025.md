# HKMA announces the new phase of Project Ensemble to support real-value transactions in tokenised deposits and digital assets

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA announces the new phase of Project Ensemble to support real-value transactions in tokenised deposits and digital assets". HKMA Press Releases, 13 November 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/11/20251113-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA launched EnsembleTX, moving Project Ensemble from sandbox experimentation into a live pilot that executes real-value transactions in tokenised deposits and digital assets.

## Key claims

- EnsembleTX marks the transition from proof-of-concept sandbox testing to a live, real-value pilot operating throughout 2026.
- The initial pilot focus is on tokenised money market fund transactions and real-time liquidity management using tokenised deposits.
- Interbank settlement initially runs through the HKD Real Time Gross Settlement system, with a planned upgrade to 24/7 tokenised central bank money settlement.
- The HKMA and the Securities and Futures Commission collaborate closely through the Ensemble Architecture Community to advance interoperability standards.
- The pilot builds on the Ensemble Sandbox, which since August 2024 tested end-to-end digital asset settlement using experimental tokenised deposits.

## Excerpts

> "EnsembleTX marks a pivotal moment...upgrading from proof-of-concept to a real-value setting."
> ~ Eddie Yue, HKMA Chief Executive

> "A critical step...is today's initiative...allowing interbank settlement of tokenised deposits in real time 24/7."
> ~ Julia Leung, SFC Chief Executive Officer

## Significance

- Signals Hong Kong's progression from regulatory sandboxing to live commercial tokenisation infrastructure, making it the first jurisdiction to operate a multi-bank tokenised deposit settlement pilot.
- Establishes the Ensemble Architecture Community standard as the interoperability framework for tokenised commercial bank money and tokenised assets in HK.
- Demonstrates the HKMA-SFC regulatory alignment model for tokenisation oversight, relevant to cross-jurisdictional comparisons with MAS Project Guardian.
- The 24/7 tokenised central bank money upgrade roadmap is directly relevant to wholesale CBDC settlement research.
