# The HKMA Unveils "Fintech 2030" at the Hong Kong FinTech Week 2025

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "The HKMA Unveils 'Fintech 2030' at the Hong Kong FinTech Week 2025". HKMA Press Releases, 3 November 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/11/20251103-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA announced Fintech 2030, a five-year strategy built on four DART pillars that commits Hong Kong to tokenising government bonds, launching Project Ensemble's live pilot, and enabling blockchain settlement via e-HKD, tokenised deposits, and regulated stablecoins.

## Key claims

- The Fintech 2030 strategy organises HKMA's fintech priorities under four pillars: Digital Money, Asset Tokenisation, Reimagined Infrastructure, and Technology Adoption (DART).
- The HKMA will accelerate real-world asset tokenisation, including financial assets and Exchange Fund papers alongside government bonds.
- Settlement on blockchains will be enabled through three digital money forms: e-HKD, tokenised commercial bank deposits, and regulated stablecoins.
- The HKMA confirmed it would launch the Project Ensemble pilot to enable real-value tokenised transactions, subsequently fulfilled on 13 November 2025.
- Collaborative use-case incubation will continue with industry stakeholders and other central banks through 2030.

## Excerpts

## Significance

- Fintech 2030 is the governing strategic document for all HKMA tokenisation and digital money activity through 2030, making it the anchoring policy reference for this channel.
- The explicit equivalence of three settlement instruments (e-HKD, tokenised deposits, regulated stablecoins) reflects a multi-rail policy model with direct implications for stablecoin licensing and CBDC design.
- The commitment to tokenise Exchange Fund papers extends sovereign tokenised-asset precedent beyond green bonds to the HKMA's own balance sheet instruments.
- The DART framework provides a structured taxonomy against which future HKMA publications can be categorised within this vault.
