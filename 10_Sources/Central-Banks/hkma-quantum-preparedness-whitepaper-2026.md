# HKMA launches quantum preparedness whitepaper and index to support banking sector's readiness for quantum era

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "HKMA launches quantum preparedness whitepaper and index to support banking sector's readiness for quantum era". HKMA Press Releases, 27 July 2026. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/07/20260727-3/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA published a whitepaper and a Quantum Preparedness Index scoring Hong Kong banks 2.3 out of 10, warning that tokenised deposits, digital assets, and blockchain settlement depend on cryptography quantum computers could eventually break.

## Key claims

- The Quantum Preparedness Index scores Hong Kong's banking sector 2.3 out of 10 across awareness, planning, pilots, and practical preparedness.
- The HKMA sets a target of full readiness, a score of 10, by 2030.
- Around 68% of surveyed banks show some quantum awareness or have entered planning or pilot stages, while 32% have not started.
- Roughly half of surveyed banks have no formal post-quantum cryptography migration plan.
- The whitepaper states that DLT applications rely on cryptography for core functions, and that a sufficiently powerful quantum computer could break RSA and elliptic-curve cryptography using Shor's algorithm.
- The HKMA links the quantum threat directly to its tokenisation programme, citing HK$16.8 billion in tokenised green bonds issued since 2023, HK$29 billion in tokenised deposits, and over HK$14 billion in bank-custodied digital assets by end-2025.
- The HKMA is developing a post-quantum cryptography toolkit with the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and running workshops to build sector-wide cryptographic agility.

## Excerpts

> "Financial systems such as secure payment networks and distributed ledger technology (DLT) applications rely on cryptography for core functions."
> ~ whitepaper, quantum threat section (as reported by cryptotimes.io, 28 July 2026)

> "Computing equals productivity now. But we can't sacrifice security while making innovations."
> ~ Arthur Yuen Kwok-hang, HKMA Deputy Chief Executive (as reported by South China Morning Post, 27 July 2026)

> "It was important to prepare now."
> ~ Carmen Chu Lap-kiu, HKMA Executive Director of Banking Supervision, on the five-to-ten-year quantum timeline (as reported by South China Morning Post, 27 July 2026)

## Significance

- Names cryptographic risk to tokenised deposits, digital-asset custody, and blockchain settlement as a supervisory priority, connecting quantum resilience directly to the wiki's tokenisation corpus.
- Quantifies HKMA's own tokenisation programme for the first time in a single document: HK$16.8 billion in tokenised green bonds, HK$29 billion in tokenised deposits, and HK$14 billion in custodied digital assets.
- Sets a 2030 supervisory deadline for post-quantum cryptography, giving tokenised-asset infrastructure providers a concrete compliance horizon.
- Extends the HKMA's Fintech 2030 resilience pillar from conventional payment security into the cryptographic foundations of Project Ensemble, tokenised bonds, and licensed stablecoins.
