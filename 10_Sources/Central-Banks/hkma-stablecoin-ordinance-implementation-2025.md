# Implementation of regulatory regime for stablecoin issuers

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Implementation of regulatory regime for stablecoin issuers". HKMA Press Releases, 29 July 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/07/20250729-4/.

## One-line summary

The HKMA confirmed the Stablecoins Ordinance commences on 1 August 2025, published four regulatory documents, and warned the public that no licence had yet been issued.

## Key claims

- The Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656) commenced operation on 1 August 2025.
- Four regulatory documents took effect simultaneously: the Guideline on Supervision of Stablecoin Issuers and the AML/CFT Guideline for licensed stablecoin issuers, plus transitional provisions documents.
- As at 29 July 2025, no licence had been issued by the HKMA under the new regime.
- The application deadline for the first batch of licences was 30 September 2025; the HKMA indicated first licences would likely be granted in early 2026.
- Members of the public holding unlicensed stablecoins do so at their own risk; falsely claiming licensee status is a criminal offence.
