# Government welcomes passage of the Stablecoins Bill

## Citation

Hong Kong Monetary Authority. "Government welcomes passage of the Stablecoins Bill". HKMA Press Releases, 21 May 2025. https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2025/05/20250521-3/.

## One-line summary

The Hong Kong Legislative Council passed the Stablecoins Bill, establishing a mandatory licensing regime for fiat-referenced stablecoin issuers under HKMA supervision.

## Key claims

- The Stablecoins Ordinance creates a licensing regime requiring any person issuing fiat-referenced stablecoins in Hong Kong, or issuing HKD-pegged stablecoins anywhere, to obtain a licence from the Monetary Authority.
- Licensed issuers must maintain proper segregation of client assets, robust stabilisation mechanisms, and par-value redemption at reasonable conditions.
- Retail investors may only receive fiat-referenced stablecoins from licensed issuers; advertising by non-licensed entities is prohibited during the transitional period.
- AML/CFT compliance, risk management, and fitness and propriety assessments are mandatory licence conditions.
- The Ordinance was expected to commence operation in 2025, subsequently confirmed as 1 August 2025.

> "The passage of the Stablecoins Bill establishes a comprehensive regulatory framework for fiat-referenced stablecoin issuers."

## Significance

- This is the primary statute establishing stablecoin regulation in Hong Kong, making it the anchoring law source for any HK stablecoin analysis in the vault.
- The HKD-peg extraterritorial provision extends HK jurisdiction to offshore issuers, a significant choice-of-law implication for cross-border stablecoin structuring.
- Par-value redemption and reserve segregation requirements directly mirror e-money token frameworks in EU MiCA, enabling direct doctrinal comparison.
- The licensing timeline (bill May 2025, commencement August 2025, first licences early 2026) is a concrete regulatory-process sequence for this vault's timeline artefact.
