# MAS Launches BLOOM Initiative to Extend Settlement Capabilities

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore. "MAS Launches BLOOM Initiative to Extend Settlement Capabilities". MAS Media Releases, 16 October 2025. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-launches-bloom-initiative-to-extend-settlement-capabilities.

## One-line summary

MAS launched BLOOM (Borderless, Liquid, Open, Online, Multi-currency) to enable wholesale settlement in tokenised bank liabilities and regulated stablecoins across G10 and Asian currencies.

## Key claims

- BLOOM extends settlement infrastructure built under Project Orchid to cover tokenised bank liabilities and regulated stablecoins as live settlement assets.
- The initiative operates across three workstreams: distribution and clearing of settlement assets, programmable compliance controls, and agentic payments.
- Circle, DBS, OCBC, Partior, Stripe, and UOB lead the distribution and clearing workstream.
- Ant International and StraitsX lead programmable compliance controls to automate cross-border checks.
- Coinbase and DBS lead the agentic payments workstream, using AI to execute transactions under predefined conditions.
- Wholesale use cases include corporate treasury management, trade finance, and agentic payments across domestic and cross-border rails.

## Excerpts

> "Project Orchid established the technical competencies necessary to support the digital Singapore dollar and explored potential use cases for it. BLOOM takes this further, enhancing the range of settlement asset options for participants."
> ~ Kenneth Gay, Chief Fintech Officer, MAS (quoted in Fintech Singapore coverage, 17 October 2025)

## Significance

- BLOOM establishes the operational architecture for regulated stablecoin settlement in wholesale markets, linking directly to MAS's regulatory framework for single-currency stablecoins.
- The agentic payments workstream positions MAS as one of the first regulators to pilot AI-automated payments within a supervised framework.
- The initiative aggregates over 16 global institutions, providing empirical data on cross-border tokenised settlement at scale.
- BLOOM's three-workstream structure provides a template other jurisdictions can adopt for deploying tokenised settlement assets.
