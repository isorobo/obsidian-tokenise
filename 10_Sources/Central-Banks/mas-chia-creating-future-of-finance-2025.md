# Creating the Future of Finance: A Journey of Innovation and Collaboration

## Citation

Chia Der Jiun. "Creating the Future of Finance: A Journey of Innovation and Collaboration". Remarks at the Singapore FinTech Festival 2025, 13 November 2025. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/creating-the-future-of-finance. Also reproduced at: https://www.bis.org/review/r251117e.htm.

## One-line summary

MAS Managing Director set out MAS's dual agenda of responsible AI adoption and a tokenised financial future, and announced forthcoming draft legislation on stablecoins and a regulatory guide on tokenising capital markets products.

## Key claims

- Asset-backed tokens have begun to scale but have not reached escape velocity; the primary constraints are standardisation, settlement assets, and institutional-grade networks.
- Unregulated stablecoins have a poor record of maintaining their peg; regulated stablecoins require sound reserve backing and reliable redemption rights.
- MAS will publish draft legislation establishing a stablecoin regulatory regime emphasising reserve quality and redemption reliability.
- MAS will publish a guide on tokenising capital markets products to provide regulatory clarity for issuers.
- In a tokenised environment, settlement assets will include CBDCs, tokenised bank liabilities, and regulated stablecoins, and each must meet institutional-grade standards.
- AI adoption in finance depends on four pillars: capabilities, adoption practices, governance of the AI lifecycle, and workforce skills.

## Excerpts

> "The tokenised future cannot be built by a single party and will require collaboration between private and public sector, within and across jurisdictions."
> ~ Chia Der Jiun, MAS Managing Director

## Significance

- The announcement of forthcoming draft stablecoin legislation signals MAS will move from its 2023 regulatory framework to primary legislation, increasing enforceability.
- The planned guide on tokenising capital markets products will reduce regulatory uncertainty for issuers, directly enabling Project Guardian to move from trials to commercial activity.
- The speech is the authoritative public statement of MAS's tokenisation policy as at November 2025 and frames the regulatory environment for all subsequent MAS actions in this area.
- Chia's framing of AI governance as a parallel track to tokenisation confirms MAS views agentic payments and AI-automated settlements as within scope for regulatory attention.
