# MAS Establishes Future of Finance Institute to Scale Financial Innovation

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore. "MAS Establishes Future of Finance Institute to Scale Financial Innovation". MAS Media Releases, 25 June 2026. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-establishes-future-of-finance-institute-to-scale-financial-innovation.

## One-line summary

MAS established the Future of Finance Institute (FFI) on 25 June 2026 to move AI and tokenisation from pilot to broad-based deployment across Singapore's financial sector.

## Key claims

- The FFI consolidates Singapore's AI and tokenisation infrastructure under one coordinating body, building on Project Guardian and Project Orchid.
- The FFI operates four capability pillars: a Knowledge Hub of validated use cases and deployment playbooks, an Innovation Garage for industry-wide collaboration, Industry Sandboxes for testing programmable money and tokenised assets, and Implementation Toolkits including a Programmable Compliance Toolkit for tokenised assets.
- The Industry Sandboxes provide controlled environments for pre-deployment validation of emerging technologies including programmable money, tokenised assets, and AI-enabled workflows.
- The Programmable Compliance Toolkit for tokenised assets is a named deliverable, signalling a shift from exploratory pilots to standardised deployment infrastructure.
- The FFI connects the financial and technology ecosystems to provide shared resources and expertise for adoption by financial institutions of all sizes.
- DPM and MAS Chairman Gan Kim Yong announced the FFI at the ABS 53rd Annual Dinner on 25 June 2026.

## Excerpts

> "FFI will initially focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Tokenisation, and help connect the financial and technology ecosystems and provide shared resources and expertise needed to promote broad-based adoption of these technologies."
> ~ MAS Media Release, 25 June 2026

> "AI will change jobs – some tasks will be automated, some roles will be redesigned, and new ones will emerge. The real measure of success of AI adoption is not just the productivity it creates, but whether workers can move with the technology as jobs change."
> ~ Gan Kim Yong, ABS Annual Dinner, 25 June 2026

> "The institute will act as a coordinating body for the next phase of Singapore's financial innovation efforts."
> ~ MAS Media Release, 25 June 2026

## Significance

- The FFI marks Singapore's transition from tokenisation experimentation (Project Guardian) to structured deployment infrastructure, making it the first central bank to publish a Programmable Compliance Toolkit for tokenised assets.
- Industry Sandboxes explicitly covering tokenised assets and programmable money provide a regulatory pathway for institutions moving from proof-of-concept to production.
- The FFI's integration of agentic AI and tokenisation under one roof reflects MAS's view that the two technologies converge at the point of automated, on-chain financial workflows.
- As a primary MAS press release, this source anchors the Singapore regulatory posture on tokenisation deployment in mid-2026.
