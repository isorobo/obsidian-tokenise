# Use of Tokenised Bank Liabilities for FX Settlement and Cross-Border Payments Under Project Guardian

## Citation

ISDA; Ant International; Monetary Authority of Singapore. "Use of Tokenised Bank Liabilities for FX Settlement and Cross-Border Payments Under Project Guardian". Project Guardian FX Workstream, 3 July 2025. https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/development/fintech/guardian/project-guardian-fx-workstream-transaction-banking.pdf.

## One-line summary

The Project Guardian FX workstream report demonstrates that tokenised bank liabilities on shared ledgers can deliver 24/7 real-time FX settlement, cutting cross-border transaction costs by an estimated 12.5% and saving over $50 billion industry-wide by 2030.

## Key claims

- Tokenised bank liabilities on a shared ledger enable 24/7 real-time FX settlement, reducing payment completion time from days to minutes or seconds.
- Universal industry adoption of this model could reduce cross-border transaction costs by 12.5%, potentially saving more than $50 billion by 2030.
- Ant International has processed over one third of its wholesale payments on-chain using tokenised deposits since 2019, providing production-grade evidence of feasibility.
- The report proposes FX data specifications, risk management frameworks, and FX documentation standards to support industry-wide adoption.
- Contributing institutions include ISDA, Ant International, BNY Mellon, HSBC, OCBC, and the Global Financial Markets Association's Global FX Division.
- The report is co-produced under MAS's Project Guardian and constitutes an industry standard-setting document for the FX workstream.

## Significance

- The $50 billion cost-saving estimate provides a quantified business case for tokenised FX settlement that regulators and banks can cite in policy decisions.
- Ant International's 2019 production data converts the proposal from theoretical to empirically grounded, providing proof of scale for wholesale tokenised payments.
- The report's FX data specifications and documentation standards constitute draft industry standards that ISDA will likely embed in its standard documentation, affecting all FX market participants.
- This is the first Project Guardian workstream output to address FX specifically, extending tokenisation from securities and funds into the largest financial market by daily volume.
