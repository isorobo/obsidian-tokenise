# Towards Achieving Trusted Open and Interoperable Networks

## Citation

Leong Sing Chiong. "Towards Achieving Trusted Open and Interoperable Networks". Welcome remarks at the Layer One Summit, Singapore FinTech Festival, 12 November 2025. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2025/towards-achieving-trusted-open-and-interoperable-networks.

## One-line summary

MAS's Deputy Managing Director argued that scaling digital assets requires trusted, open, and interoperable networks, and announced Project Guardian expansion, the BLOOM initiative, and GL1 as the three pillars delivering that infrastructure.

## Key claims

- Scaling digital assets depends on three infrastructure conditions: standardised tokens, a deep pool of reliable settlement assets, and institutional-grade interoperable networks.
- Project Guardian is expanding from bilateral trials to cross-border applications involving multiple asset classes and participants.
- Les Gardiennes, co-led by Banque de France and MAS with UBS and Societe Generale-FORGE, tests repo transactions using tokenised assets and digital money.
- BLOOM (launched October 2025) advances tokenised deposits and regulated stablecoins for wholesale settlement, involving over 16 global institutions.
- The SGD Testnet provides shared ledger infrastructure for live tokenised settlement trials, initially for a select group of wholesale participants.
- The Global Layer One (GL1) initiative, backed by MAS, Bank of England, Banque de France, and ECB, develops interoperable infrastructure and compliance toolkits to avoid proprietary silos.

## Excerpts

> "Technology has the potential to transform finance, but trust must underpin it."
> ~ Leong Sing Chiong, MAS Deputy Managing Director

> "Open runways, where innovation can take flight, and markets can truly connect."
> ~ Leong Sing Chiong, MAS Deputy Managing Director (on the GL1 vision)

## Significance

- The speech is the clearest public articulation by MAS of its three-layer digital asset architecture: tokens, settlement assets, and network infrastructure.
- GL1 names four major central banks as backers, signalling multilateral convergence on interoperability standards before domestic regimes fragment.
- Les Gardiennes demonstrates repo execution using tokenised assets between SG and euro-area institutions, the first live cross-border repo pilot in this category.
- The speech provides the policy framing for BLOOM and the Deutsche Bundesbank MoU signed the following day.
