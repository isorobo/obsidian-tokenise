# MAS and Deutsche Bundesbank Sign MoU on Tokenisation and Cross-Border Settlement

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore; Deutsche Bundesbank. "MAS and Deutsche Bundesbank Sign MoU on Tokenisation and Cross-Border Settlement". MAS Media Releases, 13 November 2025. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-and-deutsche-bundesbank-sign-mou-on-tokenisation-and-cross-border-settlement.

## One-line summary

MAS and Deutsche Bundesbank formalised bilateral cooperation to develop cross-border tokenised settlement solutions and common standards for interoperability between Singapore and Germany.

## Key claims

- The MoU commits both institutions to develop settlement solutions that reduce cost and processing time for cross-border transfers between Singapore and Germany.
- The agreement promotes common standards for cross-border payments, FX, and securities flows involving tokenised assets to enhance interoperability between digital asset platforms.
- The Deutsche Bundesbank joined the Project Guardian Policymaker Group in November 2024, making this MoU a deepening of an existing bilateral relationship.
- The MoU was signed by Leong Sing Chiong (MAS Deputy Managing Director, Markets and Development) and Burkhard Balz (Deutsche Bundesbank Executive Board member) at the Singapore FinTech Festival 2025.
- Alexandra Hachmeister (Deutsche Bundesbank Director General Digital Euro) co-signed alongside Balz, reflecting the Bundesbank's digital currency agenda.

## Excerpts

> "Through this new partnership with the Deutsche Bundesbank on digital asset settlement, we hope to enhance financial connectivity in ways that benefit individuals, corporates and financial market participants in both our economies."
> ~ Leong Sing Chiong, MAS Deputy Managing Director

> "The partnership with MAS reflects our shared commitment to advancing new financial infrastructures. Together, we aim to foster technological innovation and set new standards for efficiency and interoperability in international payments and securities transactions."
> ~ Burkhard Balz, Deutsche Bundesbank Executive Board member

## Significance

- The MoU creates a formal bilateral channel between Singapore and the euro area for harmonising tokenised asset standards, extending Project Guardian's reach into European regulatory infrastructure.
- Bundesbank's involvement as a Digital Euro architect links the BLOOM/Project Guardian settlement framework to potential future euro-denominated tokenised settlement.
- The MoU is evidence of jurisdictional competition and cooperation in establishing interoperability standards before a global default emerges.
- This is one of the first bilateral MoU arrangements specifically naming tokenised asset interoperability as a shared regulatory objective.
