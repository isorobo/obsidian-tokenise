# Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime (SAFR)

## Citation

Monetary Authority of Singapore. "MAS Partners Industry to Develop Safeguards for AI Agents in Finance" (Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime, Version 1.0). MAS Media Releases, 3 July 2026. https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-partners-industry-to-develop-safeguards-for-ai-agents-in-finance.

## One-line summary

MAS and industry partners published the SAFR white paper, a runtime governance framework that lets financial institutions deploy autonomous AI agents within defined policy and risk limits.

## Key claims

- SAFR sets out governance checkpoints that validate and log each proposed AI agent action before execution, addressing the fact that agents act faster than continuous human oversight allows.
- The framework rests on four pillars: policy-bound execution, real-time validation, auditability, and interoperability across institutions.
- SAFR builds on MAS's Project Mindforge AI Risk Management Toolkit, launched March 2026, moving from risk assessment to runtime operational controls.
- MAS developed SAFR under BuildFin.ai, its programme for responsible AI development and deployment in finance, with major financial institutions and FinTechs.
- Tested use cases include agent-assisted payments and treasury transactions, wealth management and advisory document review, and client engagement and content generation within approved boundaries.
- The framework mandates human intervention when AI agent decisions cross defined risk thresholds, preserving a human-in-the-loop control point for high-risk actions.

## Excerpts

> "Firms require real-time controls that keep agent behaviour inside the mandates, policies and risk limits they have defined."
> ~ MAS SAFR white paper, 3 July 2026 (as reported in secondary coverage)

> "Financial institutions need real-time safeguards to ensure that the behaviour of AI agents remains within predefined mandates, policies and risk boundaries."
> ~ MAS media release, 3 July 2026 (as reported in secondary coverage)

## Significance

- SAFR is MAS's first published operational control standard for agentic AI in finance, moving the regulator's agentic-AI work from consultation and toolkit to a deployable runtime framework.
- The framework's governance-checkpoint model gives the wiki a concrete primary-source definition of "agentic-AI trading" controls, distinct from generic AI-governance guidance.
- SAFR sits alongside the Future of Finance Institute's Industry Sandboxes and Programmable Compliance Toolkit, showing MAS treats AI agent governance and tokenised-asset deployment as parallel infrastructure tracks.
- The named use case of agent-assisted treasury and payment transactions links agentic-AI governance directly to the settlement-asset infrastructure (tokenised deposits, stablecoins, wholesale CBDC) that Project Guardian and BLOOM are building.
