# Africa Finance Corporation Issues CHF 350m Digital Bond via SIX/SDX

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Africa Finance Corporation Issues CHF 350m Digital Bond via SIX/SDX". Ledger Insights, 13 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/africa-finance-corporation-issues-chf-350m-digital-bond-via-six-sdx/.

## One-line summary

Africa Finance Corporation issued a CHF 350 million digital bond on SIX's SDX platform, the first digital bond from an African institution on the venue.

## Key claims

- Africa Finance Corporation issued a CHF 350 million ($431 million) digital bond through SIX's SDX platform.
- The bond carries a five-year maturity and a 1.4925% coupon.
- The issuance is the first digital bond by an African institution through a central securities depository and exchange.
- Commerzbank AG acted as technical lead arranger, with Deutsche Bank in a supporting role.
- The issuance ended a drought in digital bond activity at SIX, whose previous issuance was a KfW bond in June 2025.
- SDX merged with SIX's main securities services entity, SIX SIS, following regulatory approval in May 2026.
- SDX has previously hosted wholesale CBDC transactions, though this issuance did not involve central bank digital currency.

## Excerpts

> "The digital format of this bond is not an end in itself but a signal of our commitment to being at the frontier of innovation in the capital markets..."
> ~ Africa Finance Corporation leadership

## Significance

- Extends SIX SDX's digital bond issuer base beyond European sovereigns and supranationals to African development finance institutions.
- The Commerzbank and Deutsche Bank arrangement structure evidences continued German bank participation in Swiss digital securities infrastructure.
- Breaks a multi-year lull in SDX digital bond issuance, a useful data point for the corpus's assessment of tokenised-bond market momentum.
- The SDX and SIX SIS merger context links this issuance to broader European CSD consolidation around digital securities settlement.
