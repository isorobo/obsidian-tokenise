# Ant International uses HSBC tokenized deposits to sweep idle cash into tokenized BENJI MMF

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Ant International uses HSBC tokenized deposits to sweep idle cash into tokenized BENJI MMF". Ledger Insights, 21 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ant-international-uses-hsbc-tokenized-deposits-to-sweep-idle-cash-into-tokenized-benji-mmf/.

## One-line summary

Ant International used HSBC tokenised deposits, via its WhaleRTP platform, to move surplus treasury cash into Franklin Templeton's tokenised BENJI money market fund, in what HSBC called a first transaction.

## Key claims

- Ant International, HSBC, Franklin Templeton (BENJI) and WhaleRTP took part; WhaleRTP reportedly connects to more than 20 banks.
- The news surfaced through LinkedIn posts rather than a formal announcement.
- Conventional money market funds have early-afternoon dealing cut-offs, while around-the-clock subscription lets surplus cash earn fund yield until needed.
- It is the second such move in four months, after Ant began using digital share classes of an Amundi money market fund on Ethereum in June.
- HSBC's tokenised deposits run on a permissioned ledger, while Franklin's fund tokens run on public blockchains.
- HSBC called it a first transaction, so the article treats it as a pilot rather than a live product.

## Excerpts

> "The solution helps clients to optimise liquidity by investing surplus funds when available and accessing them when needed"
> ~ article body (Martijn Stoker, HSBC)

> ~ article body

## Significance

- Shows a tokenised deposit leg linked to a tokenised fund leg across a permissioned and public ledger.
- Follows the earlier Ant tokenised deposit partnerships note and the Amundi precedent.
- Raises the question of why a bank would help clients move cash out of its own deposits.
