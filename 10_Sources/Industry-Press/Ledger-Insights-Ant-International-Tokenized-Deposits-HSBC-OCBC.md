# Ant International extends tokenized deposit partnerships with HSBC, OCBC

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Ant International extends tokenized deposit partnerships with HSBC, OCBC". Ledger Insights, 7 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ant-international-extends-tokenized-deposit-partnerships-with-hsbc-ocbc/.

## One-line summary

Ant International is widening its tokenized deposit corridors with HSBC and OCBC on its WhaleRTP treasury platform, with a Middle East focus.

## Key claims

- Ant International is expanding tokenized deposit partnerships with HSBC and OCBC, both existing partners, to more payment corridors for its WhaleRTP treasury platform.
- WhaleRTP is supported by 20 banks worldwide and enables real-time transfers in more than 17 currencies.
- For HSBC's service the main new focus is the Middle East, with pilots moving tokenized dirhams within the UAE and cross-border US dollar transfers from the UAE to Hong Kong and Singapore.
- HSBC's service also supports EUR, GBP, HKD and SGD.
- Ant's Kelvin Li said the company aims to build a global treasury hub in the Middle East with regional bank partners.
- Only the free preview of the article was available.
