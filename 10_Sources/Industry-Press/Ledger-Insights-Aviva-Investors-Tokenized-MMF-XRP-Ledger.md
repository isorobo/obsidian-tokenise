# Aviva Investors Launches Its First Tokenized MMF Share Class on XRP Ledger

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Aviva Investors Launches Its First Tokenized MMF Share Class on XRP Ledger". Ledger Insights, 2 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/aviva-investors-launches-its-first-tokenized-mmf-share-class-on-xrp-ledger/.

## One-line summary

Aviva Investors launched a tokenised share class of its US Dollar Liquidity Fund on the XRP Ledger, approved by the Central Bank of Ireland.

## Key claims

- Aviva Investors launched its first tokenised money market fund share class for the Aviva Investors US Dollar Liquidity Fund.
- The tokens run on the XRP Ledger, following a partnership with Ripple agreed in February.
- The Central Bank of Ireland approved the tokens as part of a European UCITS fund structure.
- The underlying fund manages $1.23 billion in assets, with a £1 million minimum investment for tokenised shares.
- Each digital token mirrors a traditional share and cannot exist independently of it.
- The tokens are not transferable and can only be issued, minted or redeemed, with daily reconciliation against book-entry shares.
- Collateral mobility is the expected major future use case, though non-transferability currently limits that benefit.

## Significance

- Adds a UCITS-regulated European asset manager to the growing list of tokenised money market fund issuers on public blockchains.
- Shows a national regulator, the Central Bank of Ireland, approving a tokenised share class within existing UCITS fund rules rather than a bespoke regime.
- Highlights the gap between token issuance and full collateral mobility, since non-transferability limits near-term utility.
- Relevant to comparing regulatory pathways used by UK and EU asset managers entering tokenised fund markets.
