# BIS Reveals Scale of Project Agorá Tokenized Deposit Transactions Using Real Money

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BIS Reveals Scale of Project Agorá Tokenized Deposit Transactions Using Real Money". Ledger Insights, 30 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/project-agora-starts-live-trials-lloyds-participates-in-3-tokenized-deposit-transactions/.

## One-line summary

The BIS disclosed the first real-money results from Project Agorá, a 22-institution, 5-central-bank pilot testing tokenised wholesale cross-border payments across six currencies.

## Key claims

- Project Agorá processed real money for the first time, moving beyond earlier simulations.
- Twenty-two financial institutions and five central banks conducted 30 transactions worth about CHF 800,000.
- The pilot covered six currencies, including the Swiss franc, euro, sterling, yen, won and dollar.
- Participating central banks were the Bank of England, Bank of France, Bank of Japan, Bank of Korea and Swiss National Bank.
- The Federal Reserve and Bank of Canada did not take part in this phase.
- Lloyds Banking Group confirmed participation in three of the transactions.
- Average settlement time ran to about 80 seconds, despite the platform having no connection to central bank settlement systems.

## Significance

- Marks the first real-money evidence from a BIS-led multi-central-bank tokenisation pilot, moving cross-border settlement tokenisation past the simulation stage.
- Demonstrates settlement speed gains for wholesale cross-border payments without full integration into existing central bank rails.
- Positions tokenised deposits and wholesale CBDC as complementary instruments within a single settlement experiment spanning six currencies.
- Bears on the corpus's tracking of standard-setter-led wholesale settlement pilots ahead of any production decision.
