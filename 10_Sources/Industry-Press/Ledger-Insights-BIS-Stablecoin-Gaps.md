# BIS Identifies Stablecoin Gaps. Regulation and Innovations Are Already Closing Them

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BIS Identifies Stablecoin Gaps. Regulation and Innovations Are Already Closing Them". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-23. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bis-identifies-stablecoin-gaps-regulation-and-innovations-are-already-closing-them/.

## One-line summary

Ledger Insights argues that the BIS's updated stablecoin critique identifies five shortcomings that private-sector innovations and new legislation are already addressing.

## Key claims

- The BIS shifted from calling stablecoins "unsound money" to outlining five specific shortcomings: financial integrity, moneyness, governance on permissionless rails, and blockchain fragmentation.
- Frank Smets, Acting Head of the BIS Monetary and Economic Department, presented a two-pronged response: coordinated regulation and tokenised commercial and central bank money integration.
- Hazel Network's programmable compliance controls already address the financial-integrity concern the BIS identifies.
- The same network demonstrates deposit-to-stablecoin transitions that reduce blockchain fragmentation.
- The US GENIUS Act may enable elasticity mechanisms that Europe and the UK have restricted.
- The article concludes that constructive answers are already taking shape for many of the BIS-identified weaknesses.

## Significance

- The BIS's shift from blanket criticism to specific gap analysis signals a more constructive regulatory posture toward stablecoins.
- The five identified shortcomings define the analytical framework regulators will apply when evaluating stablecoin licence applications under the GENIUS Act and equivalent regimes.
- The article provides a real-time comparative view of regulatory and technological responses, useful for tracking the stablecoin regulatory timeline.
- Direct relevance to the vault's stablecoins and monetary-policy domains.
