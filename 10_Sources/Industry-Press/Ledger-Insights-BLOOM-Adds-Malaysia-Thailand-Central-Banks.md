# Mastercard, 2 central banks join Singapore cross border digital money project, BLOOM

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Mastercard, 2 central banks join Singapore cross border digital money project, BLOOM". Ledger Insights, 23 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/mastercard-2-central-banks-join-singapore-cross-border-digital-money-project-bloom/.

## One-line summary

MAS announced that Bank Negara Malaysia and the Bank of Thailand, plus nine more firms, joined BLOOM, its cross-border settlement initiative using tokenised deposits and regulated stablecoins.

## Key claims

- Bank Negara Malaysia and the Bank of Thailand joined BLOOM.
- Nine more industry participants joined, including Visa, Mastercard, Ripple, Maybank and the World Bank Group.
- BLOOM launched a year earlier and now counts 24 industry members, including Partior.
- It has three workstreams: acceptance and settlement across jurisdictions, interoperability between digital money arrangements, and programmable controls such as identity and sanctions screening.
- A cross-border payment may cross several tokenised deposit networks, stablecoins and card or fintech rails, and each handoff now needs a bilateral deal.
- The article is paywalled after the introduction, so this note covers the free portion only.
