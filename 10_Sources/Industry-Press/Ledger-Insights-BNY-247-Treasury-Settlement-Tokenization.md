# Analysis: BNY Targets 24/7 Treasury Settlement, with Tokenization as One Tool Among Several

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Analysis: BNY Targets 24/7 Treasury Settlement, with Tokenization as One Tool Among Several". Ledger Insights, 26 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/analysis-bny-targets-24-7-treasury-settlement-with-tokenization-as-one-tool-among-several/.

## One-line summary

BNY plans to pilot tokenised Treasuries on a private blockchain by end of 2026 and offer round-the-clock settlement for conventional and tokenised Treasuries in 2027.

## Key claims

- BNY plans to pilot tokenised Treasuries on its private blockchain by the end of 2026.
- BNY intends to offer 24/7 settlement for conventional and tokenised Treasuries from 2027.
- BNY earlier settled a Treasury transaction after Fedwire Securities Service closing hours, involving Ripple's RLUSD and Dreyfus acting for OpenEden's USDO stablecoin.
- Both RLUSD and USDO hold short-dated Treasuries as reserves with BNY as custodian.
- BNY is the dominant triparty agent for US repo, supporting $24.3 trillion in average daily clearance and $2.5 trillion in daily payments.
- The after-hours demonstration relied on BNY's custody hub position and settled in commercial bank money, not central bank money.
- The demonstration extended only to counterparties already holding custody accounts at BNY.

## Excerpts

> "BNY occupies a position no other institution matches. It is the dominant triparty agent for US repo, supporting $24.3 trillion in average daily clearance and processing $2.5 trillion in payments each day."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Shows a systemically important custodian treating tokenisation as one settlement tool among several, not a wholesale replacement for existing rails.
- Documents a concrete after-hours Treasury settlement using stablecoin reserve mechanics, linking tokenised Treasuries directly to stablecoin reserve composition.
- Bears on market-infrastructure analysis of how triparty repo dominance shapes which institutions can extend settlement hours first.
- Relevant to tracking the gap between commercial-bank-money settlement demonstrations and genuine 24/7 central-bank-money settlement.
