# BNY tokenizes a Treasury for repo. The question is why it needs to

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BNY tokenizes a Treasury for repo. The question is why it needs to". Ledger Insights, 14 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bny-tokenizes-a-treasury-for-repo-the-question-is-why-it-needs-to/.

## One-line summary

BNY reports the first use of tokenised collateral on a major triparty repo platform, while Ledger Insights questions whether a distributed ledger was needed.

## Key claims

- Citadel Securities bought a two-year Treasury note on Tradeweb and BNY recorded a tokenised representation on its private distributed ledger.
- The token served as collateral in an overnight triparty repo, with BNY as triparty agent and cash lender.
- BNY says the bond settled within minutes rather than on the standard T+1 cycle.
- BNY already runs intraday repo, launched in early 2025, and clears around $24 trillion a day.
- BNY has been the only major US triparty agent since JPMorgan exited in 2019.
- The article offers two BNY reasons for tokenising and suggests an unstated third; the analysis is paywalled.
