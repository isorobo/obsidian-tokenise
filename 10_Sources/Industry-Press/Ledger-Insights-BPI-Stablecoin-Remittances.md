# Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI) to Launch Stablecoin Remittances

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Bank of the Philippine Islands (BPI) to Launch Stablecoin Remittances". Ledger Insights, 29 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bank-of-the-philippine-islands-bpi-to-launch-stablecoin-remittances/.

## One-line summary

BPI partnered with stablecoin infrastructure firm Meridian to pilot stablecoin-based inbound remittances, coordinated with the Philippine central bank.

## Key claims

- BPI, the Philippines' second-largest bank, partnered with stablecoin infrastructure firm Meridian for inbound remittances.
- The pilot initially targets payroll credits for informal workers, such as freelancers and virtual assistants working remotely.
- Expansion to diaspora remittances is planned ahead of the ASEAN 49 Summit in November.
- The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, the Philippine central bank, is coordinating the initiative.
- The Philippines received $38.34 billion in remittances in 2024, ranking fourth globally, with 40% originating from the United States.
- The initiative aims to retain deposits within the traditional banking system by offering faster, cheaper remittance alternatives to informal stablecoin use.

## Excerpts

> "Filipinos move billions of pesos every year, and it is our responsibility to make sure that their money arrives faster, cheaper."
> ~ TG Limcaoco, President and CEO, BPI

## Significance

- Shows a systemically important national bank adopting stablecoin rails defensively, to prevent deposit flight to informal stablecoin remittance channels.
- Involves direct central bank coordination, distinguishing this from unsupervised stablecoin remittance activity already occurring among Filipino workers.
- Provides a concrete emerging-market test case for stablecoin remittance economics against a $38 billion annual remittance corridor.
- Relevant to tracking how central banks in large remittance-receiving economies respond to stablecoin adoption pressure.
