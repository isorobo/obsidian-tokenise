# Baillie Gifford Launches $BAGEY Tokenized Bond Fund on Ethereum, Solana

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Baillie Gifford Launches $BAGEY Tokenized Bond Fund on Ethereum, Solana". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-22. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/baillie-gifford-launches-bagey-tokenized-bond-fund-on-ethereum-solana/.

## One-line summary

Baillie Gifford launched $BAGEY, a natively issued short-duration corporate bond fund on Ethereum and Solana, making it the UK asset manager's first publicly available tokenised fund.

## Key claims

- Baillie Gifford, with $237 billion in assets under management, issued its first publicly available tokenised fund, $BAGEY (Baillie Gifford Enhanced Yield Fund).
- The fund is an actively managed short-duration corporate bond fund issued natively on both Ethereum and Solana blockchains.
- The firm chose digitally native issuance rather than wrapping an existing fund, making the blockchain the official ownership record.
- Theo Golden, head of digital assets and tokenisation, applied the criterion: "Does tokenisation improve the outcome for the client without weakening the proposition?"
- Native issuance means ownership transfers synchronise with token movement, eliminating the risk of ownership records falling out of sync with token trading.
- Wrapper tokens risk record discrepancy because tokens trade continuously while ownership updates under traditional structures occur only during business hours.

## Excerpts

> "Does tokenisation improve the outcome for the client without weakening the proposition? If it doesn't, we don't do it."
> ~ Theo Golden, head of digital assets and tokenisation, Baillie Gifford

## Significance

- A major UK asset manager applying a native-issuance approach rather than a wrapper structure sets a precedent for the tokenised-fund market.
- The dual-chain (Ethereum and Solana) strategy signals institutional acceptance of multiple public blockchain environments for securities issuance.
- The ownership-synchronisation rationale directly addresses a core legal risk in the tokenised securities corpus: the gap between on-chain token state and official register.
- Relevant to the vault's tokenised-fund and blockchain-settlement domains.
