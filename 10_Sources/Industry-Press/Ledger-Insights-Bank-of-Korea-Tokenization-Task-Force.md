# Bank of Korea Forms Tokenization Task Force as It Eyes Tokenized Government Bonds

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Bank of Korea Forms Tokenization Task Force as It Eyes Tokenized Government Bonds". Ledger Insights, 6 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/bank-of-korea-forms-tokenization-task-force-as-it-eyes-tokenized-government-bond/.

## One-line summary

The Bank of Korea has set up a dedicated tokenisation unit to explore issuing government bonds on its unified ledger platform.

## Key claims

- The Bank of Korea established an asset tokenisation unit inside its Digital Currency Office.
- The unit will explore tokenising Korean government bonds on the Digital Currency System, the unified ledger underlying Project Hangang.
- The same ledger already hosts Korea's wholesale CBDC and tokenised deposits.
- The Bank of Korea previously floated government bond tokenisation in a paper released at the ECB Forum.
- The initiative references BIS Project Pine, run with the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, on automated central bank operations.
- Simulations suggest tokenisation could automate margin calls, haircut calculations and repo execution.
- Hosting bonds and wholesale CBDC on one ledger is intended to support 24/7 central bank operations and faster crisis response.

## Significance

- Adds a fourth major central bank, alongside the Fed, ECB and BIS-linked programmes, exploring tokenised sovereign debt on a unified ledger.
- Links wholesale CBDC infrastructure directly to government bond tokenisation, a combination central to BIS unified-ledger theory.
- Evidences central banks drawing on BIS Project Pine research to automate collateral and repo operations.
- Extends the corpus's coverage of Asia-Pacific central bank tokenisation programmes beyond MAS and HKMA.
