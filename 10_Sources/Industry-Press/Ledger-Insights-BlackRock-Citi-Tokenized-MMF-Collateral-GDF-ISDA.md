# BlackRock, Citi, 46 Firms in Tokenized MMF Collateral Trials with GDF and ISDA

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BlackRock, Citi, 46 Firms in Tokenized MMF Collateral Trials with GDF and ISDA". Ledger Insights, 7 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/blackrock-citi-46-firms-in-tokenized-mmf-collateral-trials-with-gdf-and-isda/.

## One-line summary

A GDF and ISDA report backed by more than 300 participants concludes tokenised money market funds can serve as institutional collateral in the United States under all three main tokenisation models, subject to two regulatory gaps.

## Key claims

- Global Digital Finance and ISDA published a report concluding tokenised money market funds can work as institutional collateral in the US under all three main tokenisation models.
- The collateral mobility working group involved more than 300 participants from over 120 firms, including BlackRock, Citi, JP Morgan, Franklin Templeton, and derivatives clearing groups CME and ICE.
- Forty-eight firms took part in sandbox simulations run by Ownera to test the models operationally.
- The report identifies two regulatory gaps: money market funds cannot serve as variation margin for cleared derivatives because that collateral must be cash only, and the SEC has issued no guidance on tokenised securities for uncleared initial margin.
- The report follows the SEC's January statement that tokenisation does not change how securities laws apply, and SEC staff FAQs confirming a transfer agent may use a blockchain as the official shareholder record.

## Significance

- Provides the widest-scale industry validation to date that tokenised MMFs can enter mainstream institutional collateral workflows, directly bearing on the tokenised-money-market-fund instrument class.
- Names the residual regulatory gaps, cash-only variation margin and unresolved uncleared initial margin guidance, that constrain near-term adoption despite legal compatibility.
- Corroborates and extends prior SEC statements confirming blockchain-based shareholder records are legally valid, reinforcing the doctrinal basis for tokenised fund structures.
- Complements Fidelity and Invesco's parallel tokenised stablecoin reserve fund launches as evidence of an emerging tokenised MMF collateral ecosystem in 2026.
