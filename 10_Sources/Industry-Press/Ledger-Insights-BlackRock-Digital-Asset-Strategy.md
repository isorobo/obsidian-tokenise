# BlackRock Outlines Three Pronged Digital Asset Strategy

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BlackRock Outlines Three Pronged Digital Asset Strategy". Ledger Insights, 16 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/blackrock-outlines-three-prong-digital-asset-strategy/.

## One-line summary

BlackRock's CFO set a target of $500 million in annual digital asset revenue by 2030, built on crypto ETFs, tokenised fund products and a push to convert digital wallet holders into asset management clients.

## Key claims

- BlackRock CFO Martin Small announced a target of $500 million in annual digital asset revenue by 2030.
- BlackRock manages $110 billion in digital asset assets under management, around five per cent of the $2.3 trillion crypto market.
- The firm's digital asset strategy rests on its Bitcoin exchange-traded fund IBIT and Ethereum exchange-traded fund ETHA, alongside the BUIDL tokenised money market fund.
- BlackRock identifies existing digital wallet holders as a distinct growth channel, distinct from traditional brokerage clients, for tokenised investment products.
- Management intends to offer model portfolios, separately managed accounts and managed accounts in tokenised format to attract that wallet-holding user base.

## Excerpts

> "They're all potential new users of model portfolios, SMAs, and managed accounts in tokenized format. We want to build a digital wallet native asset manager."
> ~ Martin Small, BlackRock CFO

## Significance

- Signals that the world's largest asset manager treats tokenised fund distribution, not just crypto ETF custody, as a core revenue line through 2030.
- BUIDL's continued central role confirms tokenised money market funds remain BlackRock's primary tokenisation product, relevant to the tokenised-money-market-fund instrument class.
- The digital-wallet-native strategy signals a distribution channel shift, treating wallet holders as a distinct client segment rather than a subset of existing brokerage customers.
- Relevant alongside the DTCC's live tokenisation transactions and GDF/ISDA collateral trials as evidence that major US institutions are converging on tokenised funds as working collateral and distribution assets in 2026.
