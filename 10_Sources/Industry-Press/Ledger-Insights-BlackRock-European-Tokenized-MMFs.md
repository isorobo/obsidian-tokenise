# BlackRock Launches First European Tokenized MMFs

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "BlackRock Launches First European Tokenized MMFs". Ledger Insights, 4 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/blackrock-launches-first-european-tokenized-mmfs/.

## One-line summary

BlackRock launched digital share classes across six European UCITS money market funds using JP Morgan's Kinexys on public Ethereum.

## Key claims

- BlackRock issued new digital share classes across six existing UCITS funds in its Institutional Cash Series.
- The digital share classes cover three currencies: dollars, euros and sterling.
- JP Morgan's Kinexys tokenises the share classes on the public Ethereum network, a shift from earlier permissioned DLT partnerships.
- JP Morgan continues to act as transfer agent and maintains the official shareholder registry.
- The blockchain layer scans transfer activity around the clock, but minting occurs only during fund operating hours.
- The launch follows Amundi's June announcement of a similar tokenised arrangement with Ant International for corporate treasury use.
- The product targets liquidity access and collateral mobility for corporate treasurers.

## Significance

- Marks the largest asset manager's move to public-chain tokenisation for regulated European money market funds.
- Signals a shift among transfer agents from permissioned to public blockchain rails for institutional products.
- Extends the corpus's evidence base on tokenised money market funds as a maturing instrument class following Aviva and Schroders launches in the same week.
- Confirms JP Morgan's Kinexys as a recurring tokenisation and transfer-agency infrastructure provider across competing asset managers.
