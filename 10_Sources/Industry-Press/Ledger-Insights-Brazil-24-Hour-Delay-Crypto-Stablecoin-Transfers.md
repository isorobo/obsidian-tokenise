# Brazil Finalizes 24 Hour Delay for Crypto, Stablecoin Transfers to Combat Fraud

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Brazil Finalizes 24 Hour Delay for Crypto, Stablecoin Transfers to Combat Fraud". Ledger Insights, 10 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/brazil-finalizes-24-hour-delay-for-crypto-stablecoin-transfers-to-combat-fraud/.

## One-line summary

Brazil's central bank finalised a rule forcing Virtual Asset Service Providers to hold large offshore-bound crypto and stablecoin transfers for up to 24 hours.

## Key claims

- Banco Central do Brasil finalised a rule requiring Virtual Asset Service Providers to hold certain crypto and stablecoin transactions for up to 24 hours.
- The hold applies where a customer's transactions reach $10,000 or more, in a single transfer or daily aggregate.
- It applies only where the destination is an offshore virtual asset entity or a self-hosted wallet.
- Virtual Asset Service Providers may flag further transactions for holds based on their own internal risk assessments.
- The rule takes effect on 1 January 2027 and requires providers to notify customers of holds and keep fraud-incident records.
- The central bank cited stablecoins' use in rapidly moving fraudulently obtained funds offshore as the rule's rationale.
- Industry body ABcripto objected during consultation, but the final rule closely tracks the late-July 2026 proposal.
