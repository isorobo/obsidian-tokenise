# Brazil launches simulated tokenization pilots for capital markets

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Brazil launches simulated tokenization pilots for capital markets". Ledger Insights, 16 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/brazil-launches-simulated-tokenization-pilots-for-capital-markets/.

## One-line summary

Brazil's securities regulator, the CVM, published rules for a DLT "Pilot Program" that bars real transactions, assets and investors and runs simulated tests for 60 days.

## Key claims

- The CVM published rules for pilot tests of capital market activity on distributed ledger technology.
- Despite the name, the rules bar real transactions, assets and investors.
- Applications are due about 15 business days after publication, roughly 5 November.
- Testing starts the day after the deadline, lasts 60 days and can be extended by 30.
- Test networks must be proposed by associations or self-regulatory organisations in the CVM's Tokenization Working Group; ANBIMA launched a DLT pilot last year and Ancord and Abrasca are expected to host tests.
- Participants must generally be CVM-regulated entities, though the CVM may allow exceptions, possibly admitting technology providers and foreign firms.
