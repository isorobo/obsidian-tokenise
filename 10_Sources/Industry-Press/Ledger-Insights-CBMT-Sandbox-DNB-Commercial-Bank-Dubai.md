# DNB, Commercial Bank of Dubai join CBMT sandbox for tokenized deposits

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "DNB, Commercial Bank of Dubai join CBMT sandbox for tokenized deposits". Ledger Insights, 7 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dnb-commercial-bank-of-dubai-join-cbmt-sandbox-for-tokenized-deposits/.

## One-line summary

Norway's DNB and the Commercial Bank of Dubai have joined the Commercial Bank Money Token sandbox, taking it to eight banks and beyond its German-led origins.

## Key claims

- DNB and the Commercial Bank of Dubai recently joined the CBMT sandbox, raising participants to eight banks.
- The German Banking Industry Committee founded CBMT; Commerzbank, DZ Bank, Helaba and UniCredit were the founding four banks.
- BNP Paribas and ABN AMRO joined about a month earlier, expanding the initiative beyond Germany.
- DNB's entry adds the Norwegian krone to the euro, US dollar and Japanese yen already in the sandbox.
- DNB plans to test how tokenized krone deposits move between parties, are exchanged for other currencies and settle securities.
