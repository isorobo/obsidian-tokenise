# CFTC clarifies futures brokers can invest customer funds in tokenized MMFs, Treasuries

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "CFTC clarifies futures brokers can invest customer funds in tokenized MMFs, Treasuries". Ledger Insights, 25 September 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/cftc-clarifies-futures-brokers-can-invest-customer-funds-in-tokenized-mmfs-treasuries/.

## One-line summary

The CFTC updated its FAQ so that futures commission merchants may hold customer funds in certain tokenized money market funds and Treasuries, while stablecoins remain excluded.

## Key claims

- The CFTC updated its FAQ on tokenized collateral and its digital asset margin collateral framework.
- Client funds of futures commission merchants and clearing houses still cannot be invested in stablecoins.
- Certain tokenized money market funds and Treasuries are now acceptable.
- Qualifying tokens must be held at a permitted depository, which may include recently authorized OCC national trust companies.
- For tokenized government MMFs, the holder must provide an acknowledgment letter confirming the assets are segregated client assets free of liens.
- A token must carry the same legal rights as the conventional asset it represents.
- Uncleared swaps still cannot use crypto or stablecoins as margin, but tokenized versions of eligible assets qualify, and amended margin rules now accept MMFs that use repo and securities lending.

## Significance

- It extends the earlier CFTC margin-collateral change into customer-fund investment, a separate regulatory channel.
- The same-legal-rights and segregation conditions show how custody and property-style protections attach to tokenized collateral.
- The exclusion of stablecoins while admitting tokenized MMFs marks a clear regulatory line between token types.
- The item is trade-press coverage; the CFTC FAQ itself was not read.
