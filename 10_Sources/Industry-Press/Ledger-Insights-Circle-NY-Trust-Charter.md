# Stablecoin Issuer Circle Receives New York Trust Charter. How Does It Fit With OCC Charter?

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Stablecoin Issuer Circle Receives New York Trust Charter. How Does It Fit With OCC Charter?". Ledger Insights, 2 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/stablecoin-issuer-circle-receives-new-york-trust-charter-how-does-it-fit-with-occ-charter/.

## One-line summary

Circle has added a New York state trust charter alongside its federal OCC charter, splitting stablecoin issuance from reserve management and custody.

## Key claims

- Circle received a trust charter from the New York Department of Financial Services.
- Circle already holds final OCC approval for First National Digital Currency Bank, operating as Circle National Trust.
- Under the dual structure, the New York trust remains the USDC issuer while the national trust manages reserves and provides custody.
- Ripple follows a comparable dual-charter model for RLUSD, but with broader OCC custody powers extending to non-affiliated institutions.
- Paxos is taking a different path, converting its NYDFS trust into a single national trust rather than operating two charters.
- Circle's reserve-management activity under the national trust is described as a future capability rather than an immediate feature.

## Significance

- Shows the largest US stablecoin issuers converging on differing dual-charter and single-charter structures under the GENIUS Act era.
- Clarifies how state trust licensing and federal OCC charters combine to separate issuance from custody and reserve management.
- Provides a direct comparator to Sony Bank and Circle's earlier OCC approvals already indexed in this channel.
- Relevant to tracking how US stablecoin issuers structure legal entities to manage custody and reserve risk separately from issuance risk.
