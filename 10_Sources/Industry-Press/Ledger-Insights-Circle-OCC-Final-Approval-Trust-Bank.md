# Circle Receives Final OCC Approval for National Trust Bank

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Circle Receives Final OCC Approval for National Trust Bank". Ledger Insights, 11 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/circle-receives-final-occ-approval-for-national-trust-bank/.

## One-line summary

Circle has converted its conditional OCC charter into a full national trust bank licence, becoming the first of five stablecoin firms to reach that milestone while moving USDC issuance to a separate New York entity.

## Key claims

- The OCC granted Circle final approval for First National Digital Currency Bank, operating as Circle National Trust, converting a conditional charter granted in December 2025 into a full national charter.
- Circle becomes the first of five OCC applicant firms to reach final approval, ahead of Coinbase, which received preliminary approval in April, and Ripple, Paxos, BitGo and Fidelity Digital Assets, which remain at conditional stage.
- At launch, the national trust bank will provide digital asset custody solely for Circle and its affiliates, with plans to later serve institutional customers such as banks and regulated derivatives organisations.
- Circle is building a two-part corporate structure in which a state-regulated entity issues USDC and the federal trust bank safeguards the reserve assets behind it.
- Circle has applied to the New York Department of Financial Services for a limited-purpose trust company charter, to which it plans to move USDC issuance once established.

## Significance

- Confirms the OCC national trust bank charter as the leading federal pathway for GENIUS Act-compliant stablecoin issuers, ahead of state-only licensing routes.
- Establishes a template of split issuance and custody entities, separating state-chartered stablecoin issuance from federally chartered reserve safekeeping, bearing on reserve-requirements doctrine.
- Positions Circle as the first mover among five competing OCC applicants, a benchmark for tracking the pace of US stablecoin bank licensing through 2026 and 2027.
- Relevant alongside the GENIUS Act and Sony Bank's parallel OCC application as evidence of a maturing federal stablecoin bank licensing regime.
