# Clearstream extends tokenization beyond issuance to settlement, custody and collateral

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Clearstream extends tokenization beyond issuance to settlement, custody and collateral". Ledger Insights, 20 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/clearstream-extends-tokenization-beyond-issuance-to-settlement-custody-and-collateral/.

## One-line summary

Clearstream plans to let clients tokenize parts of the assets it custodies on permissioned Besu infrastructure, starting with fixed income and tokenized money market funds.

## Key claims

- Clearstream, a Deutsche Boerse Group subsidiary, plans to let clients tokenize parts of the EUR 22 trillion in assets it custodies, with first issuances expected later this year.
- It announced its next-generation digital securities infrastructure in June, consolidating earlier distributed ledger activities.
- Tokenized securities will carry full ownership and voting rights, and Clearstream will work only with institutions in a B2B2C model.
- It uses permissioned DLTs and has chosen Besu, citing compatibility with other financial market infrastructures and a possible permissionless future.
- It is initially avoiding equities because of their many corporate actions, starting with fixed income, tokenized money market funds and retail structured products.
- The DTCC, by contrast, is starting with its 1,000 largest equities, Treasuries and certain ETFs.
