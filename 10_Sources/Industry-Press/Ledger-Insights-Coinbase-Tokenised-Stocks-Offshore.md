# Coinbase to Launch Tokenized Stocks Offshore

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Coinbase to Launch Tokenized Stocks Offshore". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-16. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/coinbase-to-launch-tokenized-stocks-offshore/.

## One-line summary

Coinbase announced 24/7 offshore tokenised stock trading with full ownership and dividend rights, targeting non-US markets while domestic regulations remain incomplete.

## Key claims

- Coinbase will launch tokenised stock trading offshore on the Base blockchain, offering 24/7 trading with full ownership and dividend rights.
- The offering targets markets outside the United States while domestic tokenised-equity regulations remain unresolved.
- Coinbase holds MiFID II licensing through Coinbase Financial Services Europe, enabling EU distribution.
- The DTC received a no-action letter in December 2025 permitting tokenisation of custodied assets including Russell 1000 stocks, major index ETFs, and US Treasuries.
- Binance launched bStocks on the same day via Alpaca (US broker), offering full ownership rights on its UAE-licensed broker infrastructure.
- bStocks are not available in the EU at launch.

## Significance

- The simultaneous launches by Coinbase and Binance signal coordinated market entry into tokenised equities at global scale.
- The offshore-first strategy reflects the gap between US regulatory intent and operative frameworks for tokenised securities.
- MiFID II licensing as the EU distribution vehicle confirms that existing securities-law frameworks, not new crypto-specific regimes, govern the first wave of tokenised equity products in Europe.
