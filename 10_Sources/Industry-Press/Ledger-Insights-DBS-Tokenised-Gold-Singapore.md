# DBS to Launch Tokenized Physical Gold for Singapore Retail Investors

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "DBS to Launch Tokenized Physical Gold for Singapore Retail Investors". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-11. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dbs-to-launch-tokenized-physical-gold-for-singapore-retail-investors/.

## One-line summary

DBS will offer Singapore's first tokenised physical gold product to retail investors via its digibank app in H2 2026, with each token backed by one gram of gold held in a Singapore vault.

## Key claims

- DBS will offer tokenised physical gold to retail customers via the DBS digibank app in H2 2026, claiming to be Singapore's first such offering.
- Each token is backed by one gram of physical gold, valued at approximately SGD 200 per token, stored in a dedicated Singapore vault.
- The product features 24/7 trading and near-instant settlement via atomic settlement.
- DBS handles all operations in-house, including custody and vault management.
- DBS is exploring listing the token on its DBS Digital Exchange (DDEx) for accredited and institutional investors.
- Gold reached an all-time high of USD 5,600 per ounce earlier in 2026, providing the demand context.

## Significance

- The retail-accessible tokenised commodity product from a major commercial bank marks a significant extension of tokenisation beyond institutional and accredited-investor boundaries.
- Atomic settlement of a physical-commodity-backed token provides a concrete real-world example of the settlement model the vault's blockchain-settlement domain analyses.
- The Singapore jurisdiction and MAS-regulated context aligns with Project Guardian's broader tokenisation-of-real-assets programme.
- Relevant to the vault's tokenised-commodity instrument class and Singapore's role as a regulatory laboratory for digital assets.
