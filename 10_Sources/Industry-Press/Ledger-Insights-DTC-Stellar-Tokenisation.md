# DTC Tokenization Service to Add Stellar as Second Public Blockchain

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "DTC Tokenization Service to Add Stellar as Second Public Blockchain". Ledger Insights, 2026-05-27. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dtc-tokenization-service-to-add-stellar-as-second-public-blockchain/.

## Key claims

- The DTCC and Stellar Development Foundation plan to enable tokenised assets on the Stellar network, with availability expected in H1 2027.
- Stellar becomes the second public blockchain supported after Canton Network; neither uses Ethereum-compatible infrastructure.
- Stellar handles tokens as native base-layer primitives rather than as smart contracts, unlike standard Ethereum approaches.
- A December 2025 SEC no-action letter permitted DTC to tokenise custodied assets including Russell 1000 stocks, major index ETFs, and US Treasuries.
- Limited live transactions are planned for July 2026, with full launch in October 2026.
- The no-action letter mandates all supported chains restrict token movements to registered wallets and allow DTC to force-transfer or burn tokens to address errors, lost tokens, or malfeasance.

## One-line summary

The DTCC will add Stellar as a second public blockchain for its tokenisation service in H1 2027, following a December 2025 SEC no-action letter that authorised tokenisation of Russell 1000 stocks, major ETFs, and US Treasuries.

## Significance

- The SEC no-action letter establishing DTCC's authority to tokenise custodied US securities is a foundational regulatory instrument for US tokenised-equity market infrastructure.
- The mandatory force-transfer and burn rights imposed by the no-action letter define the custody and control doctrine applicable to tokenised securities on public chains.
- The choice of non-EVM chains (Canton, Stellar) by DTCC challenges the assumption that Ethereum is the default institutional settlement layer.
