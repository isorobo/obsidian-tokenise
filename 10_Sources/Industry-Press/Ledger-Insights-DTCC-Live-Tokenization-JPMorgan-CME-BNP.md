# DTCC Processes First Live Tokenization Transactions with JP Morgan, CME, BNP Paribas

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "DTCC Processes First Live Tokenization Transactions with JP Morgan, CME, BNP Paribas". Ledger Insights, 15 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/dtcc-processes-first-live-tokenization-transactions-with-jp-morgan-cme-bnp-paribas/.

## One-line summary

The DTCC ran its first live tokenised-securities transactions with JP Morgan, CME, BNP Paribas, Societe Generale and Citadel Securities, ahead of a full October 2026 rollout following an SEC no-action letter.

## Key claims

- The DTCC launched the first live phase of its tokenisation service, with JP Morgan converting Invesco's QQQ exchange-traded fund into tokenised form as a test transaction.
- The SEC issued a no-action letter in December 2025 permitting tokenisation of eligible securities, clearing the regulatory path for the DTCC pilot.
- Participants in the live phase include JP Morgan, CME, BNP Paribas, Societe Generale and Citadel Securities, testing tokenised stocks, exchange-traded funds and US Treasuries.
- Demonstrated use cases include posting tokenised securities as margin, using tokenised Treasuries as collateral, and executing securities lending transactions.
- Real-time transfers between allow-listed wallets occur directly between parties without routing through the DTCC's conventional T+1 settlement infrastructure, correcting a common misconception about the service.
- The DTCC plans a full rollout of the tokenisation service in October 2026.

## Excerpts

> "Real time direct transfers are enabled between parties without involving the DTCC directly, although all wallets that touch the tokens must be included in the allow list."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Marks the first live use of DTCC tokenisation infrastructure by major US market participants, moving the service from pilot design into production testing ahead of an October 2026 rollout.
- Demonstrates tokenised Treasuries and tokenised equities functioning as working collateral and margin instruments inside core US market infrastructure, not merely as settlement experiments.
- Clarifies that DTCC-tokenised securities transfer directly between allow-listed wallets, a transfer-doctrine departure from the DTCC's conventional T+1 book-entry settlement model.
- Relevant alongside the GDF/ISDA tokenised money market fund collateral trials as parallel evidence that 2026 US market infrastructure is normalising tokenised instruments as collateral.
