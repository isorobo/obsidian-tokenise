# EU banking regulator classifies USDC as significant stablecoin

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "EU banking regulator classifies USDC as significant stablecoin". Ledger Insights, 15 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/eu-banking-regulator-classifies-usdc-as-significant-stablecoin/.

## One-line summary

The European Banking Authority classified Circle's USDC as the first significant e-money token under MiCA, raising reserve and capital requirements and adding EBA supervision alongside the French ACPR.

## Key claims

- The EBA classified USDC as the first significant electronic money token (EMT) under MiCA.
- Circle's French subsidiary will be supervised by both the ACPR and the EBA.
- The EBA takes responsibility for own funds, reserve assets, liquidity requirements, and recovery and redemption plans.
- Significant EMTs must hold 60% of reserves at commercial banks instead of 30%, and the capital requirement rises from 2% to 3% of reserves.
- The EBA did not say which of the seven criteria USDC met; any three trigger the classification.
- The European System of Central Banks has proposed replacing the bank deposit requirement with daily and weekly liquidity buckets, as the Commission consults on the MiCA review.
