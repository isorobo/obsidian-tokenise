# ECB Targets 21 September Go Live for Project Pontes

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "ECB Targets 21 September Go Live for Project Pontes". Ledger Insights, 4 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ecb-targets-21-september-go-live-for-project-pontes-a-primer-on-tokenized-central-bank-money/.

## One-line summary

The ECB set 21 September 2026 as the production go-live date for Project Pontes, its settlement platform for DLT transactions in central bank money.

## Key claims

- Project Pontes lets institutions settle DLT-based transactions using tokenised central bank money.
- The platform offers two settlement models: cash tokens on the Eurosystem's DLT, and a trigger model that initiates conventional TARGET payments from DLT instructions.
- Both models keep settlement in central bank money rather than commercial bank money or stablecoins.
- Testing began in early August 2026.
- The registration deadline for the initial go-live was 7 August 2026, with later participation still available.
- Institutions must demonstrate adequate testing to their national central bank before accessing the production environment.
- Production go-live is targeted for 21 September 2026.

## Excerpts

> "The European Central Bank is launching Project Pontes, targeting a production go-live date of 21 September 2026."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Sets a concrete, dated production milestone for Eurosystem wholesale DLT settlement, moving the project from pilot to live infrastructure.
- Preserves central bank money as the settlement asset, distinguishing the Eurosystem approach from commercial tokenised deposit or stablecoin models.
- Offers two coexisting settlement models, cash tokens and trigger payments, that the corpus can compare against BIS unified-ledger designs.
- Gives the wiki a fixed date against which to track European wholesale CBDC settlement readiness.
