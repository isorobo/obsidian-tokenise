# EU Council wants only significant crypto firms supervised by ESMA

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "EU Council wants only significant crypto firms supervised by ESMA". Ledger Insights, 13 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/eu-council-wants-only-significant-crypto-firms-supervised-by-esma/.

## One-line summary

EU finance ministers narrowed the Commission's proposal for direct ESMA supervision of all MiCA crypto-asset service providers to cover only "significant" providers, over the Commission's objection.

## Key claims

- The Commission's December regulatory package proposed that all MiCA-regulated CASPs be supervised directly by ESMA instead of national authorities.
- The Council of the EU agreed the main elements of its negotiating position and limited ESMA supervision to "significant" CASPs.
- Under MiCA today a significant CASP has at least 15 million EU users; the ministers kept that threshold but added alternative criteria that effectively lower the bar.
- A CASP with just over 1 million users could qualify if it also meets at least two other tests, such as assets in custody or trading volume.
- The change sits in the market integration and supervision package (MISP), which is separate from the Commission's review of MiCA ("MiCA 2"), for which a consultation closed in August.
- Commissioner Maria Luis Albuquerque criticised the compromise. Ledger Insights read only the free portion of the article; the remainder is paywalled.
