# FAB issues digital bond via Euroclear

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "FAB issues digital bond via Euroclear". Ledger Insights, 16 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/fab-issues-digital-bond-via-euroclear/.

## One-line summary

First Abu Dhabi Bank issued an AED 1 billion digital green bond on Euroclear's D-FMI platform, its second digital bond and at least the fourth by a MENAT bank on D-FMI in a year.

## Key claims

- FAB issued an AED 1 billion (about $272 million) three-year digital green bond with a 5.75% coupon.
- The notes are listed on the Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange.
- It is FAB's second digital bond; the first, about $100 million, was issued in July 2025 on HSBC's Orion platform.
- Ledger Insights counts at least four D-FMI digital bonds by MENAT banks in the past year and three D-FMI issuances in roughly two months.
- Group CFO Lars Kramer framed the deal as a step toward sustainable finance through automation and efficiency.
- The article is partly paywalled; this note rests on the visible portion.
