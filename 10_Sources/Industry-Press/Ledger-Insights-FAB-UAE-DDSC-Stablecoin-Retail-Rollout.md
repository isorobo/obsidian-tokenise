# FAB Backed UAE Stablecoin DDSC Cleared for Retail Rollout via Crypto Exchanges

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "FAB Backed UAE Stablecoin DDSC Cleared for Retail Rollout via Crypto Exchanges". Ledger Insights, 6 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/fab-backed-uae-stablecoin-ddsc-cleared-for-retail-rollout-via-crypto-exchanges/.

## One-line summary

The UAE dirham stablecoin DDSC has received central bank clearance to list on VARA-regulated exchanges, extending it from institutional settlement into retail and merchant payments.

## Key claims

- DDSC, the UAE dirham stablecoin backed by IHC, First Abu Dhabi Bank and Sirius International Holding, received a No Objection Certificate from the Central Bank of the UAE.
- The NOC allows DDSC distribution via selected exchanges regulated by Dubai's Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority, though no exchanges were named and listings remain subject to meeting the NOC's conditions.
- The approval bridges two UAE regulatory regimes: dirham-denominated payment tokens fall under the central bank's Payment Token Services Regulation, while Dubai crypto exchanges answer to VARA.
- DDSC launched in February 2026 on ADI Chain, a Layer 2 blockchain developed by the ADI Foundation, an IHC subsidiary founded by Sirius.
- Sovereign investor ADQ was named as a third initial partner alongside FAB and IHC when the initiative was announced in April 2025, but has not featured in announcements since launch.

## Significance

- Documents a rare example of a central-bank-licensed dirham stablecoin receiving explicit clearance for retail distribution, evidencing the UAE's dual payment-token and exchange regulatory architecture.
- Confirms the CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation and VARA exchange licensing as two distinct but interlocking approval gates for stablecoin retail rollout.
- Provides a Gulf-region comparator to US GENIUS Act and EU MiCA retail stablecoin distribution regimes, widening the wiki's jurisdictional coverage.
- Signals bank-backed stablecoin issuance, since FAB is a major UAE bank, expanding beyond institutional settlement into consumer payments.
