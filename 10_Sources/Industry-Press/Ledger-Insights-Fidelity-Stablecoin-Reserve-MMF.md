# Fidelity Launches Stablecoin Reserve MMF

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Fidelity Launches Stablecoin Reserve MMF". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-18. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/fidelity-launches-stablecoin-reserve-mmf/.

## One-line summary

Fidelity launched the Fidelity Reserves Digital Fund, a money market fund targeting stablecoin issuers as reserve asset holders, joining at least eight other major institutions in a rapidly crowded market.

## Key claims

- Fidelity launched the Fidelity Reserves Digital Fund, a money market fund designed for stablecoin issuers to hold as reserve assets.
- The fund is structured as a conventional fund compliant with the GENIUS Act, though it may issue a blockchain share class in future.
- The GENIUS Act limits stablecoin reserve investments in Treasuries to maturities of 93 days or less, plus cash and repo.
- At least eight major institutions have launched or adapted similar funds: BNY, Goldman Sachs, Federated Hermes, Morgan Stanley, JP Morgan, BlackRock, and State Street.
- Fee competition is severe, with nearly all funds charging between 0.17% and 0.18% after fee waivers due to the narrow allowable asset universe.
- The compressed asset scope and near-identical fee structures limit differentiation across competing reserve fund offerings.

## Significance

- The GENIUS Act's reserve-asset constraints directly shape the product design of stablecoin-reserve money market funds, creating a regulatory-driven commoditisation effect.
- The crowded market for stablecoin-reserve MMFs signals that institutional infrastructure for the GENIUS Act stablecoin framework is now being built at pace.
- The 93-day maturity cap is a key doctrinal limit for reserve-requirements analysis.
