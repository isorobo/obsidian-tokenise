# Hong Kong SFC Approves Baillie Gifford Native Tokenized Fund

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Hong Kong SFC Approves Baillie Gifford Native Tokenized Fund". Ledger Insights, 15 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hong-kong-sfc-approves-baillie-gifford-native-tokenized-fund/.

## One-line summary

Hong Kong's Securities and Futures Commission approved distribution of Baillie Gifford's digitally native tokenised bond fund BAGEY to professional investors, treating the on-chain token itself as the legal record of ownership.

## Key claims

- The Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission approved the Baillie Gifford Enhanced Yield Fund, BAGEY, for distribution to professional investors in Hong Kong.
- BAGEY is an actively managed portfolio of short-duration government and corporate bonds issued directly on the Ethereum and Solana public blockchains, developed in partnership with BNY.
- Unlike most tokenised funds, which wrap conventional custodial holdings in a token, BAGEY is digitally native: the token constitutes the investor's holding and the blockchain is the official ownership record.
- The fund is structured as a UK-regulated open-ended investment company denominated in US dollars, meaning the SFC approval sits alongside UK fund regulation rather than replacing it.
- Investors can subscribe or redeem using fiat currency or USDC stablecoins, with same-day redemptions available up to ten per cent of net asset value.

## Significance

- Provides a live doctrinal example of on-chain instruments as the primary legal record of ownership rather than a derivative wrapper, bearing directly on the property-category and control doctrines.
- Confirms Hong Kong's SFC as an active approver of digitally native fund structures, extending Hong Kong's tokenisation regulatory posture beyond bonds into fund distribution.
- USDC-denominated subscription and redemption ties a mainstream stablecoin directly into regulated fund settlement mechanics.
- Relevant alongside the original BAGEY launch on Ethereum and Solana as the regulatory follow-on that converts a technical launch into a distributable regulated product.
