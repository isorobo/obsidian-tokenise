# HSBC First for Digital Securities Sandbox Green Light, Integration with LSEG for Digital Gilt

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "HSBC First for Digital Securities Sandbox Green Light, Integration with LSEG for Digital Gilt". Ledger Insights, 17 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hsbc-first-for-digital-securities-sandbox-green-light-integration-with-lseg-for-digital-gilt/.

## One-line summary

The Bank of England approved HSBC's Orion platform as the first Digital Securities Depository under the UK Digital Securities Sandbox, and HSBC signed a memorandum of understanding with LSEG to link infrastructure for the UK's forthcoming digital gilt.

## Key claims

- The Bank of England granted HSBC's Orion platform Gate 2 approval on 13 July 2026, making it the first firm authorised to operate as a Digital Securities Depository under the Digital Securities Sandbox.
- The approval permits Orion to handle digitally native bond issuance, servicing and settlement rather than a tokenised wrapper around conventional holdings.
- HSBC and the London Stock Exchange Group signed a memorandum of understanding to build a bilateral Digital Securities Depository link, with LSEG's infrastructure serving investor settlement and Orion serving as issuer depository.
- The UK's digital government bond, DIGIT, is scheduled to launch by the first quarter of 2027, with HSBC providing the issuance platform.
- HSBC has already issued more than $5 billion in digitally native bonds on Orion, making it the operator of the world's largest live digital bond platform.

## Excerpts

> "enabling investors to hold DIGIT through either infrastructure"
> ~ Ledger Insights article body, on the HSBC-LSEG memorandum of understanding

## Significance

- Confirms the Bank of England's Digital Securities Sandbox as an operative regulatory gateway, not merely a proposed framework, with a live Gate 2 approval.
- Establishes the market infrastructure link, HSBC Orion as issuer depository and LSEG as investor-side settlement, that DIGIT will run on ahead of its 2027 launch.
- Positions HSBC as the incumbent infrastructure provider for UK sovereign digital debt, extending its lead from corporate digital bond issuance into government issuance.
- Relevant alongside the UK-US Transatlantic Taskforce statement on tokenised assets as evidence that UK digital-gilt infrastructure and stablecoin policy are advancing on parallel tracks in mid-2026.
