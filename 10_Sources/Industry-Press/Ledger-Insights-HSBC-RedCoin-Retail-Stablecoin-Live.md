# HSBC goes live with RedCoin stablecoin

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "HSBC goes live with RedCoin stablecoin". Ledger Insights, 23 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/hsbc-goes-live-with-redcoin-stablecoin/.

## One-line summary

HSBC launched RedCoin (RCHKD), a Hong Kong dollar stablecoin licensed under the Stablecoins Ordinance, through its PayMe app as the first bank-issued retail stablecoin in Hong Kong.

## Key claims

- RedCoin (RCHKD) is licensed under Hong Kong's Stablecoins Ordinance and regulated by the HKMA.
- At launch, PayMe's 3.3 million users can send stablecoins to each other and pay at more than 100,000 merchants.
- HSBC plans to let customers subscribe to tokenised investment products using RedCoin, starting with a share class of its HKD money market fund.
- A closed-loop model relies on existing PayMe bank KYC, which largely covers Hong Kong's requirement to identify every stablecoin holder.
- Standard Chartered's Anchorpoint launched a beta of HKDAP in August, limited to institutional and professional investors.
- The article questions what the stablecoin layer adds for everyday users who can already do much of this through PayMe.
- The article is paywalled after its opening section, so this note covers the free portion only.

## Significance

- Records the first retail bank stablecoin under the Hong Kong licensing regime, following the Anchorpoint beta.
- Shows the KYC-by-closed-loop route to meeting holder identification rules.
- Links stablecoin rails to a tokenised money market fund share class.
