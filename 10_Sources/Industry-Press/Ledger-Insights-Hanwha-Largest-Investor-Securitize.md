# Korea's Hanwha Is Largest Investor in Listed Tokenization Firm Securitize

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Korea's Hanwha Is Largest Investor in Listed Tokenization Firm Securitize". Ledger Insights, 21 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/koreas-hanwha-is-largest-investor-in-listed-tokenization-firm-securitize/.

## One-line summary

Hanwha Group holds a 9.7% stake in Securitize following its NYSE listing, ahead of Blockchain Capital, CEO Carlos Domingo and Morgan Stanley.

## Key claims

- Hanwha Group emerged as Securitize's largest shareholder, holding a 9.7% stake across multiple group entities, per SEC filings.
- Securitize listed on the NYSE via SPAC merger on 2 July 2026, raising $400 million at a $1.25 billion pre-money valuation.
- Securitize's market capitalisation stood at $1.06 billion following the listing.
- Blockchain Capital holds 6.1%, CEO Carlos Domingo holds 5.4% and Morgan Stanley holds 5%.
- All existing shareholders face lockup restrictions on their shares.
- Securitize manages BlackRock's BUIDL tokenised money market fund.
- Hanwha previously invested $5 million in Singapore's iStox, now ADDX, in 2020, and committed 30 billion won to Digital Asset's funding round.
