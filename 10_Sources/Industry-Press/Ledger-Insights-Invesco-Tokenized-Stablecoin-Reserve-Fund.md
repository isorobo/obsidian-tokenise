# Invesco to Launch Tokenized Stablecoin Reserve Fund

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Invesco to Launch Tokenized Stablecoin Reserve Fund". Ledger Insights, 28 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/invesco-to-launch-tokenized-stablecoin-reserve-fund/.

## One-line summary

Invesco has filed with the SEC to register a tokenised money market fund targeting stablecoin issuers, issuing shares directly as tokens on a public blockchain under GENIUS Act reserve requirements.

## Key claims

- Invesco filed on 24 June 2026 to register the Invesco Stablecoin Reserves Onchain Fund with the SEC, with effectiveness expected around late August.
- The fund invests in a GENIUS Act-compliant asset class: cash, Treasury securities with 93-day or shorter maturity, and overnight repurchase agreements backed by Treasuries.
- Fund shares are issued directly as tokens on a public blockchain, distinguishing this offering from conventional tokenised funds that hold off-chain shares.
- Superstate serves as sub-transfer agent alongside Invesco Investment Services, combining off-chain records with on-chain tokens.
- Ethereum is referenced extensively in the SEC risk disclosures, though the specific blockchain is not confirmed in the filing.

## Significance

- Confirms that major traditional asset managers are racing to build GENIUS Act-compliant reserve infrastructure for stablecoin issuers, deepening the connection between stablecoin regulation and tokenised fund markets.
- The direct on-chain issuance model — shares as tokens on a public blockchain — represents a structural shift from conventional fund administration and raises novel custody and transfer-agent questions.
- Invesco's SEC filing places this development firmly in US securities law, providing primary documentary evidence of regulatory posture toward tokenised stablecoin reserve products.
- Relevant alongside [[10_Sources/Industry-Press/Ledger-Insights-Fidelity-Stablecoin-Reserve-MMF|Fidelity Stablecoin Reserve MMF]] as part of a pattern of institutional GENIUS Act reserve fund launches.
