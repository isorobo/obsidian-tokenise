# Why Japanese Logistics Firm Will Use JPYC for Driver Payments

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Why Japanese Logistics Firm Will Use JPYC for Driver Payments". Ledger Insights, 20 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/why-japanese-logistics-firm-will-use-jpyc-for-driver-payments/.

## One-line summary

Japanese logistics firm AZ-COM Maruwa Holdings will adopt the JPYC yen stablecoin to pay contractor drivers instantly amid a national driver shortage.

## Key claims

- AZ-COM Maruwa Holdings, an Amazon logistics partner, intends to adopt JPYC stablecoin for contractor driver and partner firm payments.
- The firm values instant cash transfer and settlement as a tool to attract and retain drivers.
- Japan's logistics sector faces a driver shortage driven by an ageing population and rising e-commerce demand.
- Logistics firms are also exploring autonomous driving and foreign worker recruitment to address the shortage.
- Instant JPYC settlement could let drivers convert to USD stablecoins for faster international remittances.
- Nikkei first reported the firm's announcement.

## Excerpts

> "the ability to instantly transfer and settle in cash, a useful tool in the competition to attract drivers"
> ~ Ledger Insights article body, characterising AZ-COM Maruwa's rationale

## Significance

- Documents a concrete yen-stablecoin payroll use case tied to a specific labour-market shortage, not a speculative pilot.
- Suggests stablecoin payroll adoption in Japan may extend beyond crypto-native firms into mainstream logistics.
- Raises a secondary remittance use case, converting JPYC to USD stablecoins, relevant to cross-border payments coverage.
- Adds a retail-facing JPYC adoption data point alongside SBI's trust-bank JPYSC stablecoin launch.
