# Jack Henry plans to support tokenized deposits using Google Cloud Universal Ledger

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Jack Henry plans to support tokenized deposits using Google Cloud Universal Ledger". Ledger Insights, 23 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/jack-henry-plans-to-support-tokenized-deposits-using-google-cloud-universal-ledger/.

## One-line summary

Jack Henry will be the first core banking provider to integrate with Google Cloud Universal Ledger, letting community banks and credit unions offer tokenised deposits, with no client or launch date named.

## Key claims

- Jack Henry says it will be the first core banking technology provider to integrate with Google Cloud Universal Ledger.
- It sells core banking software to more than 7,200 community banks and credit unions.
- The announcement named no client bank and no launch date.
- Other Universal Ledger initiatives include a CME project on wholesale payments and tokenisation, and Swiss trials by AMINA Bank, Incore Bank and Crypto Finance.
- US community banks show growing interest in tokenised deposits, tied to deposit retention worries amid the stablecoin yield dispute.
- Survey figures cited: 11% of Jack Henry clients exploring, and in a CSBS survey almost 4% with a solution and about 17.5% planning one within 12 months.
- The article is partly paywalled, so this note covers the visible portion only.
