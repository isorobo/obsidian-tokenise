# Korea's Financial Services Commission publishes draft tokenization rules

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Korea's Financial Services Commission publishes draft tokenization rules". Ledger Insights, 2 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/koreas-financial-services-commission-publishes-draft-tokenization-rules/.

## One-line summary

Korea's FSC has released draft rules for tokenized securities issuance and fractional investments, open for comment until 11 November 2026 and due to take effect on 4 February 2027.

## Key claims

- The FSC released draft rules for tokenized securities issuances and fractional investments, following a roadmap it published the previous month.
- The rules cover tokenized traditional instruments and tokenized fractional investments, such as trust beneficiary tokens tied to real estate rental income or music revenue.
- Legislation passed earlier in 2026 recognizes distributed ledger-based securities.
- Public comments are open until 11 November, and the rules are scheduled to take effect on 4 February 2027, subject to FSC resolution, legislative review and cabinet approval.
- Each ledger used for electronic registration must include the Korea Securities Depository and at least two account management entities as participants, which also limits how many chains can be used.
- Directly charging a fee for the use of distributed ledgers is prohibited, because the ledgers serve as a public register.
- Industry has pushed back on the distributed ledger requirements.

## Significance

- It records a concrete Korean implementing step for DLT-based securities, with a dated comment period and effective date.
- The participant requirements (Korea Securities Depository plus two account managers per ledger) show a permissioned, infrastructure-centred model rather than open chains.
- The item is trade-press coverage; the FSC draft itself was not read, so check the primary text before relying on the detail.
- It complements the Bank of Korea tokenization work already in the vault.
