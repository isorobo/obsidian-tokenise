# MEP who led digital euro legislation issues rallying cry to scale tokenization across Europe

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "MEP who led digital euro legislation issues rallying cry to scale tokenization across Europe". Ledger Insights, 21 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/mep-who-led-digital-euro-legislation-issues-rallying-cry-to-scale-tokenization-across-europe/.

## One-line summary

MEP Fernando Navarrete, who coordinates digital euro legislation, published a non-paper proposing a High-Level Digital Markets Task Force to scale tokenisation across the EU.

## Key claims

- The non-paper focuses on removing obstacles that stop existing projects scaling, not on new rules.
- It describes a "Strategic European Paradox": the EU was first with MiCA and the DLT Pilot Regime but not first to production.
- Switzerland, the UK, Singapore and the US are ahead, and liquidity concentrates where it already sits, so the lag could become permanent.
- Existing pieces include the ECB's Pontes, the DLT Pilot sandbox and private stablecoin efforts such as Qivalis, but they lack unified political direction.
- He proposes a task force modelled on the UK's Wholesale Digital Markets Champion, bringing together the Commission, Eurogroup, ECB and ESAs.
