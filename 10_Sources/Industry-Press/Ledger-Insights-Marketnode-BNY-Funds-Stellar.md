# Marketnode Tokenizes BNY Funds on Stellar

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Marketnode Tokenizes BNY Funds on Stellar". Ledger Insights, 17 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/marketnode-tokenizes-bny-funds-on-stellar/.

## One-line summary

Singapore's Marketnode is tokenising BNY Investments funds on Stellar for institutional and accredited investors.

## Key claims

- Marketnode, a Singapore financial market infrastructure firm, plans to tokenise BNY Investments funds on the Stellar network.
- Access will be limited to institutional and accredited investors.
- Marketnode's Fundnode settlement platform processed nearly 1.5 million transactions in its first full year since its 2024 launch.
- Marketnode was established in 2022 by Temasek and the Singapore Exchange, with HSBC and Euroclear investing subsequently.
- Franklin Templeton's BENJI tokenised money market fund already runs on Stellar, and the DTCC will add Stellar as a third supported blockchain for tokenised stocks next year.
- Stellar's native token architecture gives regulated institutions built-in controls to correct errors or fraudulent transactions.
- CGS International and WisdomTree have separate Marketnode distribution arrangements, extending the platform's tokenised fund ecosystem.

## Significance

- Adds BNY to the roster of major asset servicers distributing tokenised funds through Marketnode, alongside Franklin Templeton and WisdomTree.
- Reinforces Stellar's position as shared infrastructure for tokenised money market and investment funds ahead of DTCC's planned support.
- Marketnode's Temasek and SGX ownership ties this deal to Singapore's broader Project Guardian tokenisation programme.
- The Fundnode volume figure gives the corpus a concrete usage metric for a live tokenised-fund settlement platform, not just a pilot announcement.
