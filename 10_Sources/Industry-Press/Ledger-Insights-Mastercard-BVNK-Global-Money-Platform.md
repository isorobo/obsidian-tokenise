# Mastercard integrates BVNK stablecoin platform as Visa goes live with prefunding

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Mastercard integrates BVNK stablecoin platform as Visa goes live with prefunding". Ledger Insights, 15 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/mastercard-integrates-bvnk-stablecoin-platform-as-visa-goes-live-with-prefunding/.

## One-line summary

Mastercard launched its Global Money Platform, the first product from its $1.8 billion BVNK acquisition, on the same day Visa moved stablecoin prefunding for Visa Direct into production.

## Key claims

- Mastercard's Global Money Platform (GMP) offers one API for accounts holding fiat and stablecoins, with conversion between them.
- The BVNK acquisition ($1.8 billion) closed in August.
- GMP supports stablecoin-linked cards and payouts via Mastercard Move; Move payouts are planned to cover over 40 currencies in more than 200 countries during 2027, with tokenised deposits also planned.
- Named early customers are Nuvei, Marqeta, Ontop, Beem and Almond FinTech; their stage is unclear.
- Visa said Visa Direct stablecoin prefunding for cross-border payments entered production, with Inyo Global as first enabler.
- Visa's standalone Visa Stablecoin Platform launched in July.
