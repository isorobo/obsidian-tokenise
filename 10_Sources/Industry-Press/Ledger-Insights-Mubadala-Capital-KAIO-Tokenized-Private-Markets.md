# Mubadala Capital Tokenizes Private Markets Strategy via KAIO

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Mubadala Capital Tokenizes Private Markets Strategy via KAIO". Ledger Insights, 25 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/mubadala-capital-tokenizes-private-markets-strategy-via-kaio/.

## One-line summary

KAIO, an Abu Dhabi tokenisation platform co-founded by Nomura's Laser Digital, launched a tokenised version of Mubadala Capital's private markets strategy across three public blockchains.

## Key claims

- KAIO, based in Abu Dhabi Global Market and co-founded by Nomura's Laser Digital, launched a tokenised Mubadala Capital private markets product.
- The tokenised product is accessible on the Base, Solana and Sui blockchains.
- The product has attracted approximately $75 million in onchain total value locked from traditional and digital asset investors.
- Mubadala Capital manages over $600 billion in assets as a subsidiary of Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund, Mubadala Investment Company.
- Mubadala Capital oversees more than $60 billion across private equity, credit, venture capital and co-investment platforms.
- Coinbase plans to add the tokenised product to its corporate balance sheet.

- Demonstrates a sovereign-wealth-linked private markets manager adopting multi-chain tokenisation rather than a single permissioned ledger.
- Coinbase's stated plan to hold the product on its corporate balance sheet signals institutional treasury demand for tokenised private credit exposure.
