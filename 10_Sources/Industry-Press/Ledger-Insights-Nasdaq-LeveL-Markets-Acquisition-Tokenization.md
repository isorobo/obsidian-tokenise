# Nasdaq to Acquire LeveL Markets ATS in 24/7 Tokenization Push

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Nasdaq to Acquire LeveL Markets ATS in 24/7 Tokenization Push". Ledger Insights, 12 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/nasdaq-to-acquire-level-markets-ats-in-24-7-tokenization-push/.

## One-line summary

Nasdaq agreed to acquire the third-largest US alternative trading system, folding it into a new digital liquidity division built around tokenisation.

## Key claims

- Nasdaq agreed to acquire LeveL Markets ATS, the third-largest US Alternative Trading System, serving 2,500 institutional clients.
- LeveL Markets will operate as a standalone entity inside a new Nasdaq division called Digital Liquidity Networks.
- Roland Chai leads the new division, which integrates Nasdaq's existing tokenisation capabilities and digital asset solutions.
- LeveL Markets recorded 56% year-over-year average daily volume growth in 2025, and Nasdaq has held a minority stake since 2021.
- LeveL Markets partnered with EU DLT Pilot Regime firm 21X in January 2026 for on-chain trading and settlement.
- Nasdaq's tokenised DTC token settlement option launches in October 2026 alongside DTCC's own tokenisation solution.
- Nasdaq also has a European tokenisation partnership with Boerse Stuttgart Group's Seturion covering structured products across seven venues.

## Excerpts

> "the programmable, always-on market infrastructure of the future"
> ~ Roland Chai, describing the mission of Nasdaq's Digital Liquidity Networks division

## Significance

- Signals a major US exchange group consolidating alternative trading system capacity directly into its tokenisation strategy, rather than building parallel infrastructure.
- The October 2026 timing alignment with DTCC's tokenisation launch evidences a converging market-infrastructure timeline across competing US venues.
- LeveL Markets' prior DLT Pilot Regime link via 21X connects US exchange consolidation to EU pilot-regime infrastructure covered elsewhere in the corpus.
- Adds a market-structure data point, 56% average daily volume growth, evidencing real trading activity behind the tokenisation narrative rather than pilot-only activity.
