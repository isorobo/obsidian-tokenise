# Nomura Plans to Partner Stablecoin Issuer Circle for Cross-Border FX

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Nomura Plans to Partner Stablecoin Issuer Circle for Cross-Border FX". Ledger Insights, 25 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/nomura-plans-to-partner-stablecoin-issuer-circle-for-cross-border-fx/.

## One-line summary

Nomura plans to use Circle's USDC stablecoin to compress FX settlement times for Japanese corporates from half a day to near-instant, while retaining its traditional role as FX dealer and spread earner.

## Key claims

- Nomura intends to collaborate with Circle to enable rapid foreign currency settlement for Japanese corporations, targeting a 2027 launch.
- Current FX settlements take approximately half a day; the USDC-based model targets near-instant settlement using blockchain rails.
- Japan's FX market processed $440 billion in daily transactions as of 2025, indicating the scale of potential adoption.
- Nomura will manage client onboarding, compliance, and banking system integration, preserving its dealer role and spread-based revenue.
- Circle issues USDC ($74 billion in circulation) and EURC; the stablecoin replaces the settlement infrastructure, not the FX transaction itself.

## Excerpts

> "The stablecoin replaces the settlement plumbing underneath, not the trade itself."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Illustrates how a major non-US FX dealer is using stablecoin settlement to compete on speed without displacing the traditional dealer-intermediation model.
- Japan is a significant jurisdiction for stablecoin adoption given its revised Payment Services Act framework; this partnership provides evidence of live commercial deployment.
- Relevant to cross-border-payments doctrine: the Nomura/Circle model separates FX price discovery from settlement, a structural change with implications for correspondent banking and SWIFT rails.
- Complements the Ripple/RLUSD Japan launch as part of a wider Japanese financial-sector stablecoin adoption pattern in mid-2026.
