# OCC relaxes reserve and yield rules in final GENIUS Act stablecoin regulations

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "OCC relaxes reserve and yield rules in final GENIUS Act stablecoin regulations". Ledger Insights, 21 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/occ-relaxes-reserve-and-yield-rules-in-final-genius-act-stablecoin-regulations/.

## One-line summary

The OCC's final GENIUS Act stablecoin rules, effective 18 January 2027, loosen the February proposals on liquidity, maturity and concentration, with reserves treated more lightly than yield.

## Key claims

- The OCC published final GENIUS Act stablecoin rules of more than 700 pages, taking effect on 18 January 2027.
- The daily liquidity requirement was cut from 10% to 5%, and the proposed 30% weekly liquidity requirement was dropped.
- The 20-day cap on weighted average maturity was removed, so an issuer could hold 5% in daily liquidity and the rest in Treasuries of up to 93 days.
- Issuers holding mostly Treasuries may obtain extra liquidity through repo while markets function normally.
- The proposed 50% single-institution cap for daily liquidity was eliminated; the 40% limit was kept but with many exclusions (government exposures, centrally cleared repo, self-held assets, qualifying custodians).
- The rule makes explicit that an issuer can custody 100% of its own assets.
- The article is paywalled after its opening section, so this note covers the free portion only.
