# Ondo Launches Onshore US Tokenized Stocks with Ownership Rights

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Ondo Launches Onshore US Tokenized Stocks with Ownership Rights". Ledger Insights, 2 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ondo-launches-onshore-us-tokenized-stocks-with-ownership-rights/.

## One-line summary

Ondo Finance has launched SEC-registered tokenised US stocks that confer full ownership and voting rights, contrasting with its synthetic offshore product and rival Dinari's dShares.

## Key claims

- Ondo Finance introduced tokenised US stocks through its SEC-registered transfer agent subsidiary, Oasis Pro TA, tokenising stocks held in custody as security entitlements.
- Token holders under the US product receive full ownership and voting rights, unlike Ondo's offshore product issued via BVI-based Ondo Global Markets, which tracks stock prices through structured notes without direct ownership.
- The offshore tokens identify holders only at issuance or redemption, similar to stablecoins, allowing use in DeFi trading, though offshore token votes are not legally binding because those tokens are synthetic.
- Ondo's onshore broker-dealer, transfer agent and custodian structure must comply with applicable US securities regulations at each layer.
- Ondo partnered with Broadridge so token holders can cast votes at annual general meetings via ProxyVote.com, distinguishing it from competitor Dinari's roughly two-year-old dShares product for accredited investors.

## Significance

- Draws a clear doctrinal line between direct-ownership security tokens and synthetic offshore price-tracking tokens, bearing directly on custody and transfer doctrine for tokenised equity.
- Demonstrates a working SEC-registered transfer agent model for blockchain-based shareholder records with voting rights, extending prior SEC staff FAQ guidance into a live consumer product.
- The Broadridge proxy-voting integration is a template for reconciling on-chain token ownership with existing corporate governance infrastructure.
- Relevant alongside DTC's Stellar integration and the GDF/ISDA tokenised collateral report as evidence of US market infrastructure absorbing tokenised securities in 2026.
