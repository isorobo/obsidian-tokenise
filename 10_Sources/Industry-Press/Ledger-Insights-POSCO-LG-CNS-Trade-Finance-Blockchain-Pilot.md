# POSCO International Runs Blockchain Trade Finance Pilot With LG CNS

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "POSCO International Runs Blockchain Trade Finance Pilot With LG CNS". Ledger Insights, 27 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/posco-international-runs-blockchain-trade-finance-pilot-with-lg-cns/.

## One-line summary

POSCO International ran a proof of concept with LG CNS testing blockchain and AI agents for trade finance settlement, with a full pilot planned for later in 2026.

## Key claims

- POSCO International, a major Korean trading company, ran a proof of concept with LG CNS as technology partner.
- The trial explored blockchain and AI agents for trade finance transaction management and settlement.
- A shared ledger let subsidiaries and trading partners access transaction records, reducing reconciliation needs.
- The Injective blockchain managed anti-money-laundering processes within the trial.
- Trade finance receivables were tokenised as real-world assets within the pilot design.
- POSCO International plans to progress to a full pilot later in 2026.

## Significance

- Combines agentic-AI transaction management with RWA tokenisation of trade finance receivables in a single corporate pilot.
- Uses a public blockchain, Injective, for AML controls within a Korean corporate group's shared ledger design.
- Extends tokenisation activity beyond financial institutions into a major industrial trading company's back-office operations.
- Relevant to tracking how AI agents and tokenisation combine in non-bank corporate trade finance settlement.
