# Partior Conducts PoC With OpenAssets to Test Tokenized Deposit Clearing for Stablecoins

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Partior Conducts PoC With OpenAssets to Test Tokenized Deposit Clearing for Stablecoins". Ledger Insights, 30 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/partior-conducts-poc-with-openassets-to-test-tokenized-deposit-clearing-for-stablecoins/.

## One-line summary

Partior and OpenAssets tested using Partior's tokenised deposit network to clear and settle stablecoin transactions on a delivery-versus-payment basis.

## Key claims

- Partior, a multi-currency tokenised deposit network backed by systemically important banks, ran a proof of concept with OpenAssets.
- The trial integrated Partior's tokenised deposit network with OpenAssets' digital asset infrastructure for delivery-versus-payment settlement.
- OpenAssets shares Valor Capital as a common backer with Partior and holds connections to Tether.
- The trial focused on stablecoin redemption and clearing operations.
- OpenAssets CEO Gabor Gurbacs advises Hadron by Tether, Tether's tokenisation offering.

## Excerpts

> "Partior, a multi-currency tokenized deposit network backed by systemically important banks, conducted a proof of concept with OpenAssets to test clearing and settling stablecoin transactions using tokenized deposits."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Tests a direct settlement bridge between bank-issued tokenised deposits and stablecoin clearing, a key interoperability question for the corpus.
- Shows shared investor backing linking a bank-grade tokenised deposit network to Tether-adjacent infrastructure.
- Bears on delivery-versus-payment settlement design as tokenised deposits and stablecoins compete for the same clearing function.
- Relevant to tracking how systemically important bank consortia position tokenised deposits against stablecoin issuers.
