# Cecabank, Crédit Mutuel Join Regulated Layer One Tokenization Platform as It Launches

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Cecabank, Crédit Mutuel Join Regulated Layer One Tokenization Platform as It Launches". Ledger Insights, 28 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/cecabank-credit-mutuel-join-regulated-layer-one-tokenization-platform-as-it-launches/.

## One-line summary

SWIAT transferred its DLT platform to the newly launched Luxembourg-based Regulated Layer One cooperative, adding Cecabank and Crédit Mutuel as founding members.

## Key claims

- SWIAT officially launched the Regulated Layer One (RL1) blockchain cooperative in Luxembourg, transferring its DLT platform to the new organisation.
- SWIAT remains RL1's technical partner after the transfer.
- Cecabank, a Spanish wholesale bank, and Crédit Mutuel, a French cooperative banking group, joined as new founding members.
- Ten institutions make up RL1's launch membership, including five German banks, ABN Amro, Natixis CIB and Standard Chartered's SC Ventures.
- L-BANK and KfW moved to "supporter" status rather than full founding membership.
- The launch coincides with the Eurosystem's Project Pontes, due to go live in September, enabling onchain settlement of tokenised assets with central bank money.

## Excerpts

> "SWIAT announced the official launch of the Regulated Layer One (RL1) blockchain cooperative, with SWIAT transferring its DLT platform to the Luxembourg organization."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Establishes a bank-owned, Luxembourg-domiciled shared settlement layer as a distinct European alternative to vendor-controlled tokenisation platforms.
- Links directly to the Eurosystem's Project Pontes, positioning RL1 as infrastructure for central-bank-money settlement of tokenised assets.
- Shows ten major European banks, including a UK participant, committing jointly to shared tokenisation infrastructure rather than proprietary platforms.
- Relevant to tracking European bank-led alternatives to Canton Network and other US-originated tokenisation rails.
