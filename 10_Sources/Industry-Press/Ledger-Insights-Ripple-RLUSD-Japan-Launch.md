# Ripple's RLUSD Stablecoin Launches in Japan

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Ripple's RLUSD Stablecoin Launches in Japan". Ledger Insights, 25 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ripples-rlusd-stablecoin-launches-in-japan/.

## One-line summary

Japan's Financial Services Agency has approved Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin for distribution through SBI VC Trade on Ethereum, subject to a ¥1 million holding cap, marking the first regulated US-dollar stablecoin launch in Japan from a non-bank issuer.

## Key claims

- Japan's FSA approved RLUSD for circulation through SBI VC Trade, the crypto exchange subsidiary of SBI Holdings.
- The launch operates on Ethereum, which hosts approximately half of all RLUSD issuance globally.
- SBI VC Trade had previously supported USDC for over a year; it now offers both stablecoins.
- Both USDC and RLUSD distributed through SBI VC Trade face a ¥1 million ($6,180) cap on transactions and holdings, a regulatory constraint that limits retail-scale adoption.
- SBI has been an investor in Ripple for over a decade and operates SBI Ripple Asia as a joint venture.

## Significance

- Provides primary evidence of Japan's revised stablecoin regulatory framework in live commercial operation, including how the FSA structures holding limits as a prudential tool.
- The ¥1 million cap raises a question central to jurisdictional comparative analysis: whether regulatory ceilings defeat the commercial case for stablecoin adoption.
- SBI's simultaneous investment in Circle (USDC issuer) and Ripple (RLUSD issuer) illustrates the strategic positioning of Japanese financial groups across competing stablecoin ecosystems.
- Complements the Nomura/Circle cross-border FX article as evidence of Japan's accelerating stablecoin integration in mid-2026.
