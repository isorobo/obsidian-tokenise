# Why SBI Is Buying Bitbank, and Why Korean Banks Are Piling into Crypto Exchanges

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Why SBI Is Buying Bitbank, and Why Korean Banks Are Piling into Crypto Exchanges". Ledger Insights, 26 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/why-sbi-is-buying-bitbank-and-why-korean-banks-are-piling-into-crypto-exchanges/.

## One-line summary

SBI Holdings' ¥46.7 billion acquisition of Bitbank positions the combined entity as Japan's largest exchange by custody assets, with SBI explicitly targeting stablecoin issuance and on-chain finance rather than crypto trading volume.

## Key claims

- SBI Holdings plans to acquire crypto exchange Bitbank for ¥46.7 billion ($289 million), expected to close around October 2026, making Bitbank a wholly owned subsidiary via SBICAH.
- The combined SBI VC Trade and Bitbank platform would hold ¥1.1 trillion ($6.8 billion) in custodial assets and serve approximately 2.92 million accounts, ranking first among domestic Japanese exchanges.
- SBI stated its strategic goal as "new business opportunities in digital assets including stablecoins and on-chain finance," not growth in crypto trading volumes.
- The acquisition followed the partial launch of JPYSC, SBI Shinsei Trust Bank's yen stablecoin, signalling a vertically integrated stablecoin and distribution strategy.
- Korean banks and financial institutions are making parallel moves into crypto exchange stakes in 2026, anticipating forthcoming stablecoin legislation in that jurisdiction.

## Significance

- Demonstrates that Japanese financial groups are pursuing vertical integration — combining trust bank stablecoin issuance (JPYSC), crypto exchange distribution (Bitbank/SBI VC Trade), and institutional custody — as a unified on-chain finance strategy.
- Provides jurisdiction-specific evidence of how Japan's stablecoin legislative framework (revised Payment Services Act) is driving M&A activity rather than organic growth alone.
- The Korean parallel is significant: it suggests a regional pattern of bank-crypto exchange integration as stablecoin regulation crystallises, relevant to comparative regulatory analysis across Asia-Pacific.
- Relevant alongside the Nomura/Circle and Ripple/RLUSD articles as evidence of Japan's accelerating institutional stablecoin adoption in mid-2026.
