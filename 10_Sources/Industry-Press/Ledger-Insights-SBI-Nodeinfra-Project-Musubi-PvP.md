# Japan's SBI Explores PvP Cross Border Payments with Korea Using Stablecoins

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Japan's SBI Explores PvP Cross Border Payments with Korea Using Stablecoins". Ledger Insights, 8 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/japans-sbi-explores-pvp-cross-border-payments-with-korea-using-stablecoins/.

## One-line summary

SBI Digital Practice and Korea's Nodeinfra are building Project Musubi, a Canton Network payment-versus-payment corridor using yen, won and dollar stablecoins.

## Key claims

- SBI Digital Practice (SBI DP) partnered with Korea's Nodeinfra to build Project Musubi.
- Project Musubi is a cross-border payment network between Japan and Korea on the Canton Network.
- The network uses yen, won and dollar-denominated stablecoins alongside tokenised assets.
- SBI DP builds integration layers connecting traditional financial systems and supports Japanese institutions.
- Nodeinfra leads the core payment protocol, Daml smart contracts and developer tools, and onboards Korean institutions and custodians.
- The design incorporates payment-versus-payment settlement and distributed netting among participants.
- SBI's own yen stablecoin, JPYSC, launched in June but remains restricted to the SBI VC Trade platform pending regulatory and tax clarification.
- Korean stablecoin legislation, expected earlier in 2026, has not yet been finalised.

## Significance

- Extends payment-versus-payment settlement design, familiar from BIS Project Agorá, to a bilateral Japan-Korea stablecoin corridor.
- Shows regulatory uncertainty in both jurisdictions constraining stablecoin circulation even as infrastructure build-out proceeds.
- Adds the Canton Network to the corpus's record of institutional DLT platforms hosting cross-border stablecoin settlement.
- Illustrates Korean financial institutions positioning around crypto exchanges and stablecoin infrastructure ahead of finalised legislation.
