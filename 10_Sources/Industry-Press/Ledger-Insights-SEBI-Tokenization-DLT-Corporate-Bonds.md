# India's SEBI Plans Tokenization, DLT Pilots for Corporate Bonds

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "India's SEBI Plans Tokenization, DLT Pilots for Corporate Bonds". Ledger Insights, 7 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/indias-sebi-plans-tokenization-dlt-pilots-for-corporate-bonds/.

## One-line summary

India's securities regulator SEBI announced a corporate bond pilot combining tokenisation, DLT and CBDC-based settlement.

## Key claims

- The Securities and Exchange Board of India announced a corporate bond pilot using tokenisation and distributed ledger technology.
- The pilot aims to test faster settlement, operational efficiencies, smart-contract programmability and CBDC-based settlement integration.
- SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey first revealed the plan in late May at the CareEdge Debt Market Summit.
- Pandey projected a limited-scale launch over six to nine months.
- The pilot builds on India's CAT (CBDC and Asset Tokenisation) Sandbox, launched in October.
- SEBI aims to standardise India's fragmented corporate bond market and leverage the digital rupee, in both retail and wholesale form.
- Global corporate digital bond issuance to date has come mainly from Siemens (€360 million across two issuances) and POSCO International ($100 million in Hong Kong), with sovereign and financial-institution issuers otherwise dominant.

## Excerpts

> "Faster settlement, operational efficiencies, programmability through smart contracts and integration with CBDC-based settlement mechanisms."
> ~ SEBI pilot description, Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Adds India to the set of major Asia-Pacific jurisdictions running combined tokenisation-CBDC pilots for debt securities.
- Positions the digital rupee's wholesale CBDC leg as core settlement infrastructure for the corporate bond pilot.
- Highlights the continued scarcity of corporate (as opposed to sovereign or SSA) tokenised bond issuance globally.
- Extends the corpus's regulator-led sandbox coverage beyond MAS Project Guardian and HKMA Project Ensemble.
