# SEC Greenlights Franklin Templeton Funds to Park Cash in Tokenized BENJI Fund

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "SEC Greenlights Franklin Templeton Funds to Park Cash in Tokenized BENJI Fund". Ledger Insights, 13 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/sec-greenlights-franklin-templeton-funds-to-park-cash-in-tokenized-benji-fund/.

## One-line summary

The SEC issued a no-action letter letting Franklin Templeton funds sweep excess cash into its tokenized BENJI money market fund.

## Key claims

- The SEC issued a no-action letter permitting Franklin Templeton's registered funds to deposit excess cash into BENJI, its tokenized money market fund.
- BENJI, launched in 2021, was the first tokenized money market fund from a major asset manager and now manages about $726 million.
- The approval addresses the transfer agent's custody of assets using private keys, a novel structural point for SEC sign-off.
- Franklin Templeton intends to use BENJI both for cash management and as securities lending collateral.
- BENJI runs on the Stellar blockchain with a smart contract wrapper, and Franklin Templeton's in-house transfer agent keeps personal information off-chain.
- The SEC required that smart contract admin keys transfer to any successor transfer agent, a condition that may template future tokenized fund structures.
- The approval precedes DTCC's planned October 2026 launch of tokenized Treasuries.

## Excerpts

> "hourly net asset value calculations, intraday trading, faster transaction processing and the potential for reduced costs, as well as enhanced data security"
> ~ Franklin Templeton, on BENJI's stated benefits

## Significance

- Establishes a regulatory precedent for SEC treatment of private-key custody by a fund transfer agent, directly relevant to the corpus's custody doctrine.
- Extends BENJI's institutional footprint from a standalone tokenised fund product to an operational cash-management sweep vehicle inside a major asset manager.
- The admin-key succession condition is a doctrinal template other tokenised fund issuers are likely to face on future no-action requests.
- Positions BENJI as infrastructure ahead of DTCC's October 2026 tokenized Treasuries launch, evidencing convergence across competing tokenisation platforms.
