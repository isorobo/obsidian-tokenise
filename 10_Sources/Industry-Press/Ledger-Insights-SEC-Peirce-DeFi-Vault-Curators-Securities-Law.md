# SEC Commissioner Peirce Puts DeFi Vault Curators on Notice re Securities Laws

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "SEC Commissioner Peirce Puts DeFi Vault Curators on Notice re Securities Laws". Ledger Insights, 22 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/sec-commissioner-peirce-puts-defi-vault-curators-on-notice-re-securities-laws/.

## One-line summary

SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce warned that DeFi vault curators exercising discretionary allocation authority, rather than merely recommending allocations, may trigger securities law obligations.

## Key claims

- SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce issued a statement, titled "Headstands and Summervaults", addressing DeFi vault curators.
- Peirce distinguished vaults with programmatic allocations set by immutable smart contracts from vaults where curators exercise discretionary authority.
- Peirce identified discretion as the trigger for securities law applicability to curator activity.
- The statement uses Morpho and Gauntlet as examples, noting Gauntlet manages over $1.5 billion in Morpho vaults.
- The distinction turns on whether a curator merely recommends allocations, as Gauntlet previously did for Aave, or directly allocates capital and selects collateral.
- The curator model now manages billions of dollars in DeFi deposits across multiple protocols.

## Excerpts

> "Headstands and Summervaults"

## Significance

- Signals the SEC is scrutinising discretionary DeFi asset management as a securities-law question distinct from token issuance.
- Bears on licensing and disclosure doctrine by testing whether curator discretion converts a protocol role into an investment-adviser or broker-dealer function.
- Relevant to agentic and automated allocation models in decentralised finance, where discretion versus pure automation is the operative legal line.
- Affects how tokenised-fund and DeFi vault products structure curator mandates to avoid triggering securities registration.
