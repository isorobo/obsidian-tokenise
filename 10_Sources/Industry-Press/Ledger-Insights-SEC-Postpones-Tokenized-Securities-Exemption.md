# SEC Postpones Meeting Expected to Unveil Tokenized Securities Exemption

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "SEC Postpones Meeting Expected to Unveil Tokenized Securities Exemption". Ledger Insights, 17 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/sec-postpones-meeting-expected-to-unveil-tokenized-securities-exemption/.

## One-line summary

The SEC postponed a meeting due to unveil a tailored crypto offering regime and a tokenized stock innovation exemption tied to its Regulation NMS reform.

## Key claims

- The SEC postponed a meeting scheduled to discuss a tailored offering regime for investment contracts involving crypto assets.
- The postponed agenda covered three exemptions for early-stage cryptocurrency fundraising, first outlined by Chair Atkins in March 2026.
- A second postponed item was the tokenized stock innovation exemption, meant to support trading via decentralised finance automated market makers.
- Automated market maker pricing conflicts with Regulation NMS rules that ban execution at prices worse than the best automated quote elsewhere.
- The SEC proposed scrapping two NMS rules in June 2026 to remove this pricing obstacle, and the 60-day comment period closed the week of publication.
- The proposed exemption would limit eligibility to issuer-sponsored tokens, addressing the risk that thin liquidity pools distort automated market maker prices.

## Significance

- Tracks the SEC's stalled progress on a bespoke exemption regime for tokenized securities trading through DeFi AMMs, a live doctrinal question for securities-law treatment of on-chain venues.
- Links directly to the SEC's June 2026 proposal to scrap two Regulation NMS rules, extending the corpus's record of that rulemaking's progress.
- Shows the tension between AMM pricing mechanics and best-execution rules, a structural design problem for any tokenised-securities market infrastructure.
- Confirms the exemption's proposed scope would be limited to issuer-sponsored tokens, a guardrail relevant to future custody and disclosure doctrine.
