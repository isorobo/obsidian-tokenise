# Schroders Receives Approval for First Tokenized MMF Share Class

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Schroders Receives Approval for First Tokenized MMF Share Class". Ledger Insights, 6 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/schroders-receives-approval-for-first-tokenized-mmf-share-class/.

## One-line summary

Schroders won Central Bank of Ireland approval for a tokenised share class of its Schroders Onchain Active Returns money market fund.

## Key claims

- Schroders, with £867 billion ($1.17 trillion) under management, launched the Schroders Onchain Active Returns (SOAR) fund.
- The Central Bank of Ireland approved the tokenised money market fund share class.
- SOAR is a USD-denominated fund using a digital twin share class, not a natively digital instrument.
- JP Morgan's Kinexys handles tokenisation and JP Morgan acts as transfer agent.
- The share class supports unit transfers between clients, collateralisation and 24/7 treasury and liquidity management.
- The approval follows BlackRock's tokenised MMF launch for Irish UCITS funds in the same week.

## Excerpts

> "By combining money market investments with the benefits of distributed ledger technology, we are offering investors a new level of access, efficiency and security."
> ~ Meagen Burnett, CFO, Schroders

## Significance

- Confirms the Central Bank of Ireland as a recurring approval venue for tokenised UCITS money market funds.
- Adds a third major asset manager, after BlackRock and Aviva Investors, to launch a tokenised MMF share class within one week.
- Reinforces JP Morgan's Kinexys as the dominant tokenisation and transfer-agency rail across competing UCITS launches.
- Evidences a doctrinal pattern of digital twin share classes as the preferred structure for regulator-approved fund tokenisation.
