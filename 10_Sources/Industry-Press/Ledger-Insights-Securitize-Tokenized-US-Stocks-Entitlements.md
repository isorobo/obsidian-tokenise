# Securitize launches tokenized stocks, but not the issuer-sponsored kind

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Securitize launches tokenized stocks, but not the issuer-sponsored kind". Ledger Insights, 8 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/securitize-launches-tokenized-stocks-but-not-the-issuer-sponsored-kind/.

## One-line summary

Securitize has launched about a dozen tokenized US-listed stocks as third-party "tokenized entitlements" on Solana, without issuer sponsorship.

## Key claims

- Securitize has launched about a dozen tokenized US-listed stocks, expected to include Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Alphabet, Tesla, Meta, Amazon, Netflix, Circle, MicroStrategy and Palantir.
- The tokens are not issuer-sponsored; Securitize tokenizes the shares without the involvement of the companies.
- The article says the tokens give ownership and voting rights similar to a broker omnibus account, unlike many offshore synthetic tokens that use special purpose vehicles.
- Securitize presents the entitlement tokens as a possible path to issuer-sponsored versions if companies later choose to issue directly.
- Securitize issues on Solana, which is not one of DTC's launch chains; DTC is preparing to release tokenized securities on a private Besu blockchain and the Canton Network.
- Regulated tokens typically require investors to be on an allowlist, so competition depends partly on allowlist size.
