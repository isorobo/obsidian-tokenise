# SoFi launches retail tokenized deposits paying 4.4%

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "SoFi launches retail tokenized deposits paying 4.4%". Ledger Insights, 22 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/sofi-launches-retail-tokenized-deposits-paying-4-4/.

## One-line summary

SoFi introduced SoFi Earn (SOFIE), an FDIC-covered tokenised deposit at SoFi Bank paying a variable 4.4%, convertible into the bank's SoFi USD stablecoin for spending or on-chain use.

## Key claims

- SOFIE is a tokenised deposit at SoFi Bank carrying FDIC coverage and paying a variable 4.4%.
- Customers convert to SoFi USD, the bank's stablecoin, to spend or move funds on-chain.
- The rollout to SoFi Crypto members runs through November.
- The 4.4% matches the promotional savings rate; the standing savings rate is 3.3%, and SOFIE pays 4.4% to any SoFi Crypto member without expiry, though the rate can change.
- The article sees SoFi competing with idle stablecoin balances at Coinbase and with tokenised money market funds more than with other banks.
- The economics work because of double-digit yields on personal loans.
- The article is paywalled after its introduction, so this note covers the free portion only.

## Significance

- A US retail tokenised deposit paired with a bank stablecoin, with interest paid to the holder.
- Bears on the stablecoin yield debate and the deposit-versus-stablecoin competition tracked elsewhere.
- Illustrates the two-token structure (deposit token plus payment stablecoin) at one US bank.
