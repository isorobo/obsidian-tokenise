# Sony Bank Receives Conditional OCC Approval for US Stablecoin Trust Bank

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Sony Bank Receives Conditional OCC Approval for US Stablecoin Trust Bank". Ledger Insights, 9 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/sony-bank-receives-conditional-occ-approval-for-us-stablecoin-trust-bank/.

## One-line summary

Sony Bank has won conditional OCC approval to charter a US national trust bank through its Connectia Trust subsidiary, aiming to issue a dollar-denominated stablecoin by 2027.

## Key claims

- Sony Bank announced preliminary conditional approval from the OCC to establish a national trust bank in the United States, applying in October 2025 through subsidiary Connectia Trust.
- The planned US business includes issuance and management of a dollar-denominated stablecoin, targeting a 2027 launch subject to further approvals in Japan and the United States.
- Sony Bank has explored stablecoins since early 2024, trialling yen-linked and other currency coins on the Polygon blockchain for Sony Group digital sales such as games.
- The bank frames the US charter as supporting Sony Group's US expansion and creating experiences for creators and fan communities.
- Ledger Insights assesses a direct US charter as the faster regulatory path, since Japan has not yet fully approved trust-type stablecoins and foreign issuers must demonstrate GENIUS Act equivalence.

## Significance

- Adds a second concurrent OCC national trust bank applicant alongside Circle, evidencing a wave of federal stablecoin charter applications in mid-2026.
- Illustrates a cross-border regulatory arbitrage pattern: a Japanese issuer choosing a US federal charter over Japan's slower trust-type stablecoin approval process.
- Ties a major non-financial technology and media conglomerate, Sony, directly into US-regulated stablecoin issuance for commerce use cases.
- Relevant alongside Circle's OCC approval and SBI's Japanese stablecoin build-out as evidence of parallel US and Japanese stablecoin licensing tracks.
