# Standard Chartered's Anchorpoint Launches Beta Version of HKDAP Stablecoin

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Standard Chartered's Anchorpoint Launches Beta Version of HKDAP Stablecoin". Ledger Insights, 12 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/standard-chartereds-anchorpoint-launches-beta-version-of-hkdap-stablecoin/.

## One-line summary

Standard Chartered's Anchorpoint began the beta issuance of HKDAP, the first stablecoin issued under a Hong Kong Monetary Authority licence.

## Key claims

- Standard Chartered's Anchorpoint unveiled the beta phase of HKDAP, a Hong Kong dollar-pegged stablecoin, with exchanges HashKey and OSL.
- HKDAP is the first stablecoin issued under the Hong Kong Monetary Authority's stablecoin licensing regime, awarded to Anchorpoint and an HSBC-led consortium in April 2026.
- Anchorpoint uses a B2B2C distribution model, with licensed distributors interacting directly with end users.
- HKMA rules require issuers to identify all stablecoin holders unless they can show the regulator that alternative risk-mitigating measures are effective.
- Beta access is restricted to institutions, corporates and professional investors, with retail access possible by year end.
- Joint venture partners Animoca Brands and Hong Kong Telecom support distribution, with Animoca's Moca Network digital identity solution flagged for future retail compliance.
- Anchorpoint is targeting cross-border payments and tokenised securities settlement as initial use cases.

## Significance

- Marks the first live issuance under Hong Kong's stablecoin licensing regime, converting the April 2026 licence award into operating product.
- The holder-identification requirement sets a comparatively strict AML baseline against which other jurisdictions' stablecoin regimes can be measured.
- Confirms Standard Chartered's continued build-out of Hong Kong digital asset infrastructure, alongside its other Canton Network backing.
- Extends the corpus's stablecoin-licensing coverage beyond the US GENIUS Act track to a live Asian regulatory precedent.
