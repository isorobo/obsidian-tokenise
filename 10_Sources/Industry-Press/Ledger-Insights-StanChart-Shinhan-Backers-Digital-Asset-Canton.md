# Standard Chartered, Shinhan Financial Become Backers of Canton Developer Digital Asset

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Standard Chartered, Shinhan Financial Become Backers of Canton Developer Digital Asset". Ledger Insights, 22 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/standard-chartered-shinhan-financial-become-backers-of-canton-developer-digital-asset/.

## One-line summary

Standard Chartered's SC Ventures and Shinhan Financial Group joined Digital Asset's $355 million funding round, backing the Canton Network the DTCC will use for its October tokenised-securities rollout.

## Key claims

- Standard Chartered's SC Ventures and Shinhan Financial Group invested in Digital Asset's $355 million funding round, announced in June.
- Digital Asset develops the Canton Network, one of two blockchains the DTCC will use for its October rollout of tokenised securities.
- Standard Chartered is the fifth systemically important bank to invest in Digital Asset within eighteen months, after BNY Mellon, BNP Paribas, Goldman Sachs and HSBC.
- Shinhan Financial is the second major Korean financial group to back Digital Asset, following Hanwha Investment & Securities' $20 million investment.
- Shinhan signed a June collaboration agreement with Canton Network involving Shinhan Asset Management and Shinhan Securities.
- KB Securities agreed to participate in the Asian expansion effort without a financial commitment.

## Excerpts

> "the potential to distribute Korean assets globally"
> ~ Shinhan Financial Group, on its Canton Network collaboration

## Significance

- Confirms five systemically important banks now hold equity in a single tokenisation infrastructure provider, concentrating institutional bet-placing on Canton Network.
- Links Digital Asset's funding round directly to the DTCC's October 2026 tokenised-securities rollout, a concrete settlement-infrastructure milestone.
- Signals South Korean financial institutions treating Canton Network as a channel to globally distribute Korean assets.
- Relevant to tracking bank consortium concentration risk in permissioned tokenisation infrastructure.
