# Standard Chartered to offer digital asset custody in Singapore

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Standard Chartered to offer digital asset custody in Singapore". Ledger Insights, 8 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/standard-chartered-to-offer-digital-asset-custody-in-singapore/.

## One-line summary

Standard Chartered plans to offer digital asset custody to institutional and accredited investor clients in Singapore, subject to regulatory approval.

## Key claims

- The service would cover certain cryptoassets, stablecoins and tokenized real-world assets.
- It would sit within the bank's Financing and Securities Services division, subject to regulatory approvals.
- The launch follows Standard Chartered's May buyout of minority investors in Zodia Custody, which consolidated custody under its own brand and avoided duplicate licensing costs.
- The bank already offers digital asset custody in the UAE (launched late 2024), Luxembourg and the UK (2025), and Hong Kong (earlier in 2026).

## Significance

- It shows a global bank extending a regulated custody offer for tokenized assets and stablecoins into a major Asian hub.
- Custody of tokens is a core doctrinal question for tokenisation; this is a market-side signal, pending approval.
- The item is trade-press coverage; no MAS approval had been announced in the text read.
