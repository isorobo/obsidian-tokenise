# State regulators diverge on stablecoin safe harbor as GENIUS Act deadline looms

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "State regulators diverge on stablecoin safe harbor as GENIUS Act deadline looms". Ledger Insights, 22 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/state-regulators-diverge-on-stablecoin-safe-harbor-as-genius-act-deadline-looms/.

## One-line summary

State bank supervisors want Treasury safe harbors for state-licensed stablecoin issuers when the GENIUS Act takes effect on 18 January, while NYDFS says the statute already allows continued operation.

## Key claims

- The Conference of State Bank Supervisors and three other state organisations asked Treasury for safe harbors for state-licensed issuers.
- NYDFS says no safe harbor is needed because "the statute already permits continued operations", and sees the bigger risk in Treasury rules being too soft on foreign stablecoins.
- States must have regimes "substantially similar" to the federal framework, self-certify, and face review by the Stablecoin Certification Review Committee; no state is expected to have approval by January.
- Treasury missed its July 2026 deadline for the principles states will be measured against.
- CSBS wants protection during SCRC review, the 180-day deficiency cure window, conditional certifications promising rules by January 2028, and temporary loss of recertification.
- CSBS warns that without these, "issuers will drift to the federal pathway through the OCC".
- The article is paywalled after the opening section, so this note covers the free portion only.
