# Citi, HSBC, UBS Among 17 Banks to Pilot Tokenized Deposits via Swift Blockchain

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Citi, HSBC, UBS Among 17 Banks to Pilot Tokenized Deposits via Swift Blockchain". Ledger Insights, 9 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/citi-hsbc-ubs-among-17-banks-to-pilot-tokenized-deposits-via-swift-blockchain/.

## One-line summary

Swift's blockchain-based shared ledger is now live for initial use, with 17 banks across six continents, including Citi, HSBC and UBS, preparing to pilot cross-border payments using tokenised deposits.

## Key claims

- Swift announced its blockchain-based shared ledger is ready for initial use, with 17 banks from six continents preparing to pilot live cross-border payments using tokenised deposits.
- The shared ledger acts as an orchestration layer rather than a standalone payments platform, recording and validating payment commitments while banks issue tokenised deposits on their own ledgers.
- The ledger runs on Besu, an open-source Ethereum-compatible framework, and supports round-the-clock fund movement, including overnight and weekend transactions.
- Interbank settlement between payer and payee banks still occurs separately through conventional channels such as RTGS systems or correspondent banking relationships.
- HSBC has connected its own Tokenised Deposit Service to the shared ledger, though first live transactions have yet to occur.

## Significance

- Marks a major market infrastructure milestone: the world's dominant messaging network moving from proof of concept to live shared-ledger orchestration for tokenised deposits.
- Demonstrates an interoperability model in which individual bank tokenised-deposit ledgers connect through a common orchestration layer rather than a single shared settlement asset.
- Confirms that legacy settlement rails, RTGS and correspondent banking, remain the final interbank settlement leg even where token issuance is distributed.
- Relevant alongside HSBC's Tokenised Deposit Service and Euroclear's stablecoin settlement work as evidence of converging bank-led tokenised money infrastructure in 2026.
