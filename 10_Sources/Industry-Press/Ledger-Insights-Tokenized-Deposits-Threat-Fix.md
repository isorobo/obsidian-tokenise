# Tokenized Deposits: The Threat and the Fix You Probably Missed

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Tokenized Deposits: The Threat and the Fix You Probably Missed". Ledger Insights, 26 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/tokenized-deposits-the-threat-and-the-fix-you-probably-missed/.

## One-line summary

Two underreported developments — Ant International's sweep from tokenised deposits to tokenised MMFs and the Hazel Network's stablecoin bridge — address the network-fragmentation problem that limits tokenised deposits to single-bank utility.

## Key claims

- Ant International's Whale treasury platform now allows corporate treasuries to sweep funds from tokenised deposits into tokenised money market funds, in collaboration with Credit Agricole, CACEIS, and Amundi.
- The sweep capability may divert cash balances away from traditional banking relationships, posing a structural threat to deposit-funded banks.
- Tokenised deposit networks suffer a fundamental limitation: a holder can only pay someone who banks with the same network participant.
- Vantage Bank and Custodia Bank's Hazel Network whitepaper proposes an automatic conversion of tokenised deposits to stablecoins (Avit) when sending outside the network, converting back on receipt.
- Multi-bank stablecoins combined with tokenised deposit networks may prove more practical than single-bank deposit token systems, particularly where the issuer holds an EU e-money token licence.

## Excerpts

> "If you hold a tokenized deposit, you can only use it to pay someone who banks with a deposit network participant."
> ~ Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Identifies a structural interoperability gap in tokenised deposit networks and names a proposed technical fix, making this a key reference for settlement infrastructure analysis.
- The Ant International/Amundi sweep demonstrates live corporate treasury integration of tokenised MMFs with tokenised deposits, a step beyond pilot conditions.
- The Hazel Network model — converting between tokenised deposits and stablecoins at network boundaries — represents a novel settlement design relevant to cross-border payments doctrine.
- Together, these developments illustrate the competitive pressure on traditional banks from the convergence of stablecoins and tokenised deposits.
