# Treasury publishes state stablecoin certification procedures under the GENIUS Act

## Citation

Ledger Insights staff. "Treasury publishes state stablecoin certification procedures under the GENIUS Act". Ledger Insights, 1 October 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/treasury-publishes-state-stablecoin-certification-procedures-under-the-genius-act/.

## One-line summary

The US Treasury-led Stablecoin Certification Review Committee has issued interim procedural rules, effective 30 September 2026, for certifying state stablecoin regimes under the GENIUS Act, although applications cannot yet be filed.

## Key claims

- The US Treasury released interim procedural rules for state stablecoin certification under the GENIUS Act.
- The Stablecoin Certification Review Committee, made up of the Treasury Secretary, the Federal Reserve Chair and the FDIC Chairman, issued the rules.
- The rules took effect on 30 September 2026, but certification applications will not be accepted until paperwork approvals conclude.
- The procedures accompany proposed rules on substantial similarity criteria that Treasury unveiled in April.
- State regulators have raised concerns about the substantial similarity proposals and object to federal stablecoin rules being applied in their jurisdictions.
- The GENIUS Act requires states to submit initial certifications by 18 January 2028.
- State regulators worry the window will close before they can comply, because no federal regulator has finalised its own GENIUS Act rules.

## Significance

- It covers the state-level track of GENIUS Act implementation, which decides whether state-chartered issuers can operate under a state regime recognised as substantially similar to the federal one.
- The January 2028 certification deadline (one year from the Act's effective date) against unfinished federal rulemaking is a concrete timing risk for the dual federal-state licensing model.
- It complements the vault's federal charter coverage of stablecoin issuers, such as the Circle and Sony Bank OCC items.
