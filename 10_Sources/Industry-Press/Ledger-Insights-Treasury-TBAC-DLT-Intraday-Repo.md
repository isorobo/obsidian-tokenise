# Treasury TBAC Presentation Outlines Potential of DLT, Intraday Repo

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Treasury TBAC Presentation Outlines Potential of DLT, Intraday Repo". Ledger Insights, 7 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/treasury-tbac-presentation-outlines-potential-of-dlt-intraday-repo-but-will-lenders-show-up/.

## One-line summary

The US Treasury's Borrowing Advisory Committee reviewed DLT-based intraday repo, estimating a market of up to $385 billion but flagging weak lender incentives.

## Key claims

- The US Treasury presented DLT-based intraday repo mechanisms to the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee.
- Estimated lender compensation is $1,200 for lending $1 billion for three hours.
- The presentation sized the potential market at $100 billion to $385 billion, against a theoretical ceiling of $2.1 trillion in bank-held collateral.
- Early transactions carry interest rates of 30 to 40 basis points.
- A typical bank reserve buffer of $20 billion could shrink through intraday repo access.
- Borrower incentives are clear: banks reduce expensive intraday liquidity costs.
- Lender incentives are weak, benefiting mainly money market funds and stablecoin issuers deploying idle cash between intraday and overnight windows.
- The presentation concluded widespread adoption is likely years away.

## Excerpts

> "Widespread adoption is likely years away."
> ~ Treasury TBAC presentation, quoted in Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Provides a rare US Treasury quantification of the addressable market for DLT-based intraday repo against tokenised Treasury collateral.
- Identifies money market funds and stablecoin issuers as the natural lender base for intraday repo liquidity.
- Names asymmetric borrower-lender incentives as the binding constraint on adoption, a doctrinal gap distinct from technology readiness.
- Anchors the corpus's coverage of tokenised Treasuries in official US policy analysis rather than vendor claims alone.
