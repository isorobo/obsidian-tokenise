# UBS Demos Permissionless Blockchain Compliance at Infrastructure Level

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "UBS Demos Permissionless Blockchain Compliance at Infrastructure Level". Ledger Insights, 2026-06-23. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/ubs-demos-permissionless-blockchain-compliance-at-infrastructure-level/.

## One-line summary

UBS and Nethermind demonstrated two proofs of concept on Ethereum Sepolia showing that banks can embed compliance controls at the block-production layer rather than only at the application layer.

## Key claims

- UBS and Nethermind completed two proofs of concept on Ethereum Sepolia embedding compliance into block production, not smart contracts alone.
- A related Deutsche Bank and Nethermind whitepaper from May 2025 addressed the same infrastructure-level architecture.
- The Basel Committee currently treats tokenised securities on permissionless blockchains equivalently to cryptocurrencies, applying punitive capital requirements.
- Most institutional compliance today operates at the application layer via allow lists and token-freezing mechanisms.
- The proofs of concept address MEV and front-running, which constitute illegal market manipulation in most jurisdictions.
- Banks transacting on Ethereum lack control over which counterparties process transactions and receive gas fees under the existing architecture.

## Significance

- Infrastructure-level compliance on permissionless blockchains is the key barrier to institutional adoption; this demonstration moves that barrier.
- The Basel Committee's punitive capital treatment of permissionless-chain assets directly affects banks' ability to use public blockchains for tokenised securities settlement.
- The proof-of-concept architecture, if adopted, could enable public-chain settlement while satisfying existing AML-CFT and market-integrity obligations.
- This is directly relevant to the vault's blockchain-settlement and market-infrastructure corpus.
