# UK Regulator Explores Tokenized Gold as Collateral

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "UK Regulator Explores Tokenized Gold as Collateral". Ledger Insights, 10 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/uk-regulator-explores-tokenized-gold-as-collateral-analysis/.

## One-line summary

The FCA is exploring tokenised gold as wholesale collateral, exposing a regulatory gap between token-level oversight and unregulated physical bullion custody.

## Key claims

- The UK Financial Conduct Authority is exploring whether tokenised gold can serve as collateral in wholesale financial markets.
- The initiative supports London's digitisation strategy for wholesale finance amid competition from Shanghai and Hong Kong as bullion trading hubs.
- HSBC already offers institutional tokenised gold trading through its Evolve platform for FX and precious metals.
- The World Gold Council has separately proposed wholesale digital gold initiatives aimed at collateral mobility without physical transfer.
- Physical gold custody falls outside the Financial Services and Markets Act's authorisation requirement, unlike custody of shares or bonds.
- A gold token will likely be treated as a regulated financial instrument, creating a mismatch between token-level regulation and unregulated underlying bullion.
- London vaults hold about $1.2 trillion of physical gold that sits outside FCA supervision, governed instead by exchange and vault-operator contract terms.
