# UK-US Transatlantic Taskforce Prioritizes Tokenized Assets, Stablecoins

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "UK-US Transatlantic Taskforce Prioritizes Tokenized Assets, Stablecoins". Ledger Insights, 15 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/uk-us-transatlantic-taskforce-prioritizes-tokenized-assets-stablecoins/.

## One-line summary

The UK and US committed jointly to integrating well-regulated stablecoins into payments and settlement, alongside confirmation that the UK's digital government bond will launch in early 2027 as the first G7 sovereign digital bond.

## Key claims

- UK Chancellor Rachel Reeves confirmed the UK's digital government bond, DIGIT, will launch in early 2027, making the UK the first G7 nation to issue a digital sovereign bond.
- A joint US-UK statement committed both governments to supporting the integration of well-regulated stablecoins into payments, settlement and tokenised financial markets.
- HM Treasury launched a consultation on modernising the Payment Services Regulation to bring UK-issued stablecoins within its scope.
- Both governments committed to avoid imposing inappropriately high ring-fenced resource requirements on foreign stablecoin issuers operating in each other's market.
- The UK previously excluded stablecoins from its Digital Securities Sandbox, but formally approved their inclusion the preceding month.

## Excerpts

> "Integration of well-regulated stablecoins into activities including payments, settlement, and tokenised financial markets"
> ~ joint US-UK statement

## Significance

- Signals coordinated UK-US stablecoin policy at government level, reducing the risk of conflicting reserve and licensing requirements between the two largest Western stablecoin markets.
- The reserve-requirement commitment bears directly on doctrine governing foreign stablecoin issuers, easing a common barrier to cross-border stablecoin operation.
- Confirms the DIGIT 2027 launch timeline from a bilateral policy source, corroborating the HSBC-LSEG infrastructure announcement covering the same instrument.
- Relevant alongside the GENIUS Act and UK Digital Securities Sandbox reforms as evidence of converging transatlantic tokenisation and stablecoin regulatory tracks in mid-2026.
