# UMBFS to Integrate with Corastone DLT for Private Markets

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "UMBFS to Integrate with Corastone DLT for Private Markets". Ledger Insights, 26 June 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/umbfs-to-integrate-with-corastone-dlt-for-private-markets/.

## One-line summary

UMB Fund Services will integrate with the Corastone permissioned DLT platform to provide a shared golden record of investor and transaction data for private markets funds, with StepStone as the first adopting client.

## Key claims

- UMB Fund Services announced integration with Corastone, a permissioned Hyperledger Fabric platform, for private markets fund administration.
- Corastone provides "a golden record of standardised investor and transaction data made available to the permissioned parties," reducing reconciliation among fund administrators, asset managers, and wealth managers.
- StepStone is the first UMBFS client to adopt the solution; Corastone launched in November 2025 with Apollo and Morgan Stanley as initial partners.
- KKR, Franklin Templeton, Fidelity Investments, Future Standard, and Hamilton Lane subsequently joined Corastone.
- This represents UMBFS's second DLT integration announcement within one week, alongside a separate iCapital deal.

## Excerpts

> "A golden record of standardized investor and transaction data made available to the permissioned parties."

## Significance

- Illustrates the shift in large fund administrators toward DLT-based shared data infrastructure as a settlement and reconciliation tool for private markets, evidence that blockchain settlement adoption is moving beyond pilot conditions.
- The Hyperledger Fabric choice — described as "a less common choice for such a new solution" — contrasts with public-chain tokenisation approaches, providing a point of comparison for permissioned-versus-public-chain architecture analysis.
- The rapid accumulation of institutional partners (Apollo, Morgan Stanley, KKR, Franklin Templeton, Hamilton Lane) within months of launch signals institutional confidence in permissioned DLT for private-markets infrastructure.
- Relevant to market-infrastructure analysis of how tokenised private credit and fund structures achieve scalable settlement without public blockchain exposure.
