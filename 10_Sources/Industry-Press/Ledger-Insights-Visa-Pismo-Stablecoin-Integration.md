# Visa to Integrate Stablecoin Platform With Pismo, Flags Acquisition Appetite

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Visa to Integrate Stablecoin Platform With Pismo, Flags Acquisition Appetite". Ledger Insights, 29 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/visa-to-integrate-stablecoin-platform-with-pismo-flags-acquisition-appetite/.

## One-line summary

Visa will integrate its Visa Stablecoin Platform with core banking subsidiary Pismo, while signalling openness to further stablecoin-sector acquisitions.

## Key claims

- Visa announced plans to integrate the Visa Stablecoin Platform (VSP) with Pismo, its cloud-native core banking subsidiary.
- CEO Ryan McInerney signalled Visa's willingness to build, integrate, invest in, or acquire stablecoin capabilities during Q3 earnings commentary.
- VSP launched earlier in July as an enterprise environment for stablecoin minting, redemption and transfer, initially supporting OpenUSD.
- The Pismo integration embeds stablecoin capabilities into a processor already used within banking infrastructure.
- Pismo can currently support tokenised deposits and plans to integrate third-party tokenised deposit infrastructure providers.

## Excerpts

> "Whether through building Visa products and services, integrating with partners, investing, or acquiring."
> ~ Ryan McInerney, CEO, Visa

## Significance

- Shows a card network embedding stablecoin issuance and settlement infrastructure directly into acquired core banking rails.
- Signals further stablecoin-sector consolidation, with a major payments incumbent explicitly flagging acquisition appetite.
- Positions tokenised deposits and stablecoins as complementary product lines within a single processor rather than competing rails.
- Relevant to tracking how incumbent payment networks respond structurally to stablecoin disintermediation risk.
