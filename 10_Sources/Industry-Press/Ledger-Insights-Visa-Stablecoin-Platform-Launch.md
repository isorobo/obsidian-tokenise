# Visa Launches Stablecoin Platform, Taking the Opposite Path to Stripe

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Visa Launches Stablecoin Platform, Taking the Opposite Path to Stripe". Ledger Insights, 16 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/visa-launches-stablecoin-platform-taking-the-opposite-path-to-stripe/.

## One-line summary

Visa launched the Visa Stablecoin Platform, letting banks and fintechs mint, redeem, hold and transfer stablecoins over Visa infrastructure rather than issuing its own stablecoin.

## Key claims

- Visa launched the Visa Stablecoin Platform, enabling financial institutions, fintechs and crypto firms to mint, redeem, hold and transfer stablecoins through Visa's infrastructure.
- The platform launches with support for Open USD, a consortium stablecoin backed by more than 140 partners.
- The platform includes wallet-as-a-service functionality and dual-approval controls for sensitive transactions.
- Visa's strategy preserves its historical role as infrastructure and governance provider, leaving stablecoin issuance to partner institutions, in contrast to Stripe's more vertically integrated approach.
- The Visa Stablecoin Platform is designed to interoperate with Visa's existing stablecoin settlement offerings.

## Excerpts

> "For most institutions the hard part isn't the concept, it's the operational reality."
> ~ Jack Forestell, Visa Chief Product and Strategy Officer

## Significance

- Confirms a major card network is building neutral stablecoin infrastructure rather than issuing a proprietary stablecoin, a distinct strategic model from bank-issued or fintech-issued coins.
- The wallet-as-a-service and dual-approval design targets institutional operational risk, the barrier Visa's own product officer identifies as the real adoption constraint.
- Positions Visa against Stripe's more integrated stablecoin strategy, giving the wiki a live comparison of two competing payment-network approaches to stablecoin infrastructure.
- Relevant alongside the GENIUS Act's licensing regime as evidence that established payment networks, not only new entrants, are building stablecoin issuance rails in 2026.
