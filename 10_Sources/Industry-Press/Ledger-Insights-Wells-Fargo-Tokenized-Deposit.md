# Wells Fargo to Launch Tokenized Deposit Offering in the Fall

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "Wells Fargo to Launch Tokenized Deposit Offering in the Fall". Ledger Insights, 4 August 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/wells-fargo-to-launch-tokenized-deposit-offering-in-the-fall/.

## One-line summary

Wells Fargo joins JP Morgan and Citi in launching a permissioned-blockchain tokenised deposit service for corporate clients from fall 2026.

## Key claims

- Wells Fargo will launch tokenised deposits on a permissioned blockchain for corporate and commercial clients starting fall 2026.
- The service operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week, year round.
- Initial currency support covers US dollars and sterling.
- Wells Fargo has already participated in Swift blockchain trials for cross-border payments.
- CFO Mike Santomassimo described the launch as expanding payment options without changing the client interface with the bank.
- Wells Fargo will automatically route eligible payments through the tokenised rail, unlike competitors that require manual transfers.
- Future plans include added programmability, more currencies and additional jurisdictions.

## Excerpts

> "Launching tokenized deposits is an important step forward as we expand payment options."
> ~ Mike Santomassimo, CFO, Wells Fargo

> "Improve speed, timing, and flexibility."
> ~ Wells Fargo, describing automatic routing of eligible payments, quoted in Ledger Insights article body

## Significance

- Adds a third major US bank, after JP Morgan and Citi, to commit to a tokenised deposit product, evidencing sector-wide convergence.
- Automatic payment routing distinguishes Wells Fargo's design from manual-transfer competitor models, a doctrinally relevant operational choice.
- Confirms Swift blockchain trial participation as a pathway from pilot to commercial tokenised deposit launch.
- Strengthens the corpus's evidence that tokenised deposits are moving from pilot to production among US and UK corporate banking clients.
