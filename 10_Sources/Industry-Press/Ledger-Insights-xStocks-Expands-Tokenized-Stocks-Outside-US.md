# xStocks Expands to Tokenizing Stocks Outside the US

## Citation

Ledger Insights. "xStocks Expands to Tokenizing Stocks Outside the US". Ledger Insights, 22 July 2026. https://www.ledgerinsights.com/xstocks-expands-to-tokenizing-stocks-outside-the-us/.

## One-line summary

Payward, owner of Kraken and xStocks, partnered with Dubai's GTN to tokenise Hong Kong stocks first, with the UK, Europe and South Korea to follow.

## Key claims

- Payward, which owns Kraken and xStocks, partnered with Dubai-based GTN to expand tokenised stock offerings beyond the US market.
- The initial expansion targets Hong Kong stocks, with the UK, Europe and South Korea planned next.
- xStocks has reached over $35 billion in transaction volume and serves nearly 200,000 holders worldwide.
- The tokens are structured as open-ended tracker certificates issued by a Jersey vehicle under Swiss ledger-based securities law.
- GTN will act as custodian in a role similar to Alpaca's function for US equities.
- GTN holds licences in the US, UK, Dubai, Hong Kong, Singapore and South Africa, and accesses ninety trading markets globally.

## Significance

- Extends the Swiss ledger-based securities certificate wrapper, previously US-focused, to non-US listed equities for the first time.
- GTN's multi-jurisdiction licence stack shows how tokenised-equity platforms route around single-jurisdiction securities barriers.
- Adds Hong Kong, UK, EU and South Korea as near-term tokenised-equity distribution targets to the wiki's market-structure coverage.
- Relevant to comparing xStocks' certificate structure against Ondo's and Coinbase's onshore US tokenised-stock models.
