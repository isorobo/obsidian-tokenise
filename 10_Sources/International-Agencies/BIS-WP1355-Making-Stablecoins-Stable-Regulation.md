# Making Stablecoins Stable(r): Can Regulation Help?

## Citation

Goel, Tirupam, Ulf Lewrick, and Isha Agarwal. "Making stablecoins stable(r): can regulation help?" *BIS Working Papers*, No. 1355, Bank for International Settlements, June 2026.

## Summary

The paper models how stablecoin issuers optimise their asset holdings in the absence of regulation and shows that the resulting equilibrium — minimal capital, bond-heavy portfolios — exposes users to default risk and generates broader financial instability. The authors evaluate two regulatory tools — capital requirements and liquidity requirements — and demonstrate that each reduces risk through distinct mechanisms: liquidity requirements shift portfolios toward cash, while capital requirements raise both cash holdings and capital buffers. Both instruments are necessary together to address systemic concerns comprehensively.

## Annotation

This paper is directly relevant to the vault's stablecoin strand. It provides the theoretical foundation for why unregulated stablecoin issuers structurally under-capitalise, and supplies a policy-grade analysis of how capital and liquidity regulation can correct that failure. The "usable buffer" framework — thresholds that may be breached under stress but incentivise reserve-building during normal times — is the same concept under active development in MiCA and the UK stablecoin regime, making this paper load-bearing for comparative regulatory analysis.
