# Hidden by complexity? Measuring stablecoin, crypto and decentralised finance ecosystems

## Citation

Aerts, Timothy; Heijmans, Ronald; Paulick, Jan; Vuletic, Violeta. "Hidden by complexity? Measuring stablecoin, crypto and decentralised finance ecosystems". BIS Working Papers No 1377, 15 September 2026. https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1377-hidden-complexity-measuring-stablecoin-crypto-and-decentralised-finance-ecosystems.

## One-line summary

On-chain indicators of crypto, DeFi and stablecoin activity depend heavily on methodological choices, so researchers and supervisors should treat them as bounded, noisy estimates with explicit assumptions.

## Key claims

- Public blockchains record every data point, yet scale, fragmentation and complexity obscure economic meaning.
- The paper uses blockchain data for Bitcoin, Ethereum and Tron to show three sources of measurement divergence.
- The three sources are aggregation of Bitcoin transaction values, spurious smart contracts from programmability, and cross-chain comparability of use cases such as stablecoins.
- Bitcoin transaction values vary by up to a factor of six across measurement approaches.
- Stablecoin activity on Ethereum is more closely tied to smart contract interactions, while on Tron it is more often held outside smart contracts.
- The authors propose granular, bounded estimates with explicit assumptions, technical classification and disaggregation.
- The measurement challenges generalise to other blockchains and DeFi ecosystems with a similar technical base.

## Significance

- Gives the vault a BIS caution on the data behind stablecoin usage claims, relevant to any source that cites on-chain volumes.
- Complements the BIS stablecoin transaction and flow papers by showing how sensitive their underlying metrics can be.
- Supports treating headline stablecoin and DeFi activity figures in other indexed sources as estimates.
