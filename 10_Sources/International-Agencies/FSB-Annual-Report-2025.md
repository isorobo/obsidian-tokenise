# Promoting Global Financial Stability: 2025 FSB Annual Report

## Citation

Financial Stability Board. "Promoting Global Financial Stability: 2025 FSB Annual Report". Financial Stability Board, 24 March 2026. https://www.fsb.org/2026/03/promoting-global-financial-stability-2025-fsb-annual-report/.

## One-line summary

The FSB's 2025 annual review reports on the full year's work including the crypto-asset peer review, urges consistent implementation of the 2023 crypto and stablecoin framework, and identifies non-bank financial intermediation leverage as the top systemic risk.

## Key claims

- Ensuring full and consistent implementation of the FSB regulatory framework for crypto-asset activities and global stablecoin arrangements is a stated priority for 2025 and forward.
- The FSB identified a slowdown in G20 reform implementation and plans strategic reviews to identify root causes.
- Non-bank financial intermediation leverage is identified as the leading financial stability concern for 2025.
- Data gaps and collection challenges persist across cross-border payment systems and digital asset markets.
- Operational resilience of financial institutions received focused FSB attention in 2025.
