# Thematic Peer Review on FSB Global Regulatory Framework for Crypto-asset Activities: Summary Terms of Reference

## Citation

Financial Stability Board. "Thematic Peer Review on FSB Global Regulatory Framework for Crypto-asset Activities: Summary Terms of Reference". Financial Stability Board, 21 February 2025. https://www.fsb.org/2025/02/thematic-peer-review-on-fsb-global-regulatory-framework-for-crypto-asset-activities/.

## One-line summary

The FSB sets out the scope, methodology, and four consultation questions for its 2025 peer review of how member jurisdictions are implementing the 2023 global crypto-asset and stablecoin regulatory framework.

## Key claims

- The peer review covers FSB member and select non-member jurisdictions' implementation of both sets of 2023 FSB high-level recommendations: one for crypto-asset activities and one for global stablecoin arrangements.
- The review examines four specific areas: business location decisions by crypto firms, regulatory compliance challenges, how financial stability vulnerabilities vary across jurisdictions, and emerging market practices that could threaten stability.
- The FSB invited stakeholder feedback with a deadline of 28 March 2025.
- The final report was scheduled for October 2025, providing a clear timeline for the accountability cycle.

## Excerpts

> "The objective of the review is to examine progress made by FSB member and select non-member jurisdictions in implementing the FSB's global regulatory framework, including any lessons learnt."
> ~ Terms of Reference, section 1

## Significance

- This document establishes the precise scope of what became the October 2025 thematic review, making it the methodological anchor for interpreting that report.
- The four consultation questions define the FSB's analytical lens on regulatory arbitrage and cross-border inconsistency.
- The document is the public record of stakeholder consultation, which distinguishes the October 2025 findings as evidence-based.
