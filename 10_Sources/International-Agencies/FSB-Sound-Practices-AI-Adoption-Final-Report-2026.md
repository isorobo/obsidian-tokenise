# Sound Practices for Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI): Final report

## Citation

Financial Stability Board. "Sound Practices for Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence (AI): Final report". Financial Stability Board, 15 October 2026. https://www.fsb.org/2026/10/sound-practices-for-responsible-adoption-of-artificial-intelligence-ai-final-report/.

## One-line summary

The FSB finalises twelve sound practices for the governance and risk management of AI in financial institutions, following its June 2026 consultation.

## Key claims

- The FSB issued twelve recommended practices to help financial institutions adopt AI responsibly, covering organisation-wide governance and risk management across the full AI lifecycle.
- Financial firms have used AI for years, but recent generative and agentic tools have drawn much more attention.
- Boards should tie AI adoption and governance to the firm's business model, risk tolerance and strategy.
- Risk frameworks should address AI explicitly, with documentation of AI use and systematic materiality and risk assessments.
- Data quality, explainability and human oversight should scale with each use case's risk and complexity.
- Cyber and ICT risks, and dependence on third-party AI providers including concentration and supply chain risks, need dedicated management.
- The final report follows a consultation that ended on 22 July 2026 and drew around 160 responses. Only the web page was readable; the PDF body text was not extractable.
