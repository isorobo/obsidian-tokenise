# Thematic Review on FSB Global Regulatory Framework for Crypto-asset Activities

## Citation

Financial Stability Board. "Thematic Review on FSB Global Regulatory Framework for Crypto-asset Activities". Financial Stability Board, 16 October 2025. https://www.fsb.org/2025/10/thematic-review-on-fsb-global-regulatory-framework-for-crypto-asset-activities/.

## One-line summary

The FSB peer review finds significant gaps and inconsistencies in how member jurisdictions have implemented its 2023 global framework for crypto-assets and stablecoins, creating financial stability risks and opportunities for regulatory arbitrage.

## Key claims

- Only 11 of 28 reviewed jurisdictions (39%) have finalised a regulatory framework for crypto-assets that addresses financial stability.
- Global stablecoin arrangement regulation lags behind crypto-asset regulation, with few jurisdictions completing comprehensive frameworks.
- Uneven implementation creates regulatory arbitrage opportunities and complicates cross-border oversight.
- The review recommends eight discrete actions directed at jurisdictions, the FSB itself, standard-setting bodies, and international organisations.
- Cross-border cooperation mechanisms remain fragmented despite crypto-assets' inherently global nature.
- A complementary review by IOSCO was published simultaneously, covering securities-focused aspects of the same framework.

## Excerpts

> "Implementation progress remains incomplete, uneven and inconsistent. This creates opportunities for regulatory arbitrage."
> ~ Press release, Arthur Yuen, Deputy Chief Executive, HKMA

> "Significant gaps and inconsistencies remain that could pose risks to financial stability and to the development of a resilient digital asset ecosystem."
> ~ Executive summary

> "The FSB is urging member jurisdictions to prioritise full and consistent implementation of its 2023 global framework recommendations."
> ~ FSB press release, 16 October 2025
