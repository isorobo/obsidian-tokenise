# How Central Banks Can Contain Financial Stability Risks as AI Accelerates Change

## Citation

Adrian, Tobias. "How Central Banks Can Contain Financial Stability Risks as AI Accelerates Change." IMF Blog, International Monetary Fund, 2026-07-23. https://www.imf.org/en/blogs/articles/2026/07/23/how-central-banks-can-contain-financial-stability-risks-as-ai-accelerates-change.

## One-line summary

The IMF's Financial Counsellor argues that AI compresses the time and distance shocks take to spread through finance, and sets out three oversight priorities for central banks to prevent AI-driven trading and lending from amplifying systemic risk.

## Key claims

- AI is becoming deeply embedded in trading, lending, market infrastructure, and financial supervision, changing how quickly shocks can become systemic.
- Under normal conditions AI-driven execution improves liquidity, lowers transaction costs, and accelerates price discovery.
- Herding is not new, but AI changes its dynamics, since many systems can react in parallel to the same information.
- Future flash crashes may arise less from coding errors and more from AI systems reacting simultaneously to shared signals.
- Central banks should pursue three priorities: strengthen oversight and governance of AI-driven trading, lending, and supervisory technology; improve visibility into AI dependencies and correlation risk; and deepen international cooperation on operational resilience and cybersecurity.
- AI should augment supervisory judgment, not replace it, and over-reliance on automated systems carries its own risk.
- Early, collective policy action can make AI reinforce financial resilience; delay risks instability that is faster, more correlated, and harder to manage than past episodes.

## Excerpts

> "AI compresses time and distance in finance. Trading, credit decisions, and supervisory analytics increasingly occur in real time, changing how shocks spread and how quickly they can become systemic."
> ~ Opening argument

> "Under normal market conditions, the effects are largely positive. AI-driven execution can help improve liquidity, lower transaction costs, and accelerate price discovery."
> ~ On AI in normal markets

> "Herding is not new, but AI can change its dynamics. Future flash crashes may arise less from coding errors and more from many AI systems reacting in parallel to the same information."
> ~ On systemic risk

> "AI should augment supervisory judgment, not replace it."
> ~ On supervisory technology

> "If policymakers act early and collectively, AI can reinforce global financial resilience. If they do not, future instability may be faster, more correlated, and harder to manage than past episodes."
> ~ Closing argument

- Supplies the wiki's clearest primary-source statement on why agentic-AI trading changes crash dynamics, naming parallel AI herding rather than coding error as the mechanism.
- Sets out three concrete oversight priorities, load-bearing for the wiki's agentic-AI and ai-governance domain nodes on central bank supervisory response.
