# New Frontiers for Digital Finance

## Citation

International Monetary Fund. "New Frontiers for Digital Finance". IMF Annual Report 2026: Navigating a Precarious World, In Focus, 23 September 2026. https://www.imf.org/annual-report/2026/in-focus/new-frontiers-for-digital-finance/.

## One-line summary

The IMF's 2026 Annual Report spotlight chapter on digital finance says stablecoins are expanding in cross-border payments and remittances, that tokenisation may matter more over the long term, and that policymakers worry about financial stability, currency substitution and financial integrity.

## Key claims

- Stablecoins are a renewed area of focus at the IMF, while tokenisation progress continues and many countries advance work on CBDCs.
- Stablecoins are expanding in cross-border payments and remittances, but they pose stability risks if the underlying assets lose value or user confidence erodes.
- The IMF expects tokenisation to have more long-term impact than stablecoins, because both physical and financial assets could eventually be tokenised.
- The largest stablecoin issuers hold substantial US Treasury holdings, which creates concentrated financial exposure.
- Asia is described as a testing ground for digital payment systems, with proactive regulation and fintech ecosystems supporting innovation in underbanked markets.
- Policymakers are concerned about financial stability, currency substitution (for example dollarisation) and financial integrity.
