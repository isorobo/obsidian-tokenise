# The Impact of Central Bank Digital Currency on Payments Competition

## Citation

Edona Reshidi, Marco Reuter and Manisha Patel. "The Impact of Central Bank Digital Currency on Payments Competition". IMF FinTech Notes, 11 November 2025. https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fintech-notes/issues/2025/11/11/the-impact-of-central-bank-digital-currency-on-payments-competition-571638.

## One-line summary

An IMF FinTech Note arguing that CBDC's effect on payments competition depends on market structure, regulation and design, with the largest gains in less competitive markets.

## Key claims

- As a payment instrument, CBDC expands end users' transaction choices.
- As platform infrastructure, CBDC gives intermediaries room to offer and innovate in payment and financial services.
- Its competitive impact depends on the existing payments landscape, regulation and CBDC design.
- In less competitive markets it may raise competition through pricing discipline, quality, contestability and broader access.
- In more competitive markets, effects are likely smaller and work mainly through wider access.
- Policymakers should address intermediary participation rules, fee structures, holding limits and interoperability standards.
