# Evaluating the Implications of CBDC for Financial Stability

## Citation

Marco Gross, Marcello Miccoli, Jeanne Verrier and Germán Villegas-Bauer. "Evaluating the Implications of CBDC for Financial Stability". IMF FinTech Notes No. 2025/008, 12 November 2025. https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fintech-notes/issues/2025/11/12/evaluating-the-implications-of-cbdc-for-financial-stability-571743.

## One-line summary

An IMF FinTech Note giving practitioners a framework to assess how a retail CBDC could affect domestic financial stability and how design can contain the risks.

## Key claims

- The note helps practitioners evaluate the domestic financial stability implications of retail CBDC.
- It identifies transmission channels through which CBDC can affect stability, negatively or positively, including bank funding and lending, run risk and payment resilience.
- The size and direction of effects depend on country characteristics, adoption and design features.
- It recommends combining estimates from existing studies with country-specific modelling.
- Appropriate design and safeguards can contain the risks while keeping benefits such as payment resilience.
- The overview refers to six channels but lists five, so the source is internally inconsistent on the count.
