# Payment Resilience in Fragile and Conflict-Affected States: Lessons for Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)

## Citation

Majid Malaika, Gabriel Soderberg and Kateryna Zhabska. "Payment Resilience in Fragile and Conflict-Affected States: Lessons for Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)". IMF FinTech Notes No. 2025/009, 12 November 2025. https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fintech-notes/issues/2025/11/12/payment-resilience-in-fragile-and-conflict-affected-states-lessons-for-central-bank-571748.

## One-line summary

An IMF FinTech Note drawing on fragile and conflict-affected states to show how payment ecosystems, and CBDC designs, can be made resilient.

## Key claims

- Payment continuity needs action across several ecosystem layers, not a single fix.
- The note assesses five layers: connectivity, infrastructure, intermediaries, payment solutions and user access.
- Useful strategies include offline capability, redundancy, interoperability, contingency planning and inclusive access.
- For CBDCs, it suggests offline capability, redundancy and interoperability with existing and private payment systems.
- Lessons come from Ukraine, West Bank and Gaza, Haiti, the CEMAC region, Tuvalu, Sudan and Yemen.
- The authors argue the lessons apply to preparedness in a wider range of countries.
