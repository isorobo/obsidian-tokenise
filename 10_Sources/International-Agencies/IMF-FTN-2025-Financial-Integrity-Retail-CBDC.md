# Financial Integrity Implications of Retail Central Bank Digital Currencies (rCBDCs)

## Citation

Kathleen Kao, Ke Chen, Stephanie Forte, Ben Aldersey and Giulio Soana. "Financial Integrity Implications of Retail Central Bank Digital Currencies (rCBDCs)". IMF FinTech Notes, 14 November 2025. https://www.imf.org/en/publications/fintech-notes/issues/2025/11/14/financial-integrity-implications-of-retail-central-bank-digital-currencies-rcbdcs-571769.

## One-line summary

An IMF FinTech Note examining how retail CBDC design features affect AML/CFT implementation and what policymakers should settle before launch.

## Key claims

- The note examines how international AML/CFT standards apply to retail CBDCs.
- It identifies categories and design characteristics of rCBDCs that carry financial integrity implications.
- rCBDC features can materially affect how well an issuing jurisdiction's AML/CFT regime works.
- Some current rCBDC designs may strain, or raise questions about, prevailing international AML/CFT standards.
- It sets out issues policymakers and competent authorities should consider before an rCBDC launch, with suggested good practices.
- Because rCBDCs are new and have limited real-world use, the analysis is preliminary in places.
