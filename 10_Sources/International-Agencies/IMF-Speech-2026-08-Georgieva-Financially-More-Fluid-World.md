# Navigating a Financially More Fluid World

## Citation

Georgieva, Kristalina. "Navigating a Financially More Fluid World". IMF Speeches (Jackson Hole Economic Policy Symposium), 28 August 2026. https://www.imf.org/en/news/articles/2026/08/28/sp082826-md-navigating-a-financially-more-fluid-world.

## One-line summary

The IMF Managing Director argues that stablecoins and tokenisation could "fluidify" global finance, which calls for internationally coordinated regulation and leaves emerging markets needing larger foreign exchange buffers and strict policy discipline.

## Key claims

- Tokenisation and stablecoins, on their own merit and by stoking competition, could make global finance more fluid.
- Stablecoins show potential to make large-value cross-border payments cheaper and faster.
- Tokenisation and stablecoins call for an internationally coordinated regulatory policy response.
- Stablecoins could complicate conditions for emerging market and developing countries, calling for larger foreign exchange buffers and strict policy discipline.
- Dollar-backed stablecoins could encourage people to replace some local-currency holdings with digital dollars, which weakens domestic interest-rate policy and makes cross-border money movements harder to manage.
- Tokenised systems remove settlement buffers by design, so margin calls and back-office functions run faster and authorities have less time to react.
- Georgieva describes blockchain's current role in global payments as "a small experiment".
