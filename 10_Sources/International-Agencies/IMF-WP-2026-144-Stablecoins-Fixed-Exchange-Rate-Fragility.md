# Stablecoins and Fragility in Fixed Exchange Rate Regimes

## Citation

Tan, Brandon Joel. "Stablecoins and Fragility in Fixed Exchange Rate Regimes." IMF Working Paper WP/26/144, International Monetary Fund, Western Hemisphere Department, 2026-07-10. https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/wp/2026/english/wpiea2026144.pdf.

## One-line summary

This IMF working paper models dollar stablecoins in fixed or managed exchange-rate economies, finding they widen dollar access in normal times but can coordinate currency runs once misalignment turns severe.

## Key claims

- Dollar stablecoins let households and businesses obtain foreign-currency claims outside official allocation channels, which is valuable where banks or governments ration dollar access.
- A global-games model shows stablecoins create a visible, high-frequency price that aggregates dispersed information about exchange-rate misalignment.
- That same public price signal has a dual role: it improves hedge allocation in normal states but can coordinate synchronised exits once fundamentals weaken.
- Stablecoins raise crisis exposure and shift the onset of a currency run to lower levels of misalignment than would trigger a run absent the stablecoin price signal.
- The USDT/BOB price became the common reference point for Bolivia's parallel exchange rate, illustrating the mechanism outside the model.
- A state-contingent policy response preserves low-friction stablecoin access in normal states while imposing temporary limits on large or panic-driven transactions once misalignment becomes severe.
- Blanket prohibitions on stablecoin access are regressive because financially excluded households benefit most from the access channel, while the coordination risk falls on the whole economy.

## Significance

- Supplies the wiki's first primary-source formal model of stablecoin-driven currency-run risk, complementing WP/26/74's issuer-side run model with a demand-side, FX-market mechanism.
- Names a real-world case, the USDT/BOB parallel rate in Bolivia, giving the wiki concrete evidence of stablecoin price discovery functioning as a public coordination signal.
- Frames a state-contingent regulatory approach, low friction in normal states, targeted restriction under stress, that sharpens the wiki's stablecoin-regulation doctrine node beyond blanket-access or blanket-prohibition framings.
- Extends the wiki's cross-border-payments domain into fixed and managed exchange-rate regimes, a jurisdictional context distinct from the reserve-currency focus of NOTE/2026/006 and WP/26/136.
