# UNIDROIT Holds Centenary Financial Markets and Technology Workstream Workshop

## Citation

Catherine High. "UNIDROIT Holds Centenary Financial Markets and Technology Workstream Workshop". UNIDROIT News, 17 September 2026 (listing date; workshop held 11 September 2026). https://www.unidroit.org/unidroit-holds-centenary-financial-markets-and-technology-workstream-workshop/.

## One-line summary

UNIDROIT's Financial Markets and Technology Committee presented a draft White Paper at a centenary workshop in Rome, covering DAPL implementation and a possible new project on digital risks and civil liability.

## Key claims

- The workshop took place at UNIDROIT headquarters in Rome on 11 September 2026 as part of the centenary programme.
- Organisers named in the item are Secretary-General Ignacio Tirado, Charles W. Mooney Jr. and Janis Sarra (FMT Committee Chair).
- The FMT Committee presented a draft White Paper assessing past initiatives and recommending future directions for UNIDROIT's financial markets and technology work.
- Discussion gave particular attention to the UNIDROIT Principles on Digital Assets and Private Law (DAPL), including implementation and convergence between common and civil law jurisdictions.
- Participants considered how digitalisation and technological innovation reshape financial markets and their private law frameworks.
- The panel examined possible recommendations on a new project on the Regulation of Digital Risks through Civil Liability.
