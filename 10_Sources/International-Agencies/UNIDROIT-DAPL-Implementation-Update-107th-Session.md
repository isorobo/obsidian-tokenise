# UNIDROIT Principles on Digital Assets and Private Law: Implementation and Promotion Update, 107th Governing Council Session

## Citation

UNIDROIT Secretariat. "UNIDROIT Principles on Digital Assets and Private Law: Implementation and Promotion Update". UNIDROIT 107th Governing Council Session, C.D. (107) 17, 27-29 May 2026. https://www.unidroit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/C.D.-107-17-DAPL-Principles.pdf.

## One-line summary

This Secretariat report to the 107th Governing Council documents global adoption of the DAPL Principles, noting legislative implementation in the US, Italy, Dubai, and Scotland, and coordinated efforts with HCCH, UNCITRAL, and CERIL on cross-border digital asset law.

## Key claims

- The DAPL Principles are now recognised as the leading international standard on the proprietary rights of digital assets, with implementing legislation enacted in the United States, Italy, Dubai, and Scotland.
- Scotland's Digital Assets (Scotland) Bill (passed March 2026) adopts a control-based approach aligned with the DAPL Principles and introduces a take-free rule protecting good-faith acquirers.
- The HCCH Experts' Group on digital tokens, mandated in March 2025, is developing PIL rules on electronic transferable records and other digital tokens, coordinating with UNIDROIT and UNCITRAL to avoid fragmentation.
- UNIDROIT participates in CERIL WP 16 on crypto-assets in EU insolvency law, contributing DAPL perspectives on custody, commingling, and shortfall.
- Unofficial translations of the DAPL Principles are underway into Spanish, Korean, Turkish, Mongolian, and Vietnamese, extending the instrument's reach beyond English and French.

## Significance

- Confirms adoption trajectory of the DAPL Principles: four jurisdictions have enacted or passed compliant legislation, with Scotland joining in March 2026.
- Documents emerging international coordination framework between UNIDROIT, HCCH, and UNCITRAL on private international law for digital tokens.
- Establishes DAPL's insolvency reach via CERIL WP 16, extending doctrinal coverage to custody, segregation, and shortfall in crypto-asset holding.
- Provides authoritative status update for the wiki's DAPL implementation strand.
