# UNIDROIT Launches Consultation on Draft Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits

## Citation

UNIDROIT Secretariat. "UNIDROIT launches consultation on draft Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits". unidroit.org News, 14 July 2026. https://www.unidroit.org/unidroit-launches-consultation-on-draft-principles-on-the-legal-nature-of-verified-carbon-credits/.

## One-line summary

UNIDROIT announces the opening of a ten-week public consultation on its draft Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits, running to 21 September 2026.

## Key claims

- UNIDROIT opened a public consultation on the draft Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits on 14 July 2026.
- The consultation runs for ten weeks, from 13 July 2026 to 21 September 2026, closing at midnight Rome time.
- The Working Group that drafted the Principles comprises international legal experts, government agencies, intergovernmental organisations, industry associations, and non-governmental organisations.
- The consultation seeks to raise awareness of the instrument, secure broad stakeholder participation, and test whether the draft adequately addresses core VCC trading issues.
- The Secretariat asks respondents to assess whether the Principles balance commercial and property law perspectives and function across different legal contexts.
- Submissions must use the prescribed template and go to a dedicated consultation email address, in English or French.

## Excerpts

> "UNIDROIT has initiated a public consultation regarding draft Principles addressing the legal nature of Verified Carbon Credits (VCCs)."
> ~ Announcement, opening paragraph

> "The consultation seeks to accomplish several objectives: raise awareness of the instrument, encourage broad stakeholder participation, assess whether core VCC trading issues are adequately addressed, and ensure the framework functions effectively across different contexts while maintaining balance between commercial and property law perspectives."
> ~ Announcement, body

## Significance

- Marks the formal transition of the VCC Principles from Working Group drafting to public consultation, the step that precedes Governing Council adoption.
- Sets a firm consultation deadline of 21 September 2026, giving the wiki a concrete date against which to track industry and jurisdiction responses.
- Confirms the consultation is bilingual, English and French, which bears on which stakeholder submissions the wiki can later track and cite.
- Functions as the news-layer companion to the substantive consultation draft text itself, useful for dating and framing citations to that document.
