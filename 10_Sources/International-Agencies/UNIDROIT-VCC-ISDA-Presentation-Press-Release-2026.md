# UNIDROIT Presents Draft Principles on the Legal Nature of VCCs to ISDA Financial Law Reform Committee

## Citation

UNIDROIT Secretariat. "UNIDROIT presents draft Principles on the Legal Nature of VCCs to ISDA Financial Law Reform Committee". unidroit.org News, 23 July 2026. https://www.unidroit.org/unidroit-presents-draft-principles-on-the-legal-nature-of-vccs-to-isda-financial-law-reform-committee/.

## One-line summary

UNIDROIT briefed the ISDA Financial Law Reform Committee on the draft VCC Principles, drawing over 100 participants from major financial institutions.

## Key claims

- UNIDROIT Legal Officer Giulia Previti presented the draft Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits to ISDA on 20 July 2026.
- The session was co-hosted by ISDA Senior Counsel Peter Werner and White & Case Financial Institutions Industry Group co-head Ingrid York.
- Over 100 participants attended, drawn from financial institutions, development banks, trading firms, law firms, rating agencies, and exchanges.
- The presentation framed the draft Principles as private law guidance to harmonise legislation and support scaling of voluntary carbon markets.
- The draft addresses VCCs as objects of proprietary rights, with provisions on innocent acquisition, custody, secured transactions, and insolvency.
- The Secretariat used the session to encourage ISDA members to submit comments before the consultation closes on 21 September 2026.

## Excerpts

> "The instrument aims to offer private law guidance to help harmonise legislative efforts across jurisdictions and support the scaling of voluntary carbon markets."
> ~ Announcement, body

> "The draft Principles address verified carbon credits as the subject of proprietary rights and aim to facilitate the trading of VCCs from their creation to retirement, with provisions on innocent acquisition, custody, secured transactions, and insolvency."
> ~ Announcement, body

## Significance

- Shows the derivatives industry, through ISDA, engaging directly with a private law instrument built for voluntary carbon markets.
- Signals likely consultation input from major dealers, custodians, and exchanges on custody and secured transactions provisions.
- Extends the wiki's evidence of stakeholder outreach beyond the Working Group and Governing Council process into commercial practice.
- Functions as a dated marker of industry uptake ahead of the 21 September 2026 consultation deadline.
