# UNIDROIT Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits: Summary Update and Authorisation to Proceed with Consultation, 107th Governing Council Session

## Citation

UNIDROIT Secretariat. "UNIDROIT Principles on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits: Summary Update and Submission of Draft Instrument for Comments and Authorisation to Proceed with Formal Consultations". UNIDROIT Governing Council, 107th Session, C.D. (107) 7, Rome, 27-29 May 2026. https://www.unidroit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/C.D.-107-07-Legal-Nature-of-Verified-Carbon-Credits-CLEAN.pdf.

## One-line summary

This Secretariat report sets out the full 25-Principle structure of the draft VCC instrument, explains the four tokenisation scenarios in Governing Council terms, and seeks authorisation to launch public consultation in July 2026.

## Key claims

- The draft instrument comprises an introduction and nine sections with 25 black-letter Principles, plus a proposed Tokenisation Annexe, covering scope, applicable law, creation, transfer, cancellation, registry, custody, secured transactions, and insolvency.
- Scenario Three tokenisation would require a new property rule establishing a legally operative link between a token and the underlying VCC, so that transferring the token transfers the proprietary interest.
- Scenario Four, natively on-chain VCCs managed without intermediaries, sits outside the Principles' scope, and products developed under it should not be labelled "VCCs".
- The Governing Council is asked to authorise public consultation on the draft instrument, excluding the still-developing Tokenisation Annexe, targeted for launch in July 2026.
- The Tokenisation Annexe is expected to be finalised after the core Principles are complete; if not ready by the end of 2026, the work may carry over into 2027.
- The Working Group has met eight times since October 2023, with a further remote session and a tenth session, scheduled 14-16 October 2026, still to come before finalisation.
- New institutional observers, including the Gold Standard Foundation and Climate Action Reserve, joined the Working Group ahead of the eighth session, reflecting growing market interest.

## Significance

- Gives the Governing Council's formal view of the complete 25-Principle architecture, the authoritative summary of the instrument ahead of public consultation.
- Confirms the July 2026 public consultation launch date, the next concrete milestone for the VCC Principles and a trigger point for industry submissions.
- Articulates the Scenario Three property rule requirement in Governing Council language, complementing the Working Group's internal Tosato memo.
- Signals institutional convergence, with the World Bank, Gold Standard Foundation, and Climate Action Reserve all now engaged as observers.
