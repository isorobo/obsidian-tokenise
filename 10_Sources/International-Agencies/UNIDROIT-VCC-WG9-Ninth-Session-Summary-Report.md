# Summary Report of the Ninth Session of the UNIDROIT Working Group on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits

## Citation

UNIDROIT Secretariat. "Summary Report of the Ninth Session of the UNIDROIT Working Group on the Legal Nature of Verified Carbon Credits". Study LXXXVI – W.G.9 – Doc. 5, Zoom, 16 June 2026. https://www.unidroit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Study-LXXXVI-W.G.9-Doc.-5-Summary-Report.pdf.

## One-line summary

The ninth session adopted the "snapshot approach" to VCC creation, severing the ongoing link between a VCC and its underlying mitigation outcome for property law purposes while retaining revocation only for fraud and complete nullity, with the draft Principles cleared for public consultation.

## Key claims

- The Working Group adopted the "snapshot approach": a VCC comes into existence if creation requirements are satisfied at that moment; post-creation events such as reversals do not extinguish the VCC as a property law matter.
- Revocation under Principle 10 is restricted to two grounds only — deliberate wrongdoing (functional equivalent of fraud) and complete nullity — both of which must relate to conditions at the time of creation.
- The Governing Council at its 107th session (27-29 May 2026) authorised submission of the revised Principles for public consultation once the Working Group completes final iteration.
- A new Principle XX on overissuance states that innocent overissuance does not cancel a VCC as a matter of property law; market mechanisms, registry rules, and regulation address the consequences.
- The Working Group deleted alternative provisions in Principle XX(2-alt) that would have imposed time-bar or transfer-ripening limitations, concluding they created four distinct credit types and undermined market certainty.

## Excerpts

> "If a VCC satisfied the requirements for creation as provided in the Principles at that particular point in time, then the VCC came into existence. Events that happened after creation no longer affected the existence of a VCC from a property law perspective."
> ~ Item 3(a), para. 11

> "Deliberate wrongdoing referred to the scenario in which a VCC, at the time of creation, was created with a defect, in the sense that the creation was materially affected by deliberate wrongdoing and but-for deliberate wrongdoing the VCC would not have been created."
> ~ Item 3(b), para. 24

> "Principle XX offered a range of ways of addressing innocent overissuance. The basic, non-alternative version of Principle XX provided essentially a statement of faith, stating that overissuance did not affect the existence of a VCC."
> ~ Item 3(c), para. 41

## Significance

- Resolves the central property law tension in the VCC Principles: the draft now adopts a clear creation-time snapshot rule, enabling market scaling without continuous dependency on environmental outcomes.
- Narrows revocation to fraud and complete nullity, aligning the instrument more closely with orthodox property law across common law and civil law systems.
- Clears the path to public consultation, the next stage before finalisation; the instrument will inform domestic law reform in multiple jurisdictions.
- Introduces Principle XX as the authoritative international statement that innocent overissuance is a market and regulatory matter, not a property law nullity.
