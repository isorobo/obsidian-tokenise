# Thematic Review Assessing the Implementation of IOSCO Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets

## Citation

IOSCO. "Thematic Review Assessing the Implementation of IOSCO Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets". IOSCO Board Final Report FR/13/25, 16 October 2025. https://www.iosco.org/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD801.pdf.

## One-line summary

IOSCO reviews how 20 jurisdictions have implemented its 2023 crypto and digital asset recommendations, finds significant progress on investor protection measures, but flags persistent gaps in cross-border co-operation and emerging risks from new business models.

## Key claims

- Twenty jurisdictions from both advanced and emerging economies participated in the review, conducted jointly by IOSCO's Fintech Task Force and Assessment Committee.
- Overall, significant progress is being made by IOSCO members toward implementing the key elements of the 2023 CDA Recommendations.
- Six focus areas were assessed: governance and conflicts of interest, fraud and market abuse prevention, cross-border co-operation, custody arrangements, retail client protections, and disclosure requirements.
- Cross-border co-operation remains a critical gap: most members have co-operation mechanisms, but hurdles to effective information sharing persist.
- New crypto business models, changing risks, and emerging risks mean that full implementation demands continued and ongoing effort.
- The review findings will serve as input into a full assessment methodology for the CDA Recommendations, enabling regular consistency assessments by IOSCO's Assessment Committee from 2026.

## Significance

- This is IOSCO's first formal implementation review of its 2023 CDA Recommendations and directly benchmarks global regulatory progress across 20 jurisdictions.
- The custody and disclosure findings are load-bearing for the wiki's analysis of legal frameworks governing digital asset intermediation and investor protection.
- The identified cross-border co-operation gaps inform the wiki's treatment of regulatory arbitrage and jurisdictional fragmentation in crypto markets.
- The transition to a full annual assessment methodology in 2026 makes this report the base document for tracking regulatory convergence across the vault's jurisdiction axis.
