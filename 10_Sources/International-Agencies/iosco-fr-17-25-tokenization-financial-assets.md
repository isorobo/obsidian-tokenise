# Tokenization of Financial Assets

## Citation

IOSCO. "Tokenization of Financial Assets". IOSCO Board Final Report FR/17/25, 11 November 2025. https://www.iosco.org/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD809.pdf.

## One-line summary

IOSCO surveys how tokenisation of bonds and money market funds is developing across capital markets, identifies four risk categories specific to DLT-based issuance, and encourages regulators to apply existing crypto and DeFi frameworks under a same-activity, same-risk principle.

## Key claims

- Tokenisation activity is growing but remains small relative to mainstream capital markets, concentrated in fixed-income products and money market funds.
- DLT-based settlement enables shorter settlement cycles and improved collateral mobility, but market participants still favour traditional post-trade infrastructure.
- Four risk categories are specific to tokenised assets: asset representation and legal transfer uncertainty, settlement finality ambiguity on Layer 2 networks, market fragmentation from multi-party tokenisation structures, and evolving operational and cyber vulnerabilities.
- The lack of interoperability across blockchains and the absence of high-quality settlement assets are the primary structural barriers to scale.
- Jurisdictions' regulatory approaches vary: some extend existing frameworks, others issue specialised guidance or operate regulatory sandboxes.
- IOSCO encourages consistent application of its Policy Recommendations for Crypto and Digital Asset Markets and its Decentralised Finance Recommendations to tokenised financial assets.

## Significance

- This is IOSCO's primary 2025 report on tokenised financial assets and establishes the global regulatory baseline for securities tokenisation oversight.
- The settlement finality analysis on Layer 2 networks is directly load-bearing for the wiki's blockchain-settlement and market-infrastructure domains.
- The identification of four DLT-specific risk categories provides a structured taxonomy for regulatory analysis of tokenised instruments across the vault.
- The same-activity principle links IOSCO's tokenisation framework to its 2023 crypto and 2024 DeFi recommendations, creating a connected regulatory lineage the wiki tracks.
