# Implementing the Federal Reserve Board's Responsibilities Under the GENIUS Act

## Citation

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. "Implementing the Federal Reserve Board's Responsibilities Under the GENIUS Act". Federal Register (Vol. 91, No. 187), 29 September 2026. https://govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-29/html/2026-19860.htm.

## One-line summary

The Federal Reserve Board proposed a new Regulation (12 CFR Part 247) for Board-supervised permitted payment stablecoin issuers, including custody rules and the Act's tying prohibition, with comments due 30 November 2026.

## Key claims

- The proposal is docket R-1899, RIN 7100-AH29, published in the Federal Register on 29 September 2026.
- Comments must be received by 30 November 2026.
- Subpart A sets scope and definitions, including "payment stablecoin", "control" and "outstanding issuance value".
- Subpart B covers permissible activities (issuance, redemption, reserve management, custody), bans on deceptive names and on paying interest or yield to holders, and later reserve, redemption, risk management, audit, supervision and capital rules.
- Subpart C sets rules for Board-supervised custodians, including segregation and omnibus accounts.
- Subpart E implements the section 4(a)(8) tying prohibition for all permitted payment stablecoin issuers.
- Subpart F covers the Board's backup enforcement authority for state-qualified issuers and a transition and waiver process for uninsured state-chartered depository institutions.
- Conforming amendments touch 12 CFR Parts 208, 211, 217, 225 and 263. (Only the first part of the long document was read; reserve, redemption and capital details were not reviewed.)

## Significance

- It is the Federal Reserve leg of the GENIUS Act rulemaking, complementing the OCC final rule and the Treasury proposal.
- The tying prohibition applies to all permitted issuers, so it matters beyond Board-supervised entities.
- The open comment period to 30 November 2026 makes it a live item to watch.
