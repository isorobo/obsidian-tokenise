# GENIUS Act: Waivers and Application Submission Guidance

## Citation

Office of the Comptroller of the Currency. "GENIUS Act: Waivers and Application Submission Guidance". OCC Bulletins (Bulletin 2026-51), 21 October 2026. https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-51.html.

## One-line summary

The OCC explains how prospective and existing stablecoin issuers apply to become permitted payment stablecoin issuers and request waivers, and says it expects to grant most prior-approval waivers to existing US issuers.

## Key claims

- Issuers operating in the US must comply with the GENIUS Act from 18 January 2027, and noncompliance carries criminal penalties.
- Issuers with a pending, substantially complete application on or before that date may request a waiver from the Act or final rule.
- The OCC may waive requirements for up to 12 months, but cannot waive them for insured depository institutions themselves.
- The OCC generally expects to grant waivers from the prior approval requirement for existing US issuers.
- Other waivers are not generally expected, because issuers have had 18 months to prepare.
- The OCC will decide within 30 days whether an application is substantially complete and aims to act on complete applications within 120 days.
- Intake opens after OMB approves the related information collection, and the OCC will announce this by press release.

## Excerpts

> "must comply with the GENIUS Act beginning January 18, 2027"
> ~ Bulletin text (compliance date)

## Significance

- It sets the practical licensing timetable (30 days completeness, 120 days decision) for the US stablecoin regime.
- It shows the transition design: existing issuers can keep operating under waivers while applications are pending.
- It pairs with the final rule bulletin as the operational companion for licensing.
