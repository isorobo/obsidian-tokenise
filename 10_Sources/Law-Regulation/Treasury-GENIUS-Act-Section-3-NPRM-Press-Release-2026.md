# Treasury Seeks Public Comment on GENIUS Act Proposed Rulemaking

## Citation

U.S. Department of the Treasury. "Treasury Seeks Public Comment on GENIUS Act Proposed Rulemaking". U.S. Department of the Treasury Press Releases, 17 August 2026. https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0605.

## One-line summary

Treasury proposed rules defining when a person "issues" payment stablecoins in the United States and when service providers may offer or sell them, including limits on foreign-issued stablecoins.

## Key claims

- Treasury issued a notice of proposed rulemaking on section 3 of the GENIUS Act, building on its earlier advance notice.
- The proposal defines what it means to "issue a payment stablecoin in the United States", which determines when a GENIUS licence is needed.
- The licensing requirement is expected to take effect on 18 January 2027.
- Digital asset service providers would generally be barred from offering foreign-issued stablecoins unless the issuer can comply with US lawful orders and any applicable reciprocal arrangement with its home jurisdiction.
- Beginning 18 July 2028, service providers would generally be restricted from offering or selling payment stablecoins to US persons unless a licensed issuer issued them.
- Comments are due within 60 days of Federal Register publication, via regulations.gov.

## Significance

- It carries the cross-border reach of the US regime, which matters for non-US stablecoin issuers and for comparison with MiCA and Asian regimes.
- The July 2028 offer and sale restriction sets a second compliance milestone after the January 2027 licensing date.
- It is the Treasury leg alongside the OCC and Federal Reserve rulemakings.
