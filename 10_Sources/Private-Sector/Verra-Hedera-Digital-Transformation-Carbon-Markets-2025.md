# Verra and Hedera to Accelerate Digital Transformation of Carbon Markets

## Citation

Verra. "Verra and Hedera to Accelerate Digital Transformation of Carbon Markets". Verra.org, 2025-05-28. https://verra.org/verra-and-hedera-to-accelerate-digital-transformation-of-carbon-markets/.

## One-line summary

Verra and the Hedera Foundation launch a five-year partnership to integrate the open-source Hedera Guardian blockchain platform into Verra's Project Hub, enabling digital monitoring, reporting, and verification (dMRV) for carbon credits.

## Key claims

- Verra is the first major carbon standards body to adopt blockchain technology at this scale, integrating Hedera Guardian with the Verra Project Hub.
- The Hedera Foundation commits five-year capital investment to fund digitalisation of 20+ carbon credit methodologies in the first year alone.
- Digital monitoring, reporting, and verification (dMRV) records every data point immutably on Hedera's distributed network, producing a tamper-proof audit trail for environmental claims.
- The open-source DLT Earth Bounty Program offers up to USD 5,000 per methodology to contributors who digitise additional VCS methodologies.
- The ALLCOT ABC Mangrove Restoration Project (VCS Project 4563) in Senegal is the pilot, using the digitalised VM0033 Methodology for Tidal Wetland and Seagrass Restoration.
- Guardian's dMRV pipeline enables "verifiable, tokenized outcomes" from carbon credit lifecycle data.

## Excerpts

> "This collaboration represents a significant advancement in Verra's digitalization strategy, enhancing our ability to deliver more efficient, transparent, and scalable climate action."
> ~ Mandy Rambharos, CEO, Verra

> "every data point is immutably recorded on Hedera's high-performance, low-cost network, creating a transparent and tamper-proof record of environmental claims"
> ~ Partnership description, Verra.org

## Significance

- Verra's formal adoption of a blockchain platform marks a reversal of its earlier arm's-length stance on distributed ledger technology in carbon markets.
- The dMRV integration creates the technical foundation for tokenised carbon credits by generating immutable on-chain records that token issuers can reference.
- The five-year commitment and methodology digitalisation target signal that Verra now treats blockchain infrastructure as core to its verification model, not an optional add-on.
- The pilot project in Senegal provides a live test of whether dMRV can satisfy both Verra's environmental integrity requirements and the transparency demands of crypto market participants.
