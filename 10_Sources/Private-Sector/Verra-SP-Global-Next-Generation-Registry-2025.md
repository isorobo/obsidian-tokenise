# Verra and S&P Global Commodity Insights to Advance Carbon Market Integration with Next-Generation Registry

## Citation

Verra. "Verra and S&P Global Commodity Insights to Advance Carbon Market Integration with Next-Generation Registry". Verra.org, 2025-08-21. https://verra.org/verra-and-sp-global-commodity-insights-to-advance-carbon-market-integration-with-next-generation-registry/.

## One-line summary

Verra partners with S&P Global Commodity Insights to build a next-generation carbon registry with transaction-ready APIs, two-way Project Hub integration, and Meta Registry connectivity to replace manual processes and enable automated carbon credit trading.

## Key claims

- The new registry is powered by S&P Global Commodity Insights' Environmental Registry software and replaces Verra's legacy manual system with an API-first architecture.
- Transaction-ready APIs enable automated transfers and retirements of carbon credits, connecting directly to CBL and Xpansiv Connect trading platforms.
- Two-way data exchange between the registry and the Verra Project Hub eliminates duplication across the project registration, monitoring, and issuance lifecycle.
- Integration with the Meta Registry ensures credits can move across registries without double counting, providing interoperability with government and Article 6 systems.
- Customisable reporting tools provide granular project-level data and credit history, addressing a core integrity demand from buyers and regulators.
- Rollout proceeds in two stages: a foundational phase within six months of announcement, then full integration in 2026.

## Excerpts

> "a technological and strategic transformation" that will build infrastructure for "a robust and resilient carbon market: one that is agile, smarter, and better connected"
> ~ Verra CEO, Verra.org press release

> "Transaction-ready application programming interfaces (APIs) that allow for automated transfers and retirements, replacing manual processes and enabling frictionless, high-volume trading across brokers, exchanges, and marketplaces"
> ~ Registry feature description, Verra.org

## Significance

- The API-first registry architecture is a prerequisite for institutional-scale carbon credit tokenisation: automated transfers enable token minting and redemption at speed without manual custodian intervention.
- Meta Registry integration directly addresses the double-counting risk that Verra identified as the primary barrier to crypto instrument adoption in its 2022 consultation.
- Connecting to Xpansiv and CBL via API moves the voluntary carbon market toward the settlement model common in tokenised financial instruments.
- The announcement shifts Verra from a paper-based standards body to a digital market infrastructure provider, reframing its role in the tokenised carbon credit ecosystem.
